The Primon Gas: Primes as Quantum States

Recent work has pushed this further. Speculative Unified Frameworks

“Gravity as Topological Charge Leakage” models particles as prime-numbered topological defects with masses $m_p \sim \ln p$, and quantizes gravitational couplings using Riemann zeta zeros[reference:7].

The “God Operator” framework claims to derive Einstein’s equations, Maxwell’s equations, and the Dirac equation from a single prime-indexed Lagrangian[reference:9].

These are highly speculative and have not been peer-reviewed or experimentally confirmed. They should be treated as mathematical explorations, not established physics.

The Honest Assessment

There is no accepted theory in which primes unify QM and GR. However, the connections that are rigorous—the primon gas, the BKL/zeta correspondence, the quantum-chaotic statistics of zeta zeros—are striking enough that many physicists take them seriously as clues. Whether this leads to a true unification remains one of the most fascinating open questions at the intersection of number theory and fundamental physics.
