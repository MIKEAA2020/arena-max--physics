## The specification

**The claim:** the arrow is structurally oriented — not merely statistical.

**The mechanism:** the initial state is *derivable from the measure structure*, not a brute fact. This is the "no brute facts" commitment applied to cosmology. The Past Hypothesis is not an arbitrary boundary condition. It is selected by *qadar* — the measure that precedes duration.

Concretely: the initial entropy, the Weyl curvature, the power spectrum, the tensor-to-scalar ratio, the number of e-folds — all should be derivable from the fundamental theory. That is a real specification. It is not a number. It is a claim about the *form* of the final theory, with observational consequences.

## What would falsify it

**The landscape.** If the final theory has a landscape of initial conditions — if the initial state is environmental, not derivable — the structural-orientation claim fails. This is the same bet as "no fundamental landscape." It is one bet with two consequences.

**The arrow itself.** If the arrow turns out to be reversible in the Sidis/Carroll–Chen sense — low entropy at both ends, reversing in the middle — then the direction is not structurally grounded. It is a boundary artifact. The claim fails.

That is weaker than a quantitative prediction. It is stronger than an interpretation. It is a **prediction about the form of the final theory**: the initial state is derivable, not arbitrary.

# Graded Polar Participationism (GPP)

## The name

**Graded** — one reality at degrees. Not flat monism. Not pluralism. One reality, many intensities (*tashkīk al-wujūd*).

**Polar** — the structure of manifestation is polarity. Every apparent contradiction is an axis. The engine.

**Participationism** — the creature's reality is received, not independent. Real, but derived. Grounded, not self-standing.

**Why not the alternatives:**

| Name | Why not |
|---|---|
| Layered concurrentism | Names the relation but not the engine or the grading |
| Graded monism | Loses the real distinction and the polarity engine |
| Divine concurrentism | Names only the divine-action model, not the metaphysics |
| Point-circle | A heuristic, not the framework |
| Polar monism | Loses the real distinction and the participation structure |

**GPP** captures all three: the grading (ontology), the polarity (engine), the participation (relation). Concurrence is a subset of participation. The informational layer is described in the account.

## I. What the view is

> **Graded Polar Participationism (GPP)** is a metaphysical framework in which there is one reality, the ground of which is a necessary, simple, incomparable, personal God. Creation is real but not independent — it is a graded participation in the divine reality, sustained at every moment. The distinction between God and creation is real and ultimate, not merely manifestational. The structure of manifestation is polarity: the one appears as many through axes, not through movement or emanation. Physical reality — time, space, causation, locality — is emergent from an informational order that is itself grounded in the divine knowledge. Consciousness is the intrinsic aspect of that informational order, grounded in divine awareness by participation. The framework does not close the questions the Quran marks as beyond: the nature of the *rūḥ*, the mechanism of divine action, the why of creation.

That is the view. The rest is elaboration.

## II. The ontology

### 1. The ground

There is a ground. It is not a thing among things.

- **Necessary** — it cannot not be.
- **Simple** — no parts, no composition.
- **Incomparable** — nothing is like it (42:11).
- **Timeless, spaceless** — not in the order it grounds.
- **Living, knowing, willing, speaking** — not inert.

It is *al-Ṣamad* — the one on whom all depend, who depends on none (112:2). It is *al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm* — the Living, the Self-Sustaining (2:255). It is *al-Awwal, al-Akhir, al-Ẓāhir, al-Bāṭin* — First, Last, Outward, Inward (57:3).

**Status:** pure metaphysics. Not derived from physics. Affirmed by the Quran, reasoned to by the tradition, consistent with physics.

### 2. The relation

Creation is real, dependent, sustained at every moment.

**Concurrence with a real distinction.** God is the primary cause and the ground of all. Creatures are real secondary causes. The distinction between them is real and ultimate — not merely manifestational. God is not the creature. The creature is not God. This is *tanzīh*.

**Graded participation.** The creature's reality is not independent. It is a received, sustained, graded participation in the divine reality. It is real — because the degree is real. It is dependent — because the ground is God. This is neither pantheism (everything is God) nor dualism (God and the world are two substances). It is graded monism with a real distinction.

**Plenitude.** Under the classical concept of freedom — self-determination, not indifference — the good diffuses itself by nature (*bonum est diffusivum sui*). A perfectly good being "cannot" withhold good. This is not compulsion. It is perfection. The manifestation is not a selection among alternatives. It is the self-expression of the good. All possibilities manifest, because nothing in the ground excludes any. The world is not coerced. It is what the ground is, at a radius.

### 3. Two-stage structure

There are two relations here, and they must not be collapsed:

| Relation | What it is | Warrant |
|---|---|---|
| **Grounding** | God → informational order | Metaphysical. Argued, not measured. |
| **Emergence** | Informational order → geometry, time, causation | Physical. Modeled, tested, revised. |

Physics addresses the second. It cannot address the first. Confusing them is the error that produces both over-claim and under-claim.

GPP uses constituting polarity throughout.

## VI. The interior — consciousness

### 15. Participationist dual-aspect monism

One reality, two aspects.

- **Relational aspect** — what physics describes: structure, function, dynamics.
- **Intrinsic aspect** — what it is like to be that structure: experience.

They are not two things. They are one thing under two descriptions.

### 16. Not Russellian

Russell places the intrinsic in matter, immanent and self-standing. GPP places it in the ground, received and participated. The intrinsic is not a property matter has. It is a mode of participation in the divine awareness that grounds the informational order. Experience is not a feature of matter. It is a mode of the *amr*.

### 17. Graded, not combined

There is no combination problem. Consciousness is graded — degrees of intensity, presence, participation. The many are the one at different radii. This is *tashkīk al-wujūd* applied to mind.

### 18. The hard problem dissolves; the mechanism remains open

We know the structure: experience is the intrinsic aspect of the informational order, grounded in divine knowledge. We do not know the mechanism — how the intrinsic relates to the relational, why it has the character it does. The Quran marks this as beyond (17:85).

## VII. The moral order

### 19. Agency and responsibility

Humans are real agents. Responsibility is real. Commands are addressed to those who can respond. The moral order is real at the ultimate level — judgment, reward, punishment are not theatrical. *ʿAdl* — God does not wrong anyone (3:182).

### 20. Evil as privation

Evil is not a created substance. It is the absence of a good that should be there. Light has units — photons. Darkness does not. Being is graded; privation is not a grade of being, it is the lack of a grade. Suffering is the experience of privation — the awareness of a good missing or being destroyed.
