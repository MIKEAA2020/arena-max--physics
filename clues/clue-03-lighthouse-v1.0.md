# The lighthouse
### Constraints and guidance for physics and neuroscience research, under three criteria: the Qur'an, logic, and empirical evidence

**Version 1.0.** This is the consolidated statement: what to do and what to avoid, where to look and where not to look, one map with every piece in place, the unknowns kept visible, and the predictions that could fail. It is written to stand alone. The audit behind it, the revision history, and the source records are kept in separate files, named at the end.

---

## §0 How to use this

A lighthouse marks the rocks and the channel. It does not sail the ship. Read this document accordingly: it is at its most valuable where it says **do not look there**, and where it says **unknown — kept open**. It is at its least valuable if read as a theory of everything, because it isn't one.

Every reader starts with **§2 — reality, plainly stated**.

Three readers:

- **The physics reader** uses §3 (what each criterion can decide), §7.1 (live questions), §8 (dead ends), §10 (predictions and tests).
- **The neuroscience reader** uses §3, §7.2, §8, §9 (P5 especially), §10.
- **The reader working on scripture and first principles** uses §1–§4, §9, §11.

**Every statement carries one of four grades:**

- **[C] Constraint** — must not be violated anywhere in a programme built on this map. Breaking one is a design error, not a preference.
- **[H] Heuristic** — points the search; may be wrong; must be revised on evidence.
- **[P] Open question** — unknown; kept visible; may not be quietly filled.
- **[F] Forbidden** — do not enter without new evidence; the reason, and the only condition that would reopen it, are stated with each.

## §1 What this document is

**What it is.** A constraint set, a research map, and a register of open questions — under three criteria: **the Qur'an, logic, and empirical evidence**. It does four things: it constrains (says what an acceptable programme must not violate), it guides (shows the live questions and what a good result looks like), it displays (one picture in which the pieces have places), and it predicts (states claims that could fail). **The account of reality itself is §2** — what it is, what it is like, and what can be said of it and with what warrant.

**What it is not.** It is not a mechanism oracle: no verse yields an equation, and any document that appears to derive physics from scripture has made the first mistake in §6. It is not a proof of God: arguments about the ground are scope-limited, and the route to faith is not the route of argument. It is not a numerology machine. And it is not a compatibility machine: **a claim that is compatible with every possible measurement constrains nothing** — that is the failure mode this map exists to prevent.

**The one-sentence shape.** The Qur'an sets what may and may not be said at the level of first principles — about God, the world, and the good — including by what it does not say; logic governs coherence and the naming of every hidden premise; empirical evidence governs what the world's structure actually is. Where the three meet, each keeps its own jurisdiction (§3), not its own preference.

## §2 Reality, plainly stated

**The account in one paragraph.** There is one reality. At its root is God — not a component of the world, not a force or a field among others, but the ground on which everything else depends, and nothing is like Him (42:11). Everything other than the ground is created: real, active, and never self-sufficient — dependent on the ground at every moment, not only at its first one (*al-Qayyūm*, 2:255; *al-Ṣamad*, 112:2). The created order is one reality appearing at degrees — a manifestation (*tajallī*), not a second thing beside God and not identical with God. It is lawful and measured (54:49; 55:7); layered, with real levels (§4); polar, each axis one reality under two poles (§4.2); informational at the level of its structure (§4.3); personal at the root; and it has an inside — it is not exhausted by its measurable face (57:3). Its structure is knowable in part by observation, never exhaustively; and some things are marked, inside the revelation itself, as beyond knowing (17:85).

**Its characteristics.**

1. **One at the root, and not composite there.** The ground has no parts (112). What is composite depends on its parts and so cannot be the last word — that is an argument, not a picture. "One" does not mean "the only thing that exists": the creation is real. It means one ground, one dependence, one order.
2. **Ground and creation are not two causes at one level.** God creates all things (39:62), and the creature's action is genuinely its own and genuinely grounded — not a second sufficient cause beside God's. The best-supported reading is **layered concurrence with a real distinction**. Neither pure occasionalism (the creature does nothing real) nor pure autonomy (the creature needs nothing) survives the text at the level of first principles. When a model seems to need two sufficient causes at one level, the instruction is: look deeper, not double (§4).
3. **Dependence is continuous, not merely original.** The world is not a machine set going and then left; it is upheld (2:255). Every level depends on what grounds it, at every moment.
4. **Lawful and measured.** Everything is by measure (54:49) and in balance (55:7); the divine pattern does not change (33:62); the world is not play (21:16). Its structural corollary, held at heuristic level: no brute facts — the constants are to be derived, not accepted as arbitrary (§10, D-8, D-9).
5. **Layered, with real levels and graded being.** Ground → manifestation → structure → world → life → mind → norm (§4). The levels are real, not bookkeeping; each is grounded in the one above without competing with it, and none is final. Gradation is real in effect: things can be more or less participant in being.
6. **Polar.** Each axis of §4.2 is one reality under two poles that constitute rather than compete. The asymmetry holds on every axis: the ground is prior in being, the manifestation prior in appearing.
7. **Informational at the level of structure.** [H] The created order's structure has informational character: finite information density, redundancy and error-correction, level-relative rendering, an interface between knower and known (§4.3). The Qur'anic vocabulary — *qadar*, *ʿilm*, *ḥifẓ*, *lawḥ maḥfūẓ*, *kitāb*, *kun* — is where this is anchored; the physics is the working-out.
8. **Intelligible — a book, not a program.** The world is spoken: *kun* (36:82) is speech, not execution; the cosmos is *bi-l-ḥaqq* (6:73), made in truth, not staged. That is why mathematics reaches it: intelligibility is grounded, not a lucky accident. [H]
9. **Personal at the root, and value is not an illusion.** The ground is God — not neutral stuff, not an indifferent principle, not a blind process. Norms are real and not reducible to facts; the moral order is not a projection. *How* norm is grounded is open (§9, P6); *that* it is grounded is a constraint.
10. **It has an inside.** What physics describes is the relational, structural aspect; the intrinsic aspect is what it is like to be that structure — the inside of the informational layer, received and graded, grounded in divine knowledge. Experience is therefore not an add-on to the physical story; it is the inside of the same reality (§9, P5). The *rūḥ* is marked beyond us (17:85).
11. **Purposive, not play.** The world has a purpose (21:16). What its end will be belongs to the revelation's register; physics may neither read a calendar off that nor deny it a place.

**What can be said of it — and with what warrant.**

- **What the Qur'an fixes.** One ground; nothing like Him; creation real, dependent, upheld; the world lawful, measured, purposive; norm real; the boundaries of knowledge declared. These hold for the whole programme.
- **What reason requires.** No regress of grounds; ground and grounded are not two causes at one level; the ground is not a member of the world it grounds; no totality contains itself; contradiction is not a feature of reality. These are coherence requirements, not discoveries.
- **What evidence decides.** Everything structural: what the levels are like, which structures exist, every value, every mechanism — and every prediction in §10. Evidence never decides the first two lists; it corrects interpretations of them.
- **Not like anything.** Since nothing resembles the ground (42:11), the account of God is given by what He is not, what He does, and how He is named — never by depiction. Stated once so no later section has to whisper it: the ground is not pictured in this document, and any sentence that pictures it has left the jurisdiction of §3.

**What cannot be said** — kept open, with reasons, at §9: why there is manifestation at all (P14); the nature of the ground (P1); the *rūḥ* (P13); which level is ours if all are actualized (P10); what the silence means (P8); how structure becomes experience (P5).

## §3 The three criteria, and what each can decide

Each criterion has a jurisdiction. **A claim evaluated outside its jurisdiction is a category error, not a stronger claim.** The failure-modes column lists the real errors this discipline exists to prevent.

| Criterion | Can decide | Cannot decide | Failure mode when overreached | Operational test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Qur'an** | First-principles constraints: there is a ground; the ground is not a part of the world and nothing is like it (42:11); creator and creature are not two equal causes at one level; the world is lawful, measured, and purposive (54:49; 33:62; 21:16); norms are real and not reducible to facts; and the boundaries of knowledge — including its own declared silences | Mechanism; measurement; what force, field, or process to look for; the content of any physics or neuroscience theory; **complete metaphysics** — the text gives terms, not systems | **Verse-to-physics matching**; **gap-filling** — inserting the divine where physics is missing; **uniqueness claims** — calling one harmonization *the* literal one; **totality claims** — demanding a closed metaphysics the text does not supply | (a) The claim can be stated with no physical vocabulary. (b) No verse is used to *produce* a mechanism. (c) Declared silences stay silent (17:85; 3:7 — and since 3:7's own criterion of clarity has no supplied list, no reading may claim to be the only one). (d) The claim respects the authority rule below. |
| **Logic** | Coherence, non-contradiction, entailment, the finding and naming of hidden premises, the separation of senses, the exposure of equivocation | What the world is like; existence claims; any empirical fact; the content of scripture | **Proof-as-physics** — treating a deducible structure as a discovered one; undefined terms carrying weight ("energy", "consciousness", "information" unanalysed) | Every inference states its hidden premises; every key term has one sense per use; when comparison flips a verdict, the criterion of comparison is named before anything is ranked. |
| **Empirical evidence** | What patterns obtain; parameter values; which structures are realized; whether a proposed mechanism exists; likelihoods under stated assumptions | Metaphysics; meaning; norm; whether the first-principles claims have instances; whether "nothing but" is true | **Overreach to "nothing but"** — turning a successful reduction into a metaphysical verdict; **interpretation-as-datum** — reporting a model's reading as a finding | Every claim of contact states a test that could fail. Pre-register where possible. Interpretation is labelled as interpretation. |

**Rules for using the three together.**

1. **Label every claim [C].** Which criterion it came from (Qur'an / logic / evidence), which band of the map it lands in, and its grade. Two minutes of labelling prevents most of the errors in the list above.
2. **Jurisdiction over preference [C].** Conflicts are resolved by jurisdiction, never by who feels stronger. An empirical result cannot overrule a Qur'anic first-principles constraint — it can only correct an *interpretation* of it. A Qur'anic constraint cannot dictate an empirical question — it can only forbid readings that contradict it. Logic decides neither facts nor scripture, only whether what is said is sayable.
3. **Silence is a boundary, not a licence [C].** Where the Qur'an is silent (the *rūḥ*: 17:85; the ambiguous verses: 3:7), the place stays open. Silence licenses inquiry, never a conclusion — in either direction. The same holds in reverse: where evidence is silent, no metaphysical verdict is earned.
4. **A claim must be able to fail [C].** Whatever is proposed here must state what result would count against it. Constructions that survive every possible outcome (flexible occasionalisms, unfalsifiable absolutes) are downgraded, not celebrated.
5. **The symmetry of undecidability [C].** A construction that shields a claim from disproof shields it from proof too. Where a proposition cannot be decided either way, the honest output is a withheld verdict with the reason recorded — not a win.
6. **Authority is not totality [C].** The Qur'an is the ultimate **authority** where it speaks — what a programme is bound by. It is not a **total explanation** — a complete metaphysics from which every question has an answer. Consequences run both ways: where the text gives terms but no resolution — as with creation and necessity (P14) — reason may conclude, leave open, or mark as beyond us, **and no verdict reached that way may be attributed to the text**; where the text is silent, the silence is not an argument (rule 3); where it speaks, preference does not overrule it. This is what makes the project's central claim possible: a synthesis can be **faithful to the Qur'an without being derived from it**.
7. **Two different relations: grounding and emergence [C].** **Emergence** — higher-level structure produced by lower-level structure — is what physics studies. **Grounding** — why there is anything at all, and on what everything depends — is not a process inside physics. Do not present an emergence result as a verdict on grounding; do not present grounding as a mechanism acting inside physics. Every failure of this rule in either direction is the same category error.

**How to read the picture.**

- **Grounding runs downward and is non-competitive [C].** Every band depends on the one above without competing with it: the lower band's real causal power is not a second sufficient cause beside the higher's — that arrangement is exactly what creates over-determination problems (the Kim constraint). When a model needs two sufficient causes at one level, **look deeper, not double**.
- **The axes are constituting, not rival [C].** Each polarity is one reality under two poles: the poles co-constitute; they do not fight. A physics that treats its dual descriptions as competitors has imported a metaphysical picture this map rejects — and a physics that treats them as identical has imported the other error: the map grounds, it does not identify the ground with what it grounds.
- **Access runs upward and is partial [C].** Evidence reaches structure, not ground. The ground enters a programme only as constraint and boundary — never as an object of measurement.
- **Band 1 is the generator.** The axes make the map generative rather than decorative: each axis is a research programme (find the pole-pairs, their asymmetry, their grammar) and a prediction shape (where two descriptions of one thing compete, look for the missing axis).
- **Open questions stay visible [C].** A bracketed P-entry may not be quietly filled by a later sentence. Any candidate answer enters §9 with a status line — never a promotion to a band.

### §4.1 The five levels

The map read top to bottom, as levels:

| Level | Content | Where it sits | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L1 · God** | The ground; not a component of the world; nothing like it (42:11); self-sufficient (*al-Ṣamad*, 112:2); the world is not play (21:16) | Band 0 | [C] as constraint; [P] as to its nature (P1) |
| **L2 · The relation** | How the ground relates to the world. The best-supported reading of the Qur'an here is **layered concurrence with a real distinction**: God is the creator of all things, and the creature's act is genuinely its own, grounded — not a second equal cause. The **point–circle picture** (below) is a *model* of how manifestation could work — a family of models with a free parameter (the radius), not a proof and not a metaphor | The grounding arrow itself | [H] as readings; [P] as to the relation's nature (P1, P14) |
| **L3 · Manifestation** | *Tajallī* / *taʿayyun* — the one appearing at every radius, synchronously and freely. The right word is manifestation; it is **not** unfolding, not emanation, not expression, not procession. Gradation is real in effect, not a second substance | Band 1 | [H] |
| **L4 · Informational structure** | The bridge, in six pieces (below), with the Qur'anic vocabulary that anchors it | Band 2 | [H] |
| **L5 · Physics** | The descriptions: spacetime, fields, quanta | Band 3 | [H] as expectations; open questions stay open |

**The point–circle picture, stated plainly.** The point is the ground: prior in being, without parts, without scale. The circle at radius *r* is the created order: one reality appearing at a degree. The radius is the measure — and measure is the first thing the world has (54:49). The point is prior in being; the circle is prior in manifestation — the ground explains, the manifestation makes visible. What the picture may claim: orientation and coherence. What it may not claim: proof, uniqueness, or an equation.

### §4.2 The axes — the engine

**The rule [C]:** every apparent contradiction is a polarity misread as a competition. The resolution is not to pick a pole; it is to find the axis.

**The inventory [H]** (deduplicated — an axis that does no work, classifying or predicting nothing, is a metaphor and does not belong here): being ↔ manifestation · timeless ↔ temporal · spaceless ↔ spatial · simple ↔ composite · intrinsic ↔ relational · definite ↔ superposed · discrete ↔ continuous · background-free ↔ background-full · measure ↔ duration · unity ↔ multiplicity · stability ↔ novelty · manifest ↔ hidden · necessary ↔ contingent.

**The asymmetry [C]:** on every axis, point prior in being, circle prior in manifestation.

**The internal check [C]:** polarity (formal), gradation (ontological), and two-aspect structure (epistemic) must be **one idea, viewed three ways** — if they become three separate assumptions, the whole framework costs more than it earns, and the claim to simplicity fails on its own terms.

### §4.3 The informational layer — the bridge

The six pieces, with the Qur'anic vocabulary that anchors them. **The anchors are vocabulary, not derivations:** they say what the framework may affirm; they do not tell physics what to find.

| Piece | Content | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Substrate-independence | The structure does not inhere in one kind of substance | God is creator of all things (39:62) — no created substance is ultimate |
| Information is primary | Information is more basic than matter | *qadar* (54:49), *kitāb* (36:12), *ʿilm* (6:59, 34:3) |
| Finite information density | A bound on information per region | *mīzān* (55:7), *qadar* (54:49) |
| Level-relative rendering | What appears at one level is generated from another | *ẓāhir/bāṭin* (57:3); levels of creation (17:44) |
| Error correction / redundancy | Stability through redundant encoding | *ḥifẓ* (15:9); *lawḥ maḥfūẓ* (85:22) |
| Interface structure | Perception and measurement reach an interface, not the structure itself | *ẓāhir/bāṭin* (57:3); *āyāt* (41:53) |

This layer was borrowed *structurally* from the simulation hypothesis, whose substance is rejected: no simulator — a simulator would be a regress and a contingent ground, a bigger creature rather than the ground; no staged moral order; the cosmos is *bi-l-ḥaqq* (6:73) — a **book**, not a **program**; *kun* (36:82) is speech, not execution. And per §5.14, because these six pieces were extracted from physics and computing, their agreement with physics is an assumption, not a result.

**What the layer commits the programme to [H]:**

- **Measure precedes duration.** If time is emergent, what it emerges from is measure. Expect discrete or quantified structure beneath temporal ordering; causal sets, loop quantum gravity, and spectral geometry are the meeting points.
- **No orphan levels.** No level is final. Classical spacetime, the Standard Model as framed, and quantum theory as framed are all incomplete by construction; the Standard Model's internal structure (gauge group, generations) should be derivable from something deeper.
- **One interface bound.** The Bekenstein bound, the holographic bound, the Landauer bound, and quantum-measurement limits should turn out to be one structure — one inequality whose special cases they are. This is the framework's one candidate for a quantitative result (D-11).
- **No free discreteness scale.** In any discrete quantum gravity, the discreteness scale should be derivable from the measure structure, not assumed (D-9).
- **Dynamics and information are one** — *qadar* and *ʿilm* as two aspects of one structure. [Speculative; held at heuristic level.]

**The four dissolutions [H], and the rule for reading them.** One move dissolves four problems:

- **The hard problem** — the relational aspect of the informational structure is what physics describes; the intrinsic aspect is what it is like to be that structure. The intrinsic is **received**, grounded in divine knowledge, graded — so this is participationist dual-aspect monism, **not** Russellian (whose intrinsic natures sit inside matter, needs nothing). The *rūḥ* stays at its boundary (17:85).
- **The measurement problem** — the superposition is the relational description; the definite outcome is the intrinsic fact. No collapse, no branching, no observer-creation.
- **The problem of time** — the point is the whole at once (*totum simul*); the radius is sequence. Wheeler–DeWitt is the view from the point; becoming is the view from the radius.
- **The quantum-gravity programme conflict** — the programmes are complementary views of one informational structure rather than rivals.

**The rule [C]:** a re-description earns nothing. A dissolution counts only where it **rules something out**. Of the four, the measurement dissolution does — it forbids a collapse mechanism, and that yields a scored prediction (D-7). The others stand as readings, and their open questions (P2, P5) stay open.

## §5 What to do

1. **[C] Label before you claim.** Criterion, band, grade — then argue (§3, rule 1).
2. **[C] Name every hidden premise.** The strongest repairs in this project's history all followed this practice: unstated premises were named, not deleted.
3. **[C] Fix the criterion before ranking.** On any comparison — interpretations, frameworks, harmonizations — declare the criterion first, score on it, and flag any change of criterion since the last verdict. This one rule prevents verdicts from oscillating. It is also what makes the quantum-gravity scorecard admissible at all (§9, P9): a ranking under a named criterion, not a free-standing verdict.
4. **[C] Prefer non-competitive, layered models.** Layered dependence is the default shape of this map; genuine over-determination is a sign of a missing level.
5. **[H] Look for invariants, measures, and constraints.** The Qur'anic warrant is general (54:49 — everything by measure; 33:62 — the divine pattern never changes), and it matches how science works: every successful science searches for structure that survives transformation. This licenses symmetry methods, invariant theory, conservation laws, measurement theory.
6. **[H] Treat dualities as clues to axes.** Where two complete descriptions of one system exist (AdS/CFT; quantized/separable; entangled reference frames; particle/wave), the expectation is that they are poles of one structure — not rivals, not identical. Build the taxonomy (project A).
7. **[H] Grade every verdict.** Verdicts come as settled / proposed-with-premises / withheld-with-reason — never as binaries. A withheld verdict with a recorded reason is an output, not a failure.
8. **[H] Keep the two neuroscience default frameworks at their proven status.** Predictive processing is a live framework with partial support; the free-energy principle is a research heuristic with contested scope. Use them as tools, never as premises.
9. **[H] Search for structure before substance.** In mind science, the surviving candidates for the mind–body bridge (P5) are organizational, not energetic. Prune the search space accordingly, and search the surviving part hard.
10. **[H] Record, don't resolve, where nothing can decide.** For the silence (P8) and the ground (P1), and for the creation-necessity bind (P14), the output is the dilemma, the reason, and the date. Asserting a resolution in either direction is forbidden.
11. **[C] Pre-register contact with data.** Every programme that will touch data states its design, thresholds, and adjudication rule before the data.
12. **[C] Check every "no other option" against the classical record.** Before declaring a dilemma exhaustive — "there is no fourth option; you choose the cost" — check whether the tradition's own authors denied one of its steps. Worked example: the inference from divine goodness to necessary creation (P14). Aquinas accepts that the good is self-diffusive and still denies the inference — creation remains a free act of the will; Bonaventure ties creation more tightly to the good yet still calls it free. The inference is argued, not forced. A tradition's denials are evidence about the structure of the problem.
13. **[C] Cite by status.** Every scriptural or traditional citation carries its standing: the Qur'an; a hadith with its grade; or a saying that circulates in the tradition without a sound chain. Worked example: "I was a hidden treasure and I loved to be known" — a Sufi motif, absent from the canonical collections and graded weak or baseless by many hadith scholars. It may be quoted as a motif; it may not carry an argument. In that discussion the weight sits instead on verifiable ground: *al-Wadūd* ("the Loving", 11:90; 85:14) and *al-Raḥmān* (55:1) as eternal names, and the classical principle in its non-necessitarian form.
14. **[C] Borrowed fit is not confirmation.** When a structure is borrowed from a field, its agreement with that field is an assumption, not a result. The informational layer (§4.3) was extracted from physics and computing; "it fits physics" therefore proves nothing about it. What can count: internal coherence; work it does in domains it was not built from (text, norm, mind); and new claims that could fail (§10). Corollary: **unification without prediction is cheap.**
15. **[C] Keep this document open to its own revision.** Every change to a graded claim records why, and by which criterion, in the audit layer (§11).

## §6 What to avoid

Each entry: the pattern, the reason, and the repair.

1. **God as an explanation for gaps [F].** Inserting the divine where physics is currently missing. Reason: every historical gap-fill has been closed by physics; the Qur'an describes the world as measured and patterned, not occluded; and the move turns the ground into a physical competitor (violating §4). *Repair:* register the gap as an open question (§9) and search for its lawful structure.
2. **Mechanism read out of verses [F].** Reason: no verse supplies mechanism, and 3:7's criterion of clarity has no supplied list, so uniqueness claims are unearned in principle. *Repair:* verses constrain first principles; cite them for that and nothing else.
3. **Verse-to-discovery matching and numerology [F].** Aligning numbers, letters, or timelines with measured constants — and, in its respectable-looking form, reading a scientific theory out of verses. Reason: zero likelihood ratio — any flexible scheme matches anything; unfalsifiable by design. Worked case: cosmic-scale **fractal cosmology** read out of 67:3–4, 54:49, 55:7. The Qur'an's stance on a scientific theory is non-doctrinal; and the strong claim fails its own test — galaxy clustering is scale-free only to about 70–100 Mpc, after which surveys find the transition to homogeneity. The genre stays closed.
4. **Making the ground an object [F].** "God field", "divine energy", "consciousness field as the ground". Reason: 42:11 forbids it; and the ground grounds — it is not identical with what it grounds. *Repair:* the ground enters a programme as constraint and boundary, never as a modelled component.
5. **Quantum free will [F].** Deriving libertarian agency from physical indeterminism. Reason: an undetermined event is not a free act; randomness is freedom's rival, not its ground. (Determinism entails no verdict about freedom; the converse fails.) *Repair:* locate freedom research at the level of agency (13:11; 18:29; 2:286), not in the physics of a gap.
6. **Oneness from entanglement [F].** Inferring metaphysical unity from non-separability, field structure, or holography. Reason: correlation is not identity. *Repair:* treat such structures as axis evidence — they make dual-description structure visible — and keep the metaphysics at band 0, where it must be argued, not inferred.
7. **Mechanism of the *rūḥ* [F].** Research framed as discovering how the spirit works. Reason: 17:85 draws the boundary inside the text. *Repair:* correlates of mind and self are permitted and welcome; mechanism claims about the spirit are category errors.
8. **Quantum-mind shortcuts [F].** Zero-point-field resonance, microtubule coherence, and the like, used as premises. Reason: no experimental support for the consciousness claim, critical review literature, and decoherence bounds on warm tissue. *Repair:* they enter only as hypotheses under test with stated kill criteria (question N-4) — never as foundations.
9. **Borrowed vocabulary [F].** A term carrying one field's meaning inside another's argument — "separation fails", "the observer does X", "energy is consciousness". Reason: this is the single most common cause of false verdicts in this project's own history. *Repair:* define the term in the field where you are using it, or do not use it.
10. **Compatibility as evidence [F].** "This is consistent with everything we know" offered as support — including **physical convergence** counted as confirmation when the framework was built from that convergence (§5.14). *Repair:* state the claim that could fail, and grade it.
11. **Interpretation as datum [F].** Reporting a favoured reading as a finding ("the block universe explains…", "the brain creates consciousness…"). *Repair:* label interpretations; test only the claims that can fail.
12. **Claiming the one true harmonization [F].** "This is *the* reading that reconciles the verses." Reason: the tool of adjudication (what counts as clear) is itself contested, so no reading may claim uniqueness. *Repair:* exhibit the reading, record the withhold, claim coherence and support only. This applies to this project's own best-supported reading (layered concurrence, §4.1) as much as to anyone's.
13. **Plenitude as a physics premise [F].** Using "all possibilities manifest" — a reading the logic of no-preference and non-arbitrariness can reach — to predict or explain the structure of our own level. Reason: if every possibility is realized somewhere, no selection is made, so nothing about *this* world is explained; used as a premise it makes the framework compatible with any measured value. *Repair:* keep the claim as a metaphysical reading (§9, P14); the defensible residue is the claim that the set of realized structures is **generated by one structure rather than assembled by fiat** — that much is testable (D-8) — plus D-9.
14. **Turning the unseen into physics [F].** Identifying jinn or angels with dark matter, plasma, electromagnetism, virtual particles, or parallel branches. Reason: each identification fails on both sides — the physical candidates have no will, community, or accountability, and the identification would make a deliberately imperceptible order measurable (7:27 denies this). *Repair:* the unseen orders are a structural open question (P13). What can be studied is the near side: how the mind processes and attributes interior influence (question N-7).
15. **Counting null results as confirmation [F].** Treating every new negative result in collapse-model searches as support for D-7. Reason: post-hoc accumulation of nulls proves nothing; a negative prediction is only as strong as the scoring rule fixed in advance. *Repair:* pre-register a dated forecast — which parameter windows should close, by when — and score against it (project I).

## §8 Where not to look

The dead ends, each with the only thing that would reopen it:

1. **God as a physical component or field** — reopen: never (42:11; the ground grounds, it is not a component).
2. **Gaps as divine mechanism** — reopen: never.
3. **Mechanisms read out of verses; numerology; verse-to-theory matching** — reopen: never.
4. **Quantum indeterminacy as free will's ground** — reopen: a principled account of how indeterminism yields authorship, which no one has produced.
5. **Metaphysical oneness from entanglement or field unity** — reopen: an argument at band 0; correlation alone never reopens it.
6. **Zero-point-field / microtubule consciousness as premise** — reopen: replicated functional-coherence evidence surviving controls (then they are results, not premises).
7. **Mechanism of the *rūḥ*** — reopen: never; correlates remain open (N-questions).
8. **"The observer" without disambiguation** — reopen: specify which sense (relational quantity / subsystem information / epistemic limit) before any claim.
9. **"Nothing but" verdicts** — reopen: never as a general move; only as a specific, earned reduction with its remainder stated.
10. **Resolving the silence by assertion** — in either direction (God obviously speaking here; God obviously absent) — reopen: never; record instead (P8).
11. **Ranking interpretations without a criterion** — reopen: state the criterion, then rank.
12. **Plenitude as a physics premise** — reopen: a derivation in which the totality does real work on our own level (explains this world's values rather than dissolving the question).
13. **Physical identifications of the unseen orders** — reopen: none. The near-side research (N-7) is open, and is not a reopening of this.
14. **Counting null results as evidence** — reopen: a pre-registered, dated forecast that then comes true.

**What this framework does not predict.** No new particle, no new force, no new number, no new effect. No specific cosmological signature; no quantum-gravity signal. It takes no position on the origin of time's arrow — statistical accounts are physics' working position, and a structural origin is neither required nor excluded here. It predicts the *shape* of future physics, not its content — which is weaker than a quantitative theory and stronger than an interpretation.

**Patterns to look for.**

- Wherever two complete descriptions of one system compete, look for the axis.
- Wherever a causation puzzle shows over-determination, look for the missing level.
- Wherever a bridge claim appears, ask for its independent warrant; the expectation is that most are unwarranted, and the discipline is to say so.
- Wherever a framework becomes compatible with everything, its discriminating core has been lost; find where it used to bleed.
- Wherever a dissolution appears, ask what it forbids. A dissolution that forbids nothing is a re-description (§4.3).
- Wherever a borrowed layer is doing explanatory work, separate assumption from evidence (§5.14).

**Questions the map makes askable.**

1. What is the **adjoint** of each fundamental construction?
2. Which **conserved quantity** does each polarity have, if any? (An axis that conserves something is a research programme; one that conserves nothing is a metaphor.)
3. Where are the **dissociation pairs** in each claimed polarity? (N-5 is the worked template.)
4. Which **open question does a proposed finding fill** — and does it meet the fill-conditions, or does it merely touch the word?
5. What would the **absence** of the predicted structure look like, stated in advance?
6. **Which level is ours** (P10), and does any answer explain, or merely absorb, the question?
7. **Which dissolution could be turned into a forbiddance** — a claim the world could fail? (D-7 is the worked example.)
