## §14 The hybrid framework: layers and constraints, revised

v2.0 §10 assembled five layers and seven structural constraints. v2.1 keeps the stack, adds the measure-first layer beneath it, moves the crossed product into it as the bridge it is, and rewrites four of the seven constraints where the audit changed their status.

**14.1 The layers.**

| Layer | Content | Status in v2.1 |
|---|---|---|
| **L0 — Measure (new)** | Volume/scale (M3) and state (M2) as the structures from which duration is recovered (§12); Conjecture M; D-14 | Three theorem-grade instances; the join is conjectural |
| **L1 — Causal order** | A partial order of events, continuum or locally finite (the repaired chess analogy: branching order obstructs a single global label) | Kept. *Changed:* discreteness is no longer an "adopted assumption"; it is open, and any discreteness scale must be derived (D-9). Order + Number is the L0–L1 interface |
| **L2 — Quantum reference frames** | Clocks and frames as physical subsystems; covariant transformation of descriptions under frame change (the Trinity; perspective-neutral framework) | Established in finite settings; the algebraic form of a frame change is the crossed product (GL-6) |
| **L3 — Conditional dynamics** | Page–Wootters conditioning; Schrödinger evolution relative to a clock (§9) | Theorem for fixed ideal clocks; non-ideal and dynamical clocks open (G2; WP1) |
| **L4 — State and flow** | Modular flow of the physical state as candidate time; Connes cocycle between clocks; **the crossed product**, which adjoins the L2 clock to the L4 algebra with the gravitational constraint (§11) | Mathematics theorem-grade; thermal time conjectural; crossed product theorem-grade at leading semiclassical order |
| **L5 — Formal shell** | The category-theoretic layer distinction (first-order normalized / higher-order compact-closed / unnormalized), the corrected no-adjoint conjecture, the rate–distortion pricing of clocks (§10) | Layer distinction: theorem-grade; C-NOADJ: conjecture, unrefereed; the "wall" reading withdrawn (Proposition 10.1) |

v2.0's reading of the stack as "appearances of the one" is replaced by the Lighthouse's: the layers are real, dependent structures at different levels of the created order (§8, gain iii), and the stack is a dependency architecture *about something*.

**14.3 What the framework does not claim** (v2.0 §10.3, retained and extended). Not that the intercept principle is proved; not that the thermal time hypothesis is confirmed; not that type III₁ or type II is derived from anything but quantum field theory and the semiclassical constraint; not that space from entanglement is a completed derivation; not that the standard form is more than mathematics; not a solution to the measurement problem; not an origin for the low-entropy condition; not a derivation of the constants; not an explanation of consciousness; and — *new* — not that the real distinction is a physical result, not that any structure here pictures the ground, and not that the problem is dissolved.

## §15 The dependency architecture and the work packages

**15.1 Three senses of solution — now two.** v2.0 distinguished derivation, mechanism, and dissolution, and held only the third "unconditionally available." Under the Lighthouse there are two (§6, item 16): *derivation* (classical time derived from a stationary structure) and *mechanism* (an account of how time emerges from something more fundamental). Both are the plan's business; both are checkable; neither is done. The architecture is a dependency architecture, not a proof chain: it establishes "if A then B," and leaves the absence of the atoms visible.

**15.5 Tractable targets.** T1 — extend geometric modular action beyond wedges and conformal spheres (A2). **T2 — void** as stated ("area law for the modular ground state"; Proposition 11.1); *replaced by* **T2′** — the area term as the entropy relative to the trace for general subregions and beyond leading order (continuing JSS 2023; feeds A9). T3 — exhibit a spectral triple as the modular structure of a specific state (A7 + GL-1). T4 — the Schrödinger equation as the semiclassical limit of the modular flow (A3 + A8). **T5** (new) — characterize when the gravitational algebra is clock-independent (P15; Chen–Penington versus DEHK).

**15.6 Conditional chains** (v2.0 §11.3, revised). *Geometrodynamics:* if A1, A3, A8 hold and GL-1 connects modular to spectral data, A6 follows as a linearized fluctuation, and with A2 plus Jacobson's input the Einstein equations follow as an equation of state. *New alternative route:* trace → generalized entropy (theorem at leading order) → Jacobson 2016 (entanglement equilibrium) → Einstein equations; this route bypasses GL-1 and is the measure-first chain of §12.2. *Probability:* if A4 holds, the projection postulate follows as an approximation to conditioning and the Bell correlations follow without microcausality violation — *with the amendment of G20:* under absoluteness, actualization is nonlocal even though signalling is not. *Parameters:* if A5 holds, the couplings are invariants of the modular data and the Standard Model enters through T3. *Arrow* (new): if A9 holds, the generalized second law (Faulkner–Speranza 2024) gives the arrow in form and the low-entropy condition gives it in fact (§17). Every chain is conditional throughout.

**15.8 Milestones.** Year 1: WP0, WP1, WP2 deliver a worked crossed-product example, a laboratory, and a first distortion measure. Year 2: WP3 verification completes. Year 3: WP4 delivers an extension or a negative result. Years 3–4: WP5's effective generator. Years 4–5: WP6 and WP7 deliver the layer interfaces and the experimental update. The priority order of the tractable targets is T5 and T1 first (both now have a literature moving toward them), T2′ second, T4 third, T3 fourth. Failure at any milestone is informative and is recorded in §20, not hidden.

**In plain terms.** Nine components must be manufactured by hand, six connectors must be fabricated (two of them have been cancelled, one has just arrived half-built from other people's workshops), and two wish-list items still cannot be ordered. The one genuinely new fact since v2.0 is that the connector the whole diagram was waiting for — the one that joins "clocks are physical systems" to "the state fixes the flow" — now exists in the literature under the name *crossed product*, which is why the first work package is to learn it. What used to be the second-most-tractable target, an area law for a "modular ground state," has been struck, because such a state cannot exist; its replacement asks the same question of the one distinguished state that does exist, which happens to sit at the wrong end of time.
