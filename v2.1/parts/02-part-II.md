# Part II — The problem of time, and what the metaphysics may say about it

## §7 The problem, stated at full strength

**7.1 The two equations.** Quantum mechanics evolves a closed system's state by an external, classical, universal parameter that is not an observable:

  iħ ∂ₜ|ψ⟩ = Ĥ|ψ⟩.  (7-1)

Canonical general relativity, written in Hamiltonian (ADM) form, has a Hamiltonian that is a pure constraint: time evolution is a gauge transformation. Quantizing, the Schrödinger equation for a closed universe becomes the Wheeler–DeWitt equation,

  Ĥ Ψ[h, φ] = 0,  (7-2)

in which no time parameter appears at all. This is the frozen formalism (Kuchař 1992; Isham 1993; Anderson 2017; Carlip–Hu 2023 for a modern review).

**7.2 Why it is a genuine clash and not bookkeeping.** Special relativity and quantum mechanics were reconciled by quantum field theory — not by making time an operator but by demoting position to a label, so that both x and t are background parameters on a fixed spacetime. (Pauli's observation that a self-adjoint time operator conjugate to a bounded-below Hamiltonian cannot exist stands; covariant time observables are POVMs, not projection-valued.) Quantum field theory on a fixed curved background also works, with the warning that "particle" becomes slicing-dependent (Unruh 1976). The trouble begins when the metric is dynamical and quantum:

- The *t* of (7-1) is a coordinate; in general relativity coordinates are gauge.
- Under (7-2) the state does not evolve, there is no agreed inner product on its solution space (the equation is Klein–Gordon-like, not Schrödinger-like), and the gauge-invariant observables — those commuting with all constraints — are constants of motion.
- A superposition of geometries is a superposition of causal structures, so there is no definite "before" and "after" in which to pose the only kind of question quantum mechanics knows how to answer: the probability of X at time t.

Time plays three roles in quantum mechanics — the evolution parameter, the "now" on which the state is defined and probabilities normalized, and what clocks read. Relativity pulls them apart: only the third (duration along a worldline) is physical, and the formalism of (7-1) is built on the first two. In the Lighthouse's lexicon (§5): quantum mechanics needs a *label* that is not gauge; relativity has *order* and *duration* and no such label.

**7.3 The families of answer, with status** (this is the field's map, not the plan's verdict).

**7.4 Where the field stands.** If there is a majority view among quantum-gravity theorists, it is that the absolute time of quantum mechanics is not fundamental: at the deepest level the description is timeless or relational, and the Schrödinger *t* is what one obtains when some degree of freedom is classical enough to serve as a clock. The disagreement is over everything that matters: which variables are clocks; whether the emergence is exact or approximate; whether a preferred foliation is secretly required; and whether the *flow* and *arrow* of time need ingredients beyond the existence of a time variable. The *arrow* is a separate problem (§17). The problem of time is whether, and how, a time variable exists at all.

**7.5 The experimental interface, so far.** No experiment discriminates between the families. The interface is becoming testable at its edges: optical clocks resolving gravitational redshift across a millimetre (2022); proposals to place a clock in a superposition of heights (Zych et al.); tabletop gravitationally-induced-entanglement proposals (Bose et al.; Marletto–Vedral); quantum-switch realizations of indefinite causal order (optical, not gravitational). The plan's WP7 is pointed at this interface.

## §8 What the metaphysics may say — the override applied

The v2.0 plan adopted (its §6) a non-dual working assumption and used it, after retracting its attempted derivations, as a *selection filter* on fundamental theories. The Lighthouse replaces the assumption. The table records what the filter said under each metaphysics; the right-hand column governs.

**What the override gives the plan that non-duality could not.** Three things. (i) *Contingency:* the Past Hypothesis and any state-selection principle were an embarrassment to a necessary Absolute; under creation they are what one expects, and the demand on them shifts from "why any selection?" to "is the selection lawful?" (D-8). (ii) *Permission for a privileged clock:* the single most consequential change, because the measure-first heuristic's sharpest physical forms (§12) are preferred-time forms. (iii) *Real levels:* the plan's layered architecture (§14) is no longer "appearances of the one" but a hierarchy of real, grounded structures, which is what a dependency architecture needs in order to be about anything.

**One cost the override adds.** v2.0 §9.3 read quantum "actualization" relationally: definiteness relative to the correlated subsystem, not absolute (relational quantum mechanics). The Lighthouse's measurement reading says the definite outcome is the **I2**-intrinsic fact. Override wins: facts are absolute — known to God, perspectival only for creatures. That commitment has a price Bell alone does not charge: the extended Wigner's-friend theorems (Frauchiger–Renner 2018; Bong et al. 2020, "local friendliness") show that *absoluteness of observed events* + locality + no-superdeterminism is inconsistent with quantum predictions. So D-7 plus absoluteness forces nonlocal *actualization*, not merely nonlocal correlation. The Lighthouse already holds locality to be emergent, so the position is consistent; the cost is entered in the ledger (§16; G20).

**What the metaphysics may not do, restated for this problem.** It may not supply a clock (God is not a clock, a field, or a frame). It may not fill the gap where the inner product on solutions of (7-2) is missing. It may not read a mechanism of emergence off any verse. It may not call the problem dissolved. What it may do is what it has done in this section: fix the jurisdiction, remove a false constraint (no privileged clock), add a true permission (contingency), and insist that every remaining question be answered by construction or left open.
