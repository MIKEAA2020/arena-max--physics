## §11 The modular programme, re-anchored and brought up to date

v2.0's fourth arc assembled the modular theory of von Neumann algebras as the language of a state that defines its own time flow, then retracted every derivation of it from metaphysical attributes while keeping the mathematics. v2.1 keeps the mathematics, states it without the glosses the Lighthouse forbids, corrects two statements, and adds the 2022–2025 results in which the programme's central move — put the clock inside the algebra — has been carried out with the gravitational constraint.

**11.1 The pair, and the fixed point (theorem).** The fundamental object is a von Neumann algebra 𝔐 on a Hilbert space with a unit vector Ω that is cyclic (𝔐Ω is dense) and separating (aΩ = 0 implies a = 0). The vacuum of a relativistic quantum field theory is cyclic and separating for every local algebra (Reeh–Schlieder). The antilinear map S: aΩ ↦ a*Ω is closable; its closure has polar decomposition S = JΔ^{1/2}, with Δ positive self-adjoint (the modular operator) and J an antiunitary involution (the modular conjugation). Tomita–Takesaki: Δ^{it}𝔐Δ^{−it} = 𝔐 and J𝔐J = 𝔐′. Since SΩ = Ω and JΩ = Ω, ΔΩ = Ω. That is the whole content of "the self-referential state": a theorem about any cyclic separating vector. It is not an image of self-existence, of mind, or of the ground (§6, items 4, 9, 17).

**11.2 Modular flow as candidate time.** σ_t(a) = Δ^{it} a Δ^{−it} is a one-parameter group of automorphisms of 𝔐: intrinsic (nothing external enters), canonical (the unique flow satisfying the KMS condition for ω at inverse temperature one — theorem), and state-preserving (ω∘σ_t = ω, so the state is stationary while the observables flow). Its generator K = −log Δ is formally a Heisenberg-picture Hamiltonian. The **thermal time hypothesis** (Connes–Rovelli 1994) identifies the modular parameter of the physical state with physical time. Register: **conjecture** — with one exact geometric instance and two nontrivial checks. The instance: for a Rindler wedge in Minkowski space the modular flow of the vacuum is the Lorentz boost (Bisognano–Wichmann 1975), the modular parameter is Rindler time, and the KMS condition is the Unruh effect. The checks: for the cosmic background radiation state the thermal time coincides with cosmological time (Rovelli 1993); for a finite-lifetime observer the thermal time of a double cone yields a temperature with the expected Unruh limit (Martinetti–Rovelli 2003). Type structure is not optional: in a thermofield-double state whose mixing operators close under multiplication, the algebra is type III₁ (Furuya–Lashkari–Moosa–Ouseph 2023) — forced by thermal mixing, not by metaphysics.

**The measure reading (M2), which is exact.** A faithful normal state ω on 𝔐 is a noncommutative probability measure — on a commutative algebra L^∞(X, μ) the normal states are exactly the probability measures absolutely continuous with respect to μ. Tomita–Takesaki says: *the measure determines a flow.* Connes's cocycle theorem (1973) says: for two faithful normal states, σ_t^φ(a) = u_t σ_t^ω(a) u_t* with u_t = (Dφ:Dω)_t the **noncommutative Radon–Nikodym derivative** — the change of measure is the change of clock — and consequently the composite ℝ → Aut(𝔐) → Out(𝔐) is independent of the state: *the algebra alone fixes the flow up to inner automorphisms; the state fixes the rest.* On a commutative algebra every state is tracial and the flow is trivial: a classical measure generates no time. Time-from-measure is a strictly noncommutative phenomenon, which is the point Connes and Rovelli made in their title. This is the Lighthouse's "measure precedes duration" in its sharpest available form: **law (algebra) → duration up to gauge; measure (state) → which clock.** It is a translation of the heuristic into a theorem, not a derivation of physics from the heuristic.

**11.3 Clocks as modular flows; the cocycle as the transformation between clocks.** A clock is a subsystem with a state, (𝔑, ω_𝔑), with its own modular flow; equivalence classes of such pairs correspond to conjugacy classes of modular flows (theorem, via Tomita–Takesaki). The clock ambiguity of §9.3 becomes "which modular flow is the time?" The cocycles between all pairs of states form a groupoid; the reading that the cocycle is a gauge field, the absolute flow gauge-dependent, and the relative modular structure the invariant content, is an **interpretation** of a theorem, and is kept as such. Two statements in v2.0 are corrected here. First, "the Connes–Takesaki theorem makes the unitary group act transitively on faithful normal states" is not correct as an exact statement: the result is **Connes–Størmer homogeneity** (1978) — a factor is type III₁ if and only if for any two faithful normal states and any ε > 0 there is a unitary u with ‖φ∘Ad u − ψ‖ < ε, i.e. transitivity holds in norm closure, approximately. (The Connes–Takesaki flow of weights is a different object; for III₁ it is trivial.) "Exactly one physical state up to gauge" is therefore not even available as an interpretation of an exact theorem. Second, and more important, the gauge reading is contradicted at the level that matters by §11.4: in the gravitational algebras, changing the clock changes the algebra.

**11.4 The crossed product: the clock inside the algebra (2022–2025).** The v2.0 plan asked how a clock could be inserted into a timeless algebraic description with the gravitational constraint respected. That has been done, semiclassically, and the results change the honest status of three of the plan's questions.

- **Witten 2022.** For a large-N holographic theory in the thermofield-double (black-hole) state, the boundary single-trace algebra is type III₁ (Leutheusser–Liu 2021–23, who also show that half-sided modular inclusions of that algebra generate an *emergent time* in the black-hole interior). Adjoining the generator of time translations — i.e. passing to the **crossed product 𝔐 ⋊_σ ℝ of the algebra by its own modular flow** — yields a type II_∞ algebra with a trace, hence a renormalized entropy. Chandrasekaran–Penington–Witten 2023: that entropy is the generalized entropy.
- **Chandrasekaran–Longo–Penington–Witten 2023.** In the static patch of de Sitter space, with an observer consisting of nothing but a clock whose Hamiltonian is bounded below, the algebra of observables gravitationally dressed to the observer's worldline is a **type II₁** factor. It has a trace defined on all of its elements, hence entropies for all states; it has a **maximum-entropy state** — empty de Sitter (the Bunch–Davies state) tensored with a simple state of the observer — whose density matrix is the identity; and the entropy of any semiclassical state agrees, up to a state-independent constant, with the generalized entropy A/4G + S_out. Without the observer, the constraint leaves no nontrivial observables at all.
- **Jensen–Sorce–Speranza 2023**: the construction for general subregions with an observer. **Witten 2023**: a background-independent algebra along an observer's worldline. **Chen–Penington 2024** ("A clock is just a way to tell the time"): gravitational algebras in cosmological spacetimes, with the argument that, within their assumptions, the algebra obtained does not depend on which clock is used. **Kudler-Flam–Leutheusser–Satishchandran 2024**: algebraic observational cosmology — observers in expanding universes.
- **De Vuyst–Eccles–Höhn–Kirklin 2024–25.** The observers in these constructions are quantum reference frames in the Trinity's sense; a proper accounting of gauge invariance and observer degrees of freedom converts the type III algebra to a type II crossed product; and **different QRFs give different algebras and different entropies** — gravitational entropy is observer-dependent, with explicit examples (a gravitational interferometer; degenerate clock superselection; monotonic versus periodic clocks). **De Vuyst–Höhn–Tsobanjan 2025** relate the perspective-neutral, algebraic, and effective QRF formalisms. **Fewster–Janssen–Loveridge–Rejzner–Waldron 2024**: QRF dressing changes the type of local algebras in QFT measurement schemes. **Faulkner–Speranza 2024**: the generalized second law in gravitational algebras.

**Consequences for the plan** (all entered in §15, §19, §20).

1. *The type question, honestly* (replacing v2.0 §7.4): the local algebras of quantum field theory on a background are hyperfinite type III₁; once the gravitational constraint and a clock are included, the algebra is type II (II_∞ with a boundary time; II₁ for a bounded-below observer Hamiltonian in de Sitter), semiclassically, to leading order in G. The "resonance" v2.0 found between non-duality and type III₁ was with an algebra that lacked its clock.
2. *The clock is inserted with the constraint.* These constructions are Page–Wootters in von Neumann-algebraic form, with gravity — the first setting in which §9's open item 4 has a worked instance.
3. *The clock ambiguity is not pure gauge* at this level: the algebra and its entropies depend on the clock (DEHK), while in some cosmological settings they do not (Chen–Penington). When the gravitational algebra is clock-independent is **P15**, and the entropy difference between clock choices is the physical distortion measure that WP2 lacked.
4. *A selection principle supplied by the algebra.* In type II₁ the trace is a state and it is the maximum-entropy state. That is a distinguished state the algebra itself provides. It is the *wrong end* of the arrow (§11.6), but it is the first state-selection fact in the programme that is a theorem.
5. *The relational and modular layers are bridged.* v2.0's glue "modular theory ↔ holography" was "established in special cases"; the glue the plan actually needed — **quantum reference frames ↔ crossed products** (GL-6) — is now partly established (DEHK; DHT). WP0 is added to absorb it.

**11.5 The standard form, and space.** The standard form (𝔐, ℋ, J, 𝒫) — algebra, Hilbert space, modular conjugation, self-dual natural cone — is unique up to unitary equivalence; faithful normal states correspond bijectively to vectors in the cone's interior (Araki–Connes); the algebra is recoverable from the cone. It is a theorem that algebra and state are two aspects of one structure *in this mathematical sense*. Nothing about the ground follows; the row "the standard form is the Absolute" is struck (§8). The emergent-space material is carried at the status v2.0's retraction fixed: a Connes-type distance on the state space as a candidate spatial metric, the cocycle as a connection, the reduction to a Bures or Fisher metric — interpretation or speculation; the modular-inclusion and crossed-product programme (Wiesbrock's half-sided modular inclusions; Liu 2025; Leutheusser–Liu's emergent times), entanglement-based geometry (van Raamsdonk 2010; Ryu–Takayanagi 2006), and Jacobson's 1995 derivation of the Einstein equation as an equation of state given the area law and the Unruh temperature — active research with partial results, not a completed derivation.

**11.6 State selection, repaired.** v2.0 §7.6 proposed three layered selection principles and named the second "the Past Hypothesis layer: the state of minimal modular energy — the modular ground state, with modular Hamiltonian positive semidefinite — whose positivity would give the flow a preferred direction." v2.0 §6.2 had already retracted "the Past-Hypothesis synthesis" as invented before §7.6 reinstated it. It must stay retracted, for a reason that is a theorem:

> **Proposition 11.1 (no modular ground state on a type III algebra).** For a faithful normal state ω on a von Neumann algebra, JΔJ = Δ⁻¹, so the spectrum of Δ is invariant under λ ↦ 1/λ. Hence K = −log Δ ≥ 0 ⇔ Δ ≤ 1 ⇔ Δ ≥ 1 ⇔ Δ = 1 ⇔ σ_t = id ⇔ ω is a trace. Type III algebras admit no faithful normal trace; tracial *states* exist only in types I_n and II₁. In type III₁ the spectrum of K is all of ℝ for every faithful normal state (Connes spectrum ℝ₊). Therefore no state on the algebras of §11.1–11.3 has a positive semidefinite modular Hamiltonian, and "minimal modular energy" has no minimizer.

So the Past-Hypothesis layer, T2 ("area law for the modular ground state"), and gaps G8 and G11 are void as written. The repair uses §11.4. In the clock-dressed **type II₁** algebra a trace exists and *is* a state; but it is the **maximum-entropy** state — in de Sitter, empty de Sitter — i.e. the equilibrium end, not the low-entropy beginning. The arrow therefore cannot come from modular positivity. It can only come from a state of **low entropy relative to the trace**, which is a *contingent* selection. Under the non-dual assumption that was an embarrassment; under the Lighthouse it is what one expects of a created order, and the demand on it is D-8's: the selection must be lawful — derivable, not brute (§17). The generalized second law in gravitational algebras (Faulkner–Speranza 2024) then supplies the arrow *in form* — entropy relative to the trace does not decrease — while the low-entropy state supplies it *in fact*.

The four classical-time features no layer yet delivers — a single shared parameter, Lorentzian signature, the Schrödinger equation for general subsystems, the Einstein equations — are atoms in §15.

**In plain terms.** Take an algebra of observables and one faithful state, and nothing else: pure algebra manufactures a flow, fixed by the pair, with the state sitting still while the observables move. In one proven case that flow is the Lorentz boost of a wedge of spacetime, Unruh temperature included. Because the state is a measure and the measure fixes the flow, the Lighthouse's slogan — measure precedes duration — is, here, a theorem. What v2.0 did not have is the news: since 2022 the clock has been put *inside* the algebra together with the gravitational constraint, the algebra's type changes, entropy becomes definable, and in de Sitter space the algebra singles out a state all by itself — the maximally mixed one, the empty universe. That is the far end of time's arrow, not its start; the start must be a contingent low-entropy state, which is exactly what a created order should have and exactly what the plan must still show to be lawful. And one thing v2.0 believed is now wrong: swapping clocks is not harmless bookkeeping. In these algebras, a different clock is a different algebra with a different entropy.
