## Summary in one page

**The problem.** Quantum theory writes its basic equation with an external, universal, classical time parameter. General relativity has no such parameter: coordinate time is a gauge label, causal order is invariant, and duration is a path-dependent functional read by a clock. Canonical quantization of a closed universe turns this into the Wheeler–DeWitt constraint ĤΨ = 0, in which no time appears, the gauge-invariant observables are constants, there is no agreed inner product, and a superposition of geometries is a superposition of causal structures with no shared "before" and "after." Nothing in this document solves this. The field's working answer — time is relational and emergent, recovered as correlations with physical clocks inside a stationary whole — is adopted as the preferred shape, with its open problems (which clock; inequivalent quantum theories for different clocks; no global clock; recovery of classical spacetime; ordering versus passage) kept visible.

**What the metaphysics contributes, and only this.** Constraints: no God-as-clock, no God-as-field, no gaps filled with the divine; time belongs to the created order and the ground is prior in being, not in time; the created order is closed *as physics* (no external physical clock) and dependent *metaphysically* (a grounding, not a clock). Permissions: the created state is contingent, so a selected state and a privileged clock are admissible — physics must earn them, not assume them. Prohibitions on itself: no dissolution of the problem by re-description; no verse yields a mechanism; no structure of physics is identified with the ground.

**What the plan does.** It keeps the relational core (Page–Wootters, the Trinity, quantum reference frames) and the modular programme (Tomita–Takesaki flow as candidate time; the Connes cocycle as the transformation between clocks), repairs three errors in v2.0's formal core, and brings the modular programme up to date: since 2022, adjoining a clock to the type III₁ algebra of quantum field theory — the crossed product by the modular flow — yields type II algebras with a trace, an entropy, and (in de Sitter) a distinguished maximum-entropy state. That work is *the plan's own title carried out*, it bridges the plan's relational and modular layers, and it changes the honest status of the type question, the clock-ambiguity question, and the state-selection question.

**The one new structural claim.** *Measure precedes duration* — the Lighthouse's central heuristic — has three exact physical instances: a Lorentzian metric is causal order plus a volume measure (M3); a clock requires a scale, so a scale-free world has order but no duration (M3); and a state on a noncommutative algebra determines a canonical flow, so a measure (M2) determines a time — which is the thermal time hypothesis, exactly. In the third form the clock ambiguity *is* the measure ambiguity, and the Lighthouse's two open questions "time's emergence" (P2) and "which measure" (P3) are one question. This is a conjecture about structure, not a result, and it is the plan's organizing bet.

**What could fail.** Fourteen predictions with falsifiers (§18); eight work packages (WP0–WP7) with go/no-go criteria (§15); twenty registered gaps (§20). The plan predicts no new particle, no new number, and no quantum-gravity signal. It predicts the shape of the final theory, and names the result that would show the shape wrong.

7. **Informational at the level of structure** [H]. Finite information density, redundancy and error correction, level-relative rendering, an interface between knower and known. ## §4 The engine, repaired

**The rule [C], unchanged.** Every apparent contradiction is a polarity misread as a competition. The resolution is not to pick a pole; it is to find the axis.

**The repair.** v1.0's inventory held thirteen axes and admitted that an axis which classifies or predicts nothing is a metaphor. It proposed "which conserved quantity does each polarity have?" as the test. That is the wrong test: conservation laws come from continuous symmetries (Noether), not from dual pairs. The audit replaces it with two admission tests, either of which qualifies an axis for band 1; an inventory entry that passes neither is retired to the glossary.

- **The trade-off test.** A constituting polarity that does work is a *conjugate pair*: two descriptions each of which requires the other and which cannot both be sharp — time/frequency, position/momentum, number/phase, **volume/York time** (§12), the discrete/continuous pair under Pontryagin duality (the dual of the integers is the circle). Such an axis carries an inequality (uncertainty, duality bound, rate–distortion curve), and the inequality is its research content.
- **The adjunction test.** A constituting polarity may instead be an *adjoint pair of constructions* — a free/forgetful, discrete/continuous, or syntax/semantics relation in which neither side is reducible to the other and each is determined by the other up to canonical isomorphism. This is the formal home of "unity and identity of opposites": Lawvere proposed it in 1969, developed it through the 1990s, and in *axiomatic cohesion* (2007) made discrete ↔ continuous a precise string of four adjoints (components ⊣ discrete ⊣ points ⊣ codiscrete); his *Aufhebung* is a formal notion of levels in which each level resolves the one below it — which is this map's band structure. Schreiber's differential cohesion extends the apparatus to gauge fields. **This is P7's best current candidate** and it is now written there.

**The inventory after the tests [H].** Admitted on the trade-off test: measure ↔ duration (as volume/York time, and as state/flow — §11–§12); discrete ↔ continuous; definite ↔ superposed (as the complementarity trade-off); intrinsic ↔ relational in sense **I2** (as the contextuality trade-off, §16). Admitted on the adjunction test: being ↔ manifestation (as grounding/emergence — but only as *formal grammar*; the ground itself is not a term of any adjunction, §1); timeless ↔ temporal (as the stationary/conditional pair of §9 and the auxiliary/physical pair of §10); background-free ↔ background-full (as the slice adjunction of §10, whose right adjoint is Newtonian time). Retired to the glossary pending a test: spaceless ↔ spatial; simple ↔ composite; unity ↔ multiplicity; stability ↔ novelty; manifest ↔ hidden; necessary ↔ contingent; silent ↔ speaking. Retirement is not denial: a retired entry may re-enter band 1 the day it carries an inequality or an adjunction.

**The asymmetry [C].** On every admitted axis: ground prior in being, manifestation prior in appearing. In a conjugate pair this asymmetry is *not* a priority of one variable over the other — the pair is symmetric — and so the slogan "measure *precedes* duration" is read as in §12: not that measure is prior to duration within physics, but that duration requires a scale and a state, while order does not.

**The internal check [C], unchanged.** Polarity (formal), gradation (ontological), and two-aspect structure (epistemic) must be one idea viewed three ways. If they become three assumptions, the framework costs more than it earns.

**The dissolutions, re-graded [C].** A re-description earns nothing; a dissolution counts only where it rules something out. v1.0 credited one of its four dissolutions (measurement) with forbidding something. The audit withdraws even that credit as stated: what the measurement reading forbids and can be scored on (collapse) is forbidden equally by Everett, Bohm, relational quantum mechanics and QBism; what is distinctive to it (no branching) is not empirically scorable. D-7 stands as a bet (§18); it is not earned by the dissolution. All four "dissolutions" are now **readings**; the problem of time in particular is **open** (P2) and nothing in this document dissolves it.

## §5 Lexical discipline [C]

Five terms carried more than one sense across the sources. Each use in this document carries its tag; a claim that mixes tags is a design error.

**Measure.**
- **M1** — *qadar*: determination, proportion, ordination (54:49). First-principles register only. Never a quantity.
- **M2** — a weighting over possibilities: a probability measure; a quantum state as a positive normalized functional on an algebra (a noncommutative probability measure); the Born measure; a cosmological measure on histories.
- **M3** — metric measure: a volume element, the conformal factor of a metric, a scale, a discreteness scale, the "Number" of causal set theory.

The Lighthouse's heuristic "measure precedes duration" is true in two exact senses and conjectural in a third (§12): duration requires M3 (a scale); a flow is determined by M2 (a state); and the conjecture is that the M2 and M3 structures of the fundamental description have a common origin. "*Qadar* is the bridge" is the M1 anchor for that conjecture — vocabulary, not evidence (§6.10).

**Intrinsic.**
- **I1** — what it is like to be a structure (the inside; §1.10; P5).
- **I2** — the actual outcome, as opposed to the superposed description (§16).

v1.0 used one word for both and so assigned creaturely experience to opposite poles in two of its dissolutions. The two senses are now separated; nothing in Part III uses I1.

**Information.** Unless stated otherwise, *information* means quantum information: Hilbert-space structure, entanglement, von Neumann entropy, and — in the algebraic setting — states on von Neumann algebras and relative entropy. Shannon and algorithmic senses are named when used. "Information is physical" (Landauer; Bekenstein) is **established** in the sense that erasure costs work (verified experimentally) and that entropy bounds hold in the regimes tested; "information is more basic than matter" is **[H]**.

**Time.** *Label* — a coordinate; gauge in general relativity; *not* gauge in quantum mechanics, where the Schrödinger parameter is external and reparametrizing it changes the dynamics. *Order* — causal precedence; invariant; determines the metric up to a conformal factor in distinguishing spacetimes (Hawking–King–McCarthy; Malament). *Duration* — proper time; a path functional read by a clock. The tripartition is internal to classical relativity and restates the problem rather than solving it (§7).

**Observer.** Never used without one of three senses: a *relational quantity* (a subsystem relative to which a description is given), *subsystem information* (what a subsystem's state encodes), or an *epistemic limit* (what an agent can know). "The observer collapses the state" is not a sentence of this document.

# Part II — The problem of time, and what the metaphysics may say about it

## §7 The problem, stated at full strength

**7.1 The two equations.** Quantum mechanics evolves a closed system's state by an external, classical, universal parameter that is not an observable:

  iħ ∂ₜ|ψ⟩ = Ĥ|ψ⟩.  (7-1)

Canonical general relativity, written in Hamiltonian (ADM) form, has a Hamiltonian that is a pure constraint: time evolution is a gauge transformation. Quantizing, the Schrödinger equation for a closed universe becomes the Wheeler–DeWitt equation,

  Ĥ Ψ[h, φ] = 0,  (7-2)

in which no time parameter appears at all. This is the frozen formalism (Kuchař 1992; Isham 1993; Anderson 2017; Carlip–Hu 2023 for a modern review).

**7.2 Why it is a genuine clash and not bookkeeping.** Special relativity and quantum mechanics were reconciled by quantum field theory — not by making time an operator but by demoting position to a label, so that both x and t are background parameters on a fixed spacetime. (Pauli's observation that a self-adjoint time operator conjugate to a bounded-below Hamiltonian cannot exist stands; covariant time observables are POVMs, not projection-valued.) Quantum field theory on a fixed curved background also works, with the warning that "particle" becomes slicing-dependent (Unruh 1976). The trouble begins when the metric is dynamical and quantum:

- The *t* of (7-1) is a coordinate; in general relativity coordinates are gauge.
- Under (7-2) the state does not evolve, there is no agreed inner product on its solution space (the equation is Klein–Gordon-like, not Schrödinger-like), and the gauge-invariant observables — those commuting with all constraints — are constants of motion.
- A superposition of geometries is a superposition of causal structures, so there is no definite "before" and "after" in which to pose the only kind of question quantum mechanics knows how to answer: the probability of X at time t.

Time plays three roles in quantum mechanics — the evolution parameter, the "now" on which the state is defined and probabilities normalized, and what clocks read. Relativity pulls them apart: only the third (duration along a worldline) is physical, and the formalism of (7-1) is built on the first two. In the Lighthouse's lexicon (§5): quantum mechanics needs a *label* that is not gauge; relativity has *order* and *duration* and no such label.

## §9 The relational core

**9.1 The mechanism** (Page–Wootters 1983). A universe satisfying a constraint of the form (7-2) is partitioned into a clock C and a system S with non-interacting total Hamiltonian Ĥ = Ĥ_C ⊗ 1 + 1 ⊗ Ĥ_S. The clock carries a time observable T̂_C conjugate to Ĥ_C (ideally [T̂_C, Ĥ_C] = iħ; realistically a covariant POVM), with clock states |t⟩. Expanding the global state in the clock basis, |Ψ⟩⟩ = ∫dt |t⟩ ⊗ |ψ(t)⟩, the constraint yields the conditional Schrödinger equation iħ ∂ₜ|ψ(t)⟩ = Ĥ_S|ψ(t)⟩. "At time t" means "conditioned on the clock reading t." The correlations are entanglement in a stationary global state; an internal observer sees ordinary evolution; the global state does not evolve. Realized with an entangled photon pair, one photon serving as the clock for the other (Moreva et al. 2014).

**9.2 Critique and repair.** Kuchař's objection — that conditioning applies a projection that does not commute with the constraint, and so yields wrong propagators for relativistic systems — is answered, within its scope, by the *Trinity* (Höhn–Smith–Lock 2021), which establishes by explicit reduction maps the equivalence of three pictures: relational Dirac observables in a clock-neutral formulation; Page–Wootters conditional states; and a relational Heisenberg picture obtained by symmetry reduction. Relational observables are quantized with covariant POVMs encompassing non-ideal clocks, and conditional probabilities are computed in the physical Hilbert space from the start. Giovannetti–Lloyd–Maccone (2015) had already repaired the propagator issue by conditioning on clock POVMs. Castro-Ruiz, Giacomini, Belenchia and Brukner (2020) extend the picture to clocks that gravitate and to the temporal localizability of events among them.

**Scope [C].** The constructions are theorem-grade in finite-dimensional and idealizable settings with constant constraint algebras. Gravity has a constraint algebra with structure functions; the observable technology is not directly available there (G6). The first work package is confined to constant algebras.

**9.3 What remains open, and where each item goes.**

1. **The clock ambiguity (multiple-choice problem).** Different clock choices produce inequivalent conditional dynamics; nothing in the state ranks them. In v2.0 this was "the central quantitative object." In v2.1 it is sharpened twice: algebraically, clock choice is state choice and the transformation between clocks is the Connes cocycle (§11.3); and in the gravitational algebras of §11.4 the choice is *not* pure gauge — different clocks give different algebras and different entropies (De Vuyst–Eccles–Höhn–Kirklin 2024–25). The open question is P15.
2. **The tensor-product structure.** Page–Wootters presupposes a split into clock and rest. The Wheeler–DeWitt state does not come with one. Any principle that selects the split from the Hamiltonian's spectrum alone (the "quantum mereology" programme of Carroll–Singh 2021 is one attempt) is a principle that derives a clock from a measure in sense **M2** — and is, for that reason, exactly what the Lighthouse's heuristic asks for. Registered as G17.
3. **Ideal clocks.** Finite precision, back-reaction, decoherence; corrections only partially understood (G2; WP1).
4. **Dynamical geometry.** The mechanism is demonstrated on fixed backgrounds (G6; the crossed-product constructions of §11.4 are the first setting in which the clock is inserted *with* the gravitational constraint).
5. **Ordering versus passage.** Conditional correlations give a B-series (an ordering), not an A-series (a flow). "Becoming" is a posit the formalism does not supply. The one programme that supplies it — causal-set sequential growth — is admitted to the landscape at §13. The question is excluded from the falsifiable core (v2.0 §12.3 retained).

**In plain terms.** Split the universe into a clock and everything else, and ask what everything else is doing given the clock's reading: the answer obeys ordinary quantum mechanics, and a photon experiment has shown it. The old objection has a careful repair, in small systems. The residue is the choice itself — which part is the clock, and which split of the whole into parts — and this plan treats that residue not as a nuisance but as the thing to be derived.

## §10 The formal no-go layer, corrected

v2.0 unified its two analogies — a chess game read only through its notation, and a sphere whose inhabitants cannot reach the ambient room — into a "temporal confinement triple" and a conjecture (C-NOADJ) that it called the formal core's load-bearing wall. The analogies are kept in formalized form. The conjecture, as stated, is false, and the correction changes what the wall is.

**10.1 The analogies, formalized** (kept from v2.0 §4). *Chess:* E a finite set of half-moves totally ordered by ⪯; the move number c₀(eᵢ) = i; the players' time τ: E → ℝ strictly increasing with increments Δᵢ (thinking times). The intrinsic description is (E, ⪯); the external description is (E, ⪯, τ); the two clocks are related by a monotone, generally nonlinear change of variable, and an observer with access only to c₀ cannot reconstruct τ because the lost information is exactly the increment vector. Two corrections stand: the chess order is total and general relativity's is partial (Layer 1 uses causal sets); and chess has a preferred clock by rule, general relativity does not. The "twin paradox" reading of two chess clocks was refuted in v2.0 and stays refuted. *Sphere:* (M, g) isometrically embedded by i: M ↪ (E, h); intrinsic distance (arc) versus extrinsic distance (chord); the chord is not a path in the inhabitants' world. The mandatory repair (G3) stands: general relativity has no ambient room, so i is reinterpreted as the inclusion of the physical (constraint-satisfying) sector into the kinematical arena — the two-tier structure of Dirac quantization — and τ lives on the auxiliary space and is never an observable.

**10.2 The triple and the functor** (v2.0 §5.1, restated). Σ a causal poset or Lorentzian manifold with its intrinsic order; i: Σ → M the inclusion into the auxiliary kinematical space; τ: M → ℝ a global parameter on the auxiliary space. The category **Int** has ordered structures (X, ⪯) and order-preserving maps; **Ext** has parameter-equipped ordered structures (X, ⪯, τ) with τ monotone, and order- and parameter-preserving maps (f with τ′∘f = τ). The forgetful functor U: **Ext** → **Int** discards τ. A *clock* is a section of U. v2.0 conjectured that U has no natural adjoint, and read that absence as "no universal clock."

**10.3 The correction (theorem).** **Ext** is the slice category **Int**/(ℝ, ≤): ordered structures over the real line. For any category with binary products, the forgetful functor from a slice C/B to C has a right adjoint, X ↦ (X × B → B). Posets have products (the product order). Hence:

> **Proposition 10.1.** U: **Int**/ℝ → **Int** has a right adjoint R: X ↦ (X × ℝ, product order, τ = projection to ℝ). The unit sends (Y, σ) to y ↦ (y, σ(y)); the bijection **Ext**((Y, σ), R X) ≅ **Int**(Y, X) is f ↦ π₁∘f. U has no left adjoint: a left adjoint L would make **Ext**(L X, (ℝ, id)) — a one-element set — biject with **Int**(X, ℝ), which has more than one element whenever X is non-empty.

So an adjoint exists, canonically, and it is worth saying what it is: R X is X with an independent real line adjoined at every event — **Newtonian absolute time**, the cofree clock. What is genuinely absent is a natural *section* that selects one clock correlated with the order. Even that fails for the wrong reason: with τ merely monotone, the constant clock τ ≡ 0 is a natural section; with τ required strictly monotone, no section exists at all because some posets (ω₁, for instance) admit no strictly monotone real-valued map — a cardinality fact, not a physical one. The poset formalization therefore carries none of the intended content. It is retained only as a definition of vocabulary (G17).

**10.4 Where the content actually lives.** The v2.0 evidence for "no universal clock" came from a user-supplied, unrefereed manuscript on quantum combs (v2.0 ref. [10]) reporting that in three *normalized* process categories — **Chan** (completely positive trace-preserving maps), ordered instruments, and finite stochastic maps — the environment-decoration functor −⊗E has neither a left nor a right adjoint when dim E > 1, by an "intercept" argument: the affine dimension of a hom-set is a polynomial in which decoration rescales the source-linear term while the normalization constant stays rigid, so no object can serve as adjoint. Read with the clock as the environment, this is the categorical form of the clock ambiguity: no universal way to remove the clock and recover a timeless normalized description unless the clock is trivial. Two things must be said about it. First, in the form "CPTP maps do not form a compact closed category, so −⊗E has no internal adjoint in **Chan**," the statement is folklore in categorical quantum mechanics — it is precisely why Kissinger and Uijlen (2017) constructed the higher-order category **Caus[CPM]**, in which internal homs exist at the price of higher-order types and the loss of trace preservation at the base. The manuscript's contribution, if it holds, is the quantitative intercept mechanism and its companions (pointwise non-representability; a one-slice gap; persistence at every comb arity; a classical analogue; the bifibration criterion; a no-programming bound). Second, the transfer from finite-dimensional process combinatorics to constrained gravitational systems is an interpretation of the mathematics, not an application (G1).

**Conjecture C-NOADJ (restated, v2.1).** *In any normalized process category containing a nontrivial clock system E, the decoration functor −⊗E has no adjoint; equivalently, no finite-memory universal evaluator recovers a clock-free normalized description.* Register: conjecture, manuscript-supported, unrefereed. The v2.0 "five-fold equivalence" is reduced to the chain that is actually proved or reported: no adjoint for −⊗E ⇔ the intercept mismatch ⇔ no bifibration of stage extensions ⇒ no universal finite-memory evaluator. The clause "no universal adjoint recovering external time from intrinsic relational order alone" is removed: by Proposition 10.1 it was false in the setting where it was stated. WP3 verifies the manuscript's results independently and restates them for algebraic categories.

**10.5 The layer distinction** (v2.0 Table 5-1, kept). First-order normalized categories (**Chan**, **Instr**, **FinStoch**): no internal hom for −⊗E (C-NOADJ). Higher-order compact-closed (**Caus[CPM]**): internal homs exist; price: higher-order types, trace preservation lost at the base. Unnormalized (**CPM**): internal homs exist; price: probability conservation lost. The reformulation that follows is one of v2.0's cleanest surviving statements and is kept: *the external time of quantum mechanics is a higher-order causal structure — not representable in the first-order normalized process theory, emerging as an internal hom at the price of a higher-order type system.* In the analogy, the first-order category is the sphere's surface and **Caus[CPM]** is the room. In the Lighthouse's grammar (§4) this is an adjunction-type axis, and Proposition 10.1 adds a second, humbler one: the slice adjunction whose right adjoint is Newtonian time. Three questions stay open and enter WP3: whether the first-order normalized category is a quotient of **Caus[CPM]** under a physically motivated condition; whether the normalization intercept is a cohomological obstruction there; and whether the Connes cocycle of §11.3 supplies the descent data for that quotient.

**10.6 The rate–distortion shell** (v2.0 §5.4, status unchanged). With T_ext the auxiliary parameter and C a clock reading, R(D) = min I(T_ext; C) subject to mean-squared estimation error ≤ D prices each clock: an intrinsic observer cannot achieve zero distortion for all parameter values unless the clock carries unbounded information, and different clocks sit at different points of the curve. Status: a formal shell — no physical distortion functional between two clock choices has been derived, only candidates (G4; WP2). The bounded-sequential-transduction template (v2.0 ref. [11]) is imported for its comparative syntax only. v2.1 gives WP2 a concrete candidate it lacked: in the gravitational algebras of §11.4, different clocks yield different entropies, and an entropy difference between clock choices is a distortion measure with a theorem behind it.

**In plain terms.** Forget everything about a world except which events come before which; a clock is any principled way of putting a parameter back. v2.0 said there is no canonical way to do this. There is: adjoin a free real line to every event — which is Newton's absolute time, and which is exactly the thing relativity says the world does not have. The real obstruction is elsewhere: in a theory that must conserve probability, there is no universal way to *remove* a nontrivial clock and recover a clock-free description. That statement has a manuscript's mathematics behind it, folklore agreeing with its headline, and a scheduled verification. There is also a door: in a richer, higher-order theory of processes the missing construction exists, at the price of changing what counts as a process.
## §11 The modular programme, re-anchored and brought up to date

v2.0's fourth arc assembled the modular theory of von Neumann algebras as the language of a state that defines its own time flow, then retracted every derivation of it from metaphysical attributes while keeping the mathematics. v2.1 keeps the mathematics, states it without the glosses the Lighthouse forbids, corrects two statements, and adds the 2022–2025 results in which the programme's central move — put the clock inside the algebra — has been carried out with the gravitational constraint.

**11.1 The pair, and the fixed point (theorem).** The fundamental object is a von Neumann algebra 𝔐 on a Hilbert space with a unit vector Ω that is cyclic (𝔐Ω is dense) and separating (aΩ = 0 implies a = 0). The vacuum of a relativistic quantum field theory is cyclic and separating for every local algebra (Reeh–Schlieder). The antilinear map S: aΩ ↦ a*Ω is closable; its closure has polar decomposition S = JΔ^{1/2}, with Δ positive self-adjoint (the modular operator) and J an antiunitary involution (the modular conjugation). Tomita–Takesaki: Δ^{it}𝔐Δ^{−it} = 𝔐 and J𝔐J = 𝔐′. Since SΩ = Ω and JΩ = Ω, ΔΩ = Ω. That is the whole content of "the self-referential state": a theorem about any cyclic separating vector. It is not an image of self-existence, of mind, or of the ground (§6, items 4, 9, 17).

**11.2 Modular flow as candidate time.** σ_t(a) = Δ^{it} a Δ^{−it} is a one-parameter group of automorphisms of 𝔐: intrinsic (nothing external enters), canonical (the unique flow satisfying the KMS condition for ω at inverse temperature one — theorem), and state-preserving (ω∘σ_t = ω, so the state is stationary while the observables flow). Its generator K = −log Δ is formally a Heisenberg-picture Hamiltonian. The **thermal time hypothesis** (Connes–Rovelli 1994) identifies the modular parameter of the physical state with physical time. Register: **conjecture** — with one exact geometric instance and two nontrivial checks. The instance: for a Rindler wedge in Minkowski space the modular flow of the vacuum is the Lorentz boost (Bisognano–Wichmann 1975), the modular parameter is Rindler time, and the KMS condition is the Unruh effect. The checks: for the cosmic background radiation state the thermal time coincides with cosmological time (Rovelli 1993); for a finite-lifetime observer the thermal time of a double cone yields a temperature with the expected Unruh limit (Martinetti–Rovelli 2003). Type structure is not optional: in a thermofield-double state whose mixing operators close under multiplication, the algebra is type III₁ (Furuya–Lashkari–Moosa–Ouseph 2023) — forced by thermal mixing, not by metaphysics.

**The measure reading (M2), which is exact.** A faithful normal state ω on 𝔐 is a noncommutative probability measure — on a commutative algebra L^∞(X, μ) the normal states are exactly the probability measures absolutely continuous with respect to μ. Tomita–Takesaki says: *the measure determines a flow.* Connes's cocycle theorem (1973) says: for two faithful normal states, σ_t^φ(a) = u_t σ_t^ω(a) u_t* with u_t = (Dφ:Dω)_t the **noncommutative Radon–Nikodym derivative** — the change of measure is the change of clock — and consequently the composite ℝ → Aut(𝔐) → Out(𝔐) is independent of the state: *the algebra alone fixes the flow up to inner automorphisms; the state fixes the rest.* On a commutative algebra every state is tracial and the flow is trivial: a classical measure generates no time. Time-from-measure is a strictly noncommutative phenomenon, which is the point Connes and Rovelli made in their title. This is the Lighthouse's "measure precedes duration" in its sharpest available form: **law (algebra) → duration up to gauge; measure (state) → which clock.** It is a translation of the heuristic into a theorem, not a derivation of physics from the heuristic.

**11.3 Clocks as modular flows; the cocycle as the transformation between clocks.** A clock is a subsystem with a state, (𝔑, ω_𝔑), with its own modular flow; equivalence classes of such pairs correspond to conjugacy classes of modular flows (theorem, via Tomita–Takesaki). The clock ambiguity of §9.3 becomes "which modular flow is the time?" The cocycles between all pairs of states form a groupoid; the reading that the cocycle is a gauge field, the absolute flow gauge-dependent, and the relative modular structure the invariant content, is an **interpretation** of a theorem, and is kept as such. Two statements in v2.0 are corrected here. First, "the Connes–Takesaki theorem makes the unitary group act transitively on faithful normal states" is not correct as an exact statement: the result is **Connes–Størmer homogeneity** (1978) — a factor is type III₁ if and only if for any two faithful normal states and any ε > 0 there is a unitary u with ‖φ∘Ad u − ψ‖ < ε, i.e. transitivity holds in norm closure, approximately. (The Connes–Takesaki flow of weights is a different object; for III₁ it is trivial.) "Exactly one physical state up to gauge" is therefore not even available as an interpretation of an exact theorem. Second, and more important, the gauge reading is contradicted at the level that matters by §11.4: in the gravitational algebras, changing the clock changes the algebra.

**11.4 The crossed product: the clock inside the algebra (2022–2025).** The v2.0 plan asked how a clock could be inserted into a timeless algebraic description with the gravitational constraint respected. That has been done, semiclassically, and the results change the honest status of three of the plan's questions.

- **Witten 2022.** For a large-N holographic theory in the thermofield-double (black-hole) state, the boundary single-trace algebra is type III₁ (Leutheusser–Liu 2021–23, who also show that half-sided modular inclusions of that algebra generate an *emergent time* in the black-hole interior). Adjoining the generator of time translations — i.e. passing to the **crossed product 𝔐 ⋊_σ ℝ of the algebra by its own modular flow** — yields a type II_∞ algebra with a trace, hence a renormalized entropy. Chandrasekaran–Penington–Witten 2023: that entropy is the generalized entropy.
- **Chandrasekaran–Longo–Penington–Witten 2023.** In the static patch of de Sitter space, with an observer consisting of nothing but a clock whose Hamiltonian is bounded below, the algebra of observables gravitationally dressed to the observer's worldline is a **type II₁** factor. It has a trace defined on all of its elements, hence entropies for all states; it has a **maximum-entropy state** — empty de Sitter (the Bunch–Davies state) tensored with a simple state of the observer — whose density matrix is the identity; and the entropy of any semiclassical state agrees, up to a state-independent constant, with the generalized entropy A/4G + S_out. Without the observer, the constraint leaves no nontrivial observables at all.
- **Jensen–Sorce–Speranza 2023**: the construction for general subregions with an observer. **Witten 2023**: a background-independent algebra along an observer's worldline. **Chen–Penington 2024** ("A clock is just a way to tell the time"): gravitational algebras in cosmological spacetimes, with the argument that, within their assumptions, the algebra obtained does not depend on which clock is used. **Kudler-Flam–Leutheusser–Satishchandran 2024**: algebraic observational cosmology — observers in expanding universes.
- **De Vuyst–Eccles–Höhn–Kirklin 2024–25.** The observers in these constructions are quantum reference frames in the Trinity's sense; a proper accounting of gauge invariance and observer degrees of freedom converts the type III algebra to a type II crossed product; and **different QRFs give different algebras and different entropies** — gravitational entropy is observer-dependent, with explicit examples (a gravitational interferometer; degenerate clock superselection; monotonic versus periodic clocks). **De Vuyst–Höhn–Tsobanjan 2025** relate the perspective-neutral, algebraic, and effective QRF formalisms. **Fewster–Janssen–Loveridge–Rejzner–Waldron 2024**: QRF dressing changes the type of local algebras in QFT measurement schemes. **Faulkner–Speranza 2024**: the generalized second law in gravitational algebras.

**Consequences for the plan** (all entered in §15, §19, §20).

1. *The type question, honestly* (replacing v2.0 §7.4): the local algebras of quantum field theory on a background are hyperfinite type III₁; once the gravitational constraint and a clock are included, the algebra is type II (II_∞ with a boundary time; II₁ for a bounded-below observer Hamiltonian in de Sitter), semiclassically, to leading order in G. The "resonance" v2.0 found between non-duality and type III₁ was with an algebra that lacked its clock.
2. *The clock is inserted with the constraint.* These constructions are Page–Wootters in von Neumann-algebraic form, with gravity — the first setting in which §9's open item 4 has a worked instance.
3. *The clock ambiguity is not pure gauge* at this level: the algebra and its entropies depend on the clock (DEHK), while in some cosmological settings they do not (Chen–Penington). When the gravitational algebra is clock-independent is **P15**, and the entropy difference between clock choices is the physical distortion measure that WP2 lacked.
4. *A selection principle supplied by the algebra.* In type II₁ the trace is a state and it is the maximum-entropy state. That is a distinguished state the algebra itself provides. It is the *wrong end* of the arrow (§11.6), but it is the first state-selection fact in the programme that is a theorem.

**11.5 The standard form, and space.** The standard form (𝔐, ℋ, J, 𝒫) — algebra, Hilbert space, modular conjugation, self-dual natural cone — is unique up to unitary equivalence; faithful normal states correspond bijectively to vectors in the cone's interior (Araki–Connes); the algebra is recoverable from the cone. The emergent-space material is carried at the status v2.0's retraction fixed: a Connes-type distance on the state space as a candidate spatial metric, the cocycle as a connection, the reduction to a Bures or Fisher metric — interpretation or speculation; the modular-inclusion and crossed-product programme (Wiesbrock's half-sided modular inclusions; Liu 2025; Leutheusser–Liu's emergent times), entanglement-based geometry (van Raamsdonk 2010; Ryu–Takayanagi 2006), and Jacobson's 1995 derivation of the Einstein equation as an equation of state given the area law and the Unruh temperature — active research with partial results, not a completed derivation.

**11.6 State selection, repaired.** v2.0 §7.6 proposed three layered selection principles and named the second "the Past Hypothesis layer: the state of minimal modular energy — the modular ground state, with modular Hamiltonian positive semidefinite — whose positivity would give the flow a preferred direction." v2.0 §6.2 had already retracted "the Past-Hypothesis synthesis" as invented before §7.6 reinstated it. It must stay retracted, for a reason that is a theorem:

> **Proposition 11.1 (no modular ground state on a type III algebra).** For a faithful normal state ω on a von Neumann algebra, JΔJ = Δ⁻¹, so the spectrum of Δ is invariant under λ ↦ 1/λ. Hence K = −log Δ ≥ 0 ⇔ Δ ≤ 1 ⇔ Δ ≥ 1 ⇔ Δ = 1 ⇔ σ_t = id ⇔ ω is a trace. Type III algebras admit no faithful normal trace; tracial *states* exist only in types I_n and II₁. In type III₁ the spectrum of K is all of ℝ for every faithful normal state (Connes spectrum ℝ₊). Therefore no state on the algebras of §11.1–11.3 has a positive semidefinite modular Hamiltonian, and "minimal modular energy" has no minimizer.

So the Past-Hypothesis layer, T2 ("area law for the modular ground state"), and gaps G8 and G11 are void as written. The repair uses §11.4. In the clock-dressed **type II₁** algebra a trace exists and *is* a state; but it is the **maximum-entropy** state — in de Sitter, empty de Sitter — i.e. the equilibrium end, not the low-entropy beginning. The arrow therefore cannot come from modular positivity. It can only come from a state of **low entropy relative to the trace**, which is a *contingent* selection. Under the non-dual assumption that was an embarrassment; under the Lighthouse it is what one expects of a created order, and the demand on it is D-8's: the selection must be lawful — derivable, not brute (§17). The generalized second law in gravitational algebras (Faulkner–Speranza 2024) then supplies the arrow *in form* — entropy relative to the trace does not decrease — while the low-entropy state supplies it *in fact*.

## §12 The measure-first layer (new in v2.1)

The Lighthouse's central physical heuristic is *measure precedes duration* (v1.0 §3; [H]). v2.0 had no place for it: under non-duality a preferred measure looked like a preferred subsystem and was filtered out. Under the real distinction it is admissible, and the audit found that it is not merely admissible but already true in three exact senses. This section states them with registers, states the conjecture that joins them, and turns the fork they open into a prediction.

**12.1 Three exact instances.**

1. **A Lorentzian metric is causal order plus a volume measure (M3) — theorem.** For past- and future-distinguishing spacetimes the causal order determines the topology, the differentiable structure and the conformal class of the metric (Hawking–King–McCarthy 1976; Malament 1977). What the order does not fix is one function: the conformal factor, equivalently the volume element. In causal set theory this becomes the slogan *Order + Number = Geometry* (Bombelli–Lee–Meyer–Sorkin 1987): the order is primitive and the count of elements supplies the volume. And duration is literally recovered by counting: the proper time between two related events is estimated by the length of the longest chain between them (Myrheim 1978; Brightwell–Gregory 1991). *Duration comes from order plus a measure of the M3 kind.*
2. **A clock requires a scale (M3) — theorem, as a statement about conformal geometry; established, as a statement about clocks.** Null geodesics and causal order are conformally invariant; proper time along a timelike curve is not. A world with only conformal structure has order and no duration — which is exactly the premise of conformal cyclic cosmology's crossover and the reason massless fields cannot serve as clocks. The first clock is mass: a particle of mass *m* is a clock of frequency *mc²/ℏ* (de Broglie; made into a working clock by Lan et al. 2013). In the canonical theory the same fact appears as a conjugate pair: York's extrinsic time, proportional to the mean curvature *K* — the local fractional rate of change of the volume element along the normal — is canonically conjugate to the volume density √h (York 1972). In unimodular gravity the cosmological constant is conjugate to a time that *is* four-volume (Henneaux–Teitelboim 1989; Unruh 1989). *Duration is conjugate to volume.* This is the conjugate-pair test of §4 passed, exactly, by the pair measure ↔ duration.
3. **A state (M2) determines a flow — theorem (§11.2).** A faithful normal state on a von Neumann algebra determines its modular flow; the change of state is the Connes cocycle; on a commutative algebra the flow is trivial. *A noncommutative probability measure determines a time.* That this time is physical time is the thermal time hypothesis — conjecture.

**12.2 The conjecture that joins them [H → conjecture].** The three instances use two different senses of measure, M3 in the first two and M2 in the third. The plan's organizing bet is that in the fundamental description they are one structure. This is not free speculation; there are four places where M2 and M3 already fuse in exact results, and the fourth is new:

- *Bekenstein–Hawking and Bousso:* entropy — an M2 quantity, the logarithm of a count of states — is bounded by, and for horizons equals, an area in Planck units — an M3 quantity (D-11's interface bound).
- *Ryu–Takayanagi 2006:* in holographic theories the entanglement entropy of a boundary region (M2) equals a minimal bulk area (M3).
- *Jacobson 1995, 2016:* the Einstein equation follows from δQ = T δS with S ∝ A on local causal horizons; or from the requirement that the vacuum maximize entanglement entropy at fixed volume. A relation between an M2 and an M3 quantity *is* the field equation.
- *The crossed product (§11.4):* on the clock-dressed type II algebra the generalized entropy A/4G + S_out is the entropy of a state with respect to the algebra's trace. The trace is an M2 object — the unique (up to scale) tracial weight — and it carries the area term. *The M3 measure appears as the M2 measure of the gravitational algebra.* That is the strongest current instance of the conjecture, and it was obtained by adjoining a clock, which is the heuristic's own prediction of where it should appear.

Stated: **Conjecture M.** *In the fundamental description the volume measure of spacetime (M3) and the state (M2) of the gravitational algebra are the same structure — the trace of the clock-dressed algebra — and physical duration is its modular flow.* Register: conjecture. Status of the parts: the trace exists and carries area (theorem, semiclassical); that its flow is physical time (thermal time hypothesis, conjecture); that nothing else is needed (open; G19). If Conjecture M holds, the Lighthouse's open questions P2 (how time emerges) and P3 (which measure) are one question, and the clock ambiguity of §9 *is* the state ambiguity of §11 *is* the measure ambiguity. If it fails, the heuristic survives in its two exact M3 forms and the plan loses its unifying thread, not its results.

**12.3 The time fork — D-14.** The two exact M3 instances are *preferred-time* forms: York time is a foliation; unimodular time is a global four-volume. The M2 instance is a *relational* form: the modular flow is a flow of the state, not a foliation. The measure-first heuristic therefore does not choose between the two branches of the oldest fork in the subject:

- **Branch A — covariant/relational.** No preferred foliation; time is recovered from correlations with physical clocks (Page–Wootters; the Trinity; the crossed product; thermal time); preferred clocks are approximate, contextual, and semiclassical.
- **Branch B — preferred time.** A physical foliation or global time variable exists at the fundamental level: constant-mean-curvature slicing and York time; shape dynamics (Barbour; Gomes–Gryb–Koslowski 2011), which trades refoliation invariance for spatial conformal invariance; unimodular time; Hořava–Lifshitz gravity; the foliation of causal dynamical triangulations; the "time of birth" of causal-set sequential growth.

v2.0 excluded Branch B on metaphysical grounds. v2.1 may not (§8), and item 19 of §6 says what a Branch-B theory owes: a *derivation* of its preferred time. The fork is open (P2, restated), and the heuristic makes a prediction across it:

> **D-14 (time fork; prediction).** *Whichever branch the final theory occupies, its physical time variable is either conjugate to a volume measure (Branch B: York, unimodular — M3) or the flow of a state (Branch A: modular — M2). Falsifier:* a fundamental theory in which the time variable is a bare parameter conjugate to nothing and generated by no state — an external Newtonian time re-admitted at the foundation — would falsify D-14 and with it the measure-first heuristic. *Discriminators between the branches, none decisive:* Lorentz-violation bounds (constrain Branch B's low-energy footprint; Hořava is already tightly constrained); black-hole interiors, where constant-mean-curvature slicings can fail (a problem for Branch B); the clock-dependence of gravitational algebras (P15 — if the algebra is clock-independent in general, Branch A loses a reason to prefer any clock and Branch B gains none).

## §16 Measurement, actualization, and the Born rule

The problem of time and the measurement problem meet at one point: quantum mechanics needs a *now* at which the state is defined and probabilities normalized (§7.2), and whatever delivers definite outcomes delivers them *in* time. This section states the Lighthouse's commitments on measurement in their strongest logical form, prices them, and records what the price buys for the time problem.

**16.1 The commitments, in their strongest form.** The Lighthouse holds (v1.0 §4): **D-7**, no collapse — there is no physical process that replaces the unitary dynamics; **D-10**, the Born rule is recovered from the measure structure, not postulated; and the definite outcome is an **I2**-intrinsic fact, absolute rather than perspectival (§8). Logically there are four ways to have outcomes:

1. *Many outcomes* (Everett). Excluded by the I2 commitment: there is one actual outcome. (The Lighthouse's reason is first-principles — facts are known to God singly — and it is entered as a premise, not a result.)
2. *Dynamical collapse* (GRW, CSL, Diósi–Penrose). D-7 bets against the whole family. Status of the bet: live and tightening. The parameter-free Diósi–Penrose model is excluded by the Gran Sasso search for collapse-induced radiation (Donadi et al. 2021); the Majorana Demonstrator (Arnquist et al. 2022) further constrains CSL and Diósi–Penrose parameter space; interferometry with large molecules and levitated-nanoparticle proposals press from the other side. *Falsifier of D-7:* a positive detection of a collapse signature — anomalous heating, spontaneous radiation, or interferometric visibility loss beyond all decoherence accounting. This is the testable content the audit asked D-7 to have.
3. *Relative facts* (relational quantum mechanics; QBism). Definiteness relative to a system or agent. **Overridden** (§8): facts are absolute.
4. *Single outcome without collapse*: the state plus something determines the actual value. This is the Lighthouse's position, and it is, by definition, a **hidden-variable theory** in Bell's sense — whether the "something" is a configuration (Bohm), a stochastic process (Nelson), or a measure over actual values (the Lighthouse's own M2 phrasing).

**16.2 The price of position 4 — theorems.**

| Theorem | What it forces on position 4 |
|---|---|
| Bell 1964 (established experimentally; loophole-free 2015) | Nonlocal dependence of outcomes on distant settings |
| Kochen–Specker 1967; Spekkens 2005 | Contextuality: the actual value depends on which compatible observables are co-measured |
| Pusey–Barrett–Rudolph 2012 | Under preparation independence the quantum state is ontic — not merely knowledge of the hidden variables |
| Frauchiger–Renner 2018; Bong et al. 2020 ("local friendliness") | *Absoluteness of observed events* + locality + no-superdeterminism is inconsistent with quantum predictions (and the inequality is violated experimentally in proof-of-principle form). With absoluteness held (§8), **actualization is nonlocal**, not merely correlation |
| No-signalling (theorem of the formalism) | None of the above is usable for signalling; the nonlocality is at the level of facts, not of messages |

**G20** is the ledger entry: the Lighthouse's measurement position is a nonlocal, contextual, ψ-ontic hidden-variable account with absolute outcomes. The Lighthouse can pay, because it already holds locality to be emergent (v1.0 §3) and it has no particle ontology (§13.2) — but it must be seen to pay, and no sentence of this document may describe the position as "just quantum mechanics without collapse."

**The I2 trade-off (admitted to the inventory, §4).** The pair *intrinsic (I2) ↔ relational* passes the trade-off test in this form: the more an outcome is treated as context-independent (an I2 fact fixed before the context), the more nonlocality and contextuality the account must carry, and the amount is quantified — by the Bell and Kochen–Specker inequality violations, and more finely by the contextual fraction of Abramsky–Brandenburger 2011, which bounds the share of any empirical model that admits a context-independent explanation. That is an inequality, which is what band 1 of the map requires.

**16.3 D-10: Born from measure.** *Theorem:* on a Hilbert space of dimension ≥ 3, any countably additive probability assignment on projections is given by a density operator (Gleason 1957); for effects (POVMs) the result holds in all dimensions ≥ 2 (Busch 2003; Caves–Fuchs–Manne–Renes 2004). So the Born rule is the *only* noncontextual probability measure on the quantum event structure. What D-10 adds, and what remains conjectural: that the measure in Gleason's theorem is the state of the world in sense M2; that it is the *same* measure whose flow is time (Conjecture M, §12.2); and — for position 4 — that the actual values are distributed according to it. The last has one worked instance: in Bohmian mechanics the Born distribution is derived as *typicality* with respect to the natural measure on configurations (Dürr–Goldstein–Zanghì 1992) — "Born from measure" in exactly the sense D-10 means, with the equilibrium hypothesis as its contingency. Alternative derivations (Deutsch–Wallace decision theory; Zurek's envariance) are recorded with their contested assumptions and are not relied on. A4/G12 — the conditioning-to-measure map in the *relational* setting, where the "now" is a clock reading — is the open atom.

**16.4 What the plan may and may not say about actualization.** It may say: outcomes are I2 facts; the measure is the Born measure; the mechanism is unknown; the account is nonlocal. It may not say: that God actualizes outcomes (§6, item 1 — the ground upholds everything and is a mechanism of nothing, rule 7 of §2); that consciousness actualizes outcomes (item 8); that "field collapse" is unproblematic (§13.2); that relational definiteness is enough (§8). The v2.0 glue "relational actualization = the collapse reading" (G14) is kept only as its *physics* component: the conditioning-to-record map through decoherence (WP7).

**In plain terms.** The Lighthouse says: one outcome really happens, nothing in nature "collapses" to make it happen, and the odds are the ones the quantum state already carries. Put that way, it is a hidden-variable view, and the theorems of the last sixty years say what such a view costs: the fact of an outcome here depends on choices made far away, depends on what else was measured alongside, and the quantum state is a real thing, not an opinion. The Lighthouse can afford this — it never believed locality was fundamental — but it must say the price out loud. The one thing it gets for free is the probability rule: mathematics shows there is only one consistent way to assign odds to quantum events, and the bet is that this one measure is also the measure whose flow is time.

## §17 The arrow of time

**17.1 Two problems, not one.** The source documents' "arrow specification" ran together two questions that must be separated (audit of clue #2):

- **(a) The low-entropy condition.** Why was the entropy of the early universe so low (Penrose's Weyl-curvature hypothesis; Albert's Past Hypothesis)? Given time-symmetric laws, this condition, not the laws, produces the thermodynamic arrow, and with it the arrows of records, memory, causation and radiation (strong consensus; the derivation of each secondary arrow from the thermodynamic one is partial).
- **(b) The orientation.** Why is there a single, global direction at all — rather than two arrows pointing away from a middle (Gold's two-boundary universe; Barbour–Koslowski–Mercati's Janus point in the N-body problem, 2014; Carroll–Chen's baby universes nucleating in both directions from a high-entropy middle, 2004)? Time-symmetric laws plus one boundary condition yield an orientation only relative to that boundary; nothing in (a) makes the orientation unique.

Under the Lighthouse, (a) is a case of **D-8**: the selection is contingent (which is expected of a created order) and must be *lawful* — derived from a principle, not posited as a brute fact. (b) is split by register. In the first-principles register the created order has one direction; that is a revelation claim and may not be entered as physics (§6, items 2, 10, 14). In the physics register (b) is **open**: the Janus-point models show that a single orientation is not forced by the laws, and no result of this plan favours one orientation over two. The plan predicts nothing about (b).

**17.2 The modular correction.** v2.0 proposed that the arrow be the positivity of a modular Hamiltonian in a "modular ground state." Proposition 11.1 shows there is no such state on any type III algebra, and that in the clock-dressed type II₁ algebra the one distinguished state is the trace — *maximum* entropy, the arrow's far end. The arrow therefore enters the modular programme in two pieces: the **generalized second law in gravitational algebras** (Faulkner–Speranza 2024), which is the arrow *in form* — entropy relative to the trace does not decrease along the flow — and the **low-entropy condition relative to the trace**, which is the arrow *in fact* and is contingent. The second is atom A9. The plan's bet, which is the Lighthouse's D-8 applied here: A9 is lawful — there is a principle (a measure-first principle, if Conjecture M holds: the initial state is distinguished by the same M2/M3 structure that fixes the flow) from which the condition follows. *Falsifier:* a demonstration that the low-entropy condition is a free parameter of the final theory with no derivation — the brute-fact outcome.

**17.3 The cosmological measure problem.** In eternal-inflation cosmology the probabilities of observations depend on a measure over an infinite ensemble of pocket universes, and no measure is agreed (the Boltzmann-brain and youngness paradoxes are symptoms). This is an M2 problem, and it is the arrow problem's cosmological form. **D-8** makes a sharp prediction here: *there is no fundamental landscape*, hence no measure over pocket universes to choose; the measure problem is predicted to disappear with the landscape, not to be solved within it. If the landscape is real and the measure problem genuine, D-8 fails and with it a central Lighthouse bet (§18). The plan does not hedge this.

**17.4 Decoherence and records** (unchanged). Decoherence is time-symmetric; it produces records only in the presence of (a). "Becoming" — whether conditional correlations constitute a real flow — remains excluded from the falsifiable core (§9, open item 5), with causal-set sequential growth as the one programme that offers a mechanism for it.

**In plain terms.** Why does time have a direction? Two questions hide in that one: why the universe started tidy, and why there is one direction rather than two pointing away from a middle. On the first, the Lighthouse bets that the tidy start was law, not luck, and now knows where to look: the one distinguished state the new algebra offers is the maximally *untidy* one, so the tidy one must be singled out by something else — ideally by the same measure that gives the flow. On the second, physics has no answer and this plan offers none; what revelation says about the direction of the world stays in its own register.
