\section*{S1. Finite-$\chi$ paired-history calculations}
\label{sec:supp-chi192}

These results characterize finite-$\chi$ warm-history behavior. They do not measure a thermodynamic coexistence interval, spinodal, physical transition bracket, or transition order.

\paragraph{Calculations at $p=.304825$.} Two calculations used the same Hermitian half-row objective and the specified convergence and gauge criteria. The ordered preparation continued from the accepted $\chi=192$, $p=.30480$ state; the small-order preparation continued from the accepted $p=.30485$ state. Both final states satisfied the acceptance criteria. The final checkpoint files are unavailable, so tensor-level checks of those states cannot be performed.

The color separation is $0.4238870216$. The same-objective difference $\Delta f_{\rm half}$ (ordered minus small-order) is $+3.5909916507\times10^{-7}$. At $\chi=128$ and the same rate, the recorded ordered-minus-disordered difference is $-4.359393\times10^{-7}$. These finite-$\chi$ comparisons are not objective error bars, calibrated crossings, or controlled bond-dimension extrapolations. Optimizer residuals are not free-energy error bars, and accepted MPS states are not global-minimum certificates.

\paragraph{Neighboring sampled rates.} At $p=.30480$, the low-order-start state was not retained as distinct. At $p=.304825$, the ordered and small-order states were distinct. At $p=.30485$, an ordered-start continuation ended in the small-order state. These outcomes define a coarse sampled finite-$\chi$ warm-history change zone near $.30480$--$.30485$; they do not determine branch-stability boundaries.

\paragraph{Incomplete continuation at $p=.30481$.} The lower-side ordered continuation did not meet the acceptance criteria. Its saved state was at cumulative iteration 19, with $\epsilon=1.308536380704902\times10^{-6}$, above the external $10^{-7}$ tolerance and internal $5\times10^{-8}$ target. The recorded optimizer time was $3222.734782934189$ s. The paired small-order history was not completed. The run ended before a final status was recorded; the cause is unknown. The partial checkpoint is at \texttt{research/transition-order/chi192-p30481/} (SHA-256 \texttt{a8097162c27ca104556edd5c789bcf36e5c9c89ef5af7eb92ad3811248319cee}). It is not an accepted state and does not refine the sampled zone.

A controlled $\chi=256$ paired-history study remains future work.
