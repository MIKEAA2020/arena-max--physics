\begin{abstract}
We develop exact replica mechanics and Born-record large-deviation tools for monitored quantum circuits, using the annealed record-moment free energy as an organizing object without conflating it with normalized trajectory entanglement. Fixed-basis measurements leak from the one-wire permutation span; independently averaged two-site gates restore exact post-gate closure through an explicit Weingarten channel. The nonzero period spectrum is positive and semisimple and compresses to bond labels. At two replicas we recover the anisotropic triangular-Ising model of Bao, Choi and Altman, its critical point $p_c^{(2)}=0.233810$, and a closed free-fermion spectrum. For three replicas, uniform matrix-product-state calculations through bond dimension $\chi=96$ locate a single transition near $p_c^{(3)}=0.3049(2)$, with sign and standard order lost together. Weak first order is favored, but a continuous transition is not excluded. We withdraw the earlier three- and four-state-Potts assignments at $n=3,4$: small-system crossing ratios did not establish either class. The relevant structural comparison is instead to $n!$ permutation labels, with the regular $S_n$-bimodule splitting into distinct symmetry sectors; this count does not determine transition order at finite local dimension. Four- and five-replica spectra retain their value as finite-size diagnostics. Independently, conditional-trajectory and conditional-purity decision are PostBQP-complete under the stated promises. We establish record-LDP and multifractal identities, retain a no-freezing result under explicit analytic-cocycle hypotheses, derive an exact annealed--quenched interpolation identity, and report quenched Haar record-SCGF data. The Clifford dyadic identity closes the three-replica annealed moment exactly and enables sign-free tilted-stabilizer SMC. Its small-system benchmarks pass, while large-system genealogy collapse prevents a coexistence verdict from the present pilot.
