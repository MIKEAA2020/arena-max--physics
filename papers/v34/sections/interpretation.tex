\section{Interpretive status of the record formalism}
\label{sec:interpretation}

The record formalism describes an ordered stream of outcomes within a specified physical instrument. Its probabilities, entropy rates, large deviations and conditional states are mathematical objects of that model. They do not derive consciousness, an all-encompassing observer, the ultimate nature of time, or an identity between the Creator and creation. The description of a record is not a claim that the world is merely an unreal appearance.

\paragraph{Governing interpretive framework.}
The interpretation adopted for this project is governed by the Qur'anic/Lighthouse framework, not by an ``only the Absolute exists'' or non-dual working premise. On that reading, God is uncreated; creation is real, created, sustained and dependent, and is really distinct from its Creator. Dependence is not identity, and distinction does not make creation a second independent absolute. This is an ontological distinction, not a spatial distance or a relation between two physical objects.\footnote{The separate framework argues this reading from text, context and tafsir before formalization; the user's choice of an override is not itself interpretive authority. Relevant textual constraints include Qur'an 42:11 (nothing like Him), 39:62 (God as Creator), and 112:1--4, together with the asymmetric-dependence argument in the Lighthouse framework, Part I, Sec.~4.1. These are not conclusions inferred from the circuit calculations.}

Consequently, a quantum state, a Hilbert space, an observable algebra, a channel, a record stream, or a modular flow is not identified with God or with a divine substance. The physical systems modeled here belong to the created order. God is not inserted as a clock, Hamiltonian, field, measurement mechanism, or gap-filling physical variable. Neither the replica calculation nor a change in its transition-order assessment establishes or refutes the grounding relation asserted by this separate interpretive framework.

\paragraph{Operational and mathematical scope.}
Symmetric unconditioned summaries need not determine Born-weighted conditional entanglement, while record-resolved information can. This distinction does not derive the Born rule, which is a premise of the process. Nor does self-averaging force agents with different histories to identical probabilities without common model and inference assumptions. A stochastic process that presupposes an event order does not by itself solve the fundamental problem of time; its temporal indexing concerns the created system, not the Creator.

Interpretations reproducing the same operational instrument reproduce the same record law. Modified-collapse dynamics, a different instrument, or a different probability rule need not do so. There is no theorem here identifying Bohmian, Everettian, QBist and collapse descriptions in general. These limits are unchanged by the governing creator--creation distinction. The mathematical claims must stand on their own proofs and hypotheses, empirical claims on their data and error controls, and this interpretive reading on its independently argued textual and philosophical warrant. No circuit theorem is advertised as a proof of theology.
