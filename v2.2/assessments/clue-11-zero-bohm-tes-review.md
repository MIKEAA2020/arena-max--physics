# Clue 11 — four web articles: anything worth borrowing?

**Asked (15 October 2026):** "any clues here, anything worth borrowing from, either as is or after modifications?" — four links: (1) Kamlapure, *Zero: The Number That Created Everything* (Medium, 1 Oct 2025); (2) Ciupa, *Zero's Journey from Nothing to Everything: Bridging Mathematics, Philosophy, and Brain-Centric Physics* (LinkedIn, c. 2024–25); (3) Ciupa, *Bohm's "Wholeness and the Implicate Order" and the Brain-Centric Many Blockworlds Interpretation of Reality* (LinkedIn); (4) Eneanya, *Spatial Time Dimension: Using the Timeline Event Series (TES)* (LinkedIn, 2025). Full texts: `/home/user/search/clue11/*.txt` (LinkedIn pages fetched with a browser user-agent; the fetch tool returned HTTP 500).

**Method.** Each article was read in full; every claim that touches the plan was sorted into (a) correct and already in v2.2, (b) correct and missing, (c) wrong, (d) imagery. Grades use the Lighthouse scale: [C] correct, [H] hypothesis, [P] programme/imagery, [F] false or unsupported. Rule 11 applies (imagery orients; nothing enters Part I from imagery).

**Short answer.** Nothing in the four pieces can be borrowed as is. One of them — the Bohm article — points, through Bohm rather than through its own thesis, at a genuine gap in the plan's landscape: the de Broglie–Bohm (pilot-wave) treatment of the problem of time, in which the time parameter is read off an actual trajectory of the 3-geometry. That is added, with its price. Everything else is either already in v2.2 in a sharper form, or is excluded by commitments the plan has already made and argued, or is wrong. Two of the pieces should not be cited for independent reasons (fabricated references; self-citation of a management framework as physics).

## 1. Kamlapure — *Zero: The Number That Created Everything* (Medium)

**What it says.** A personal essay: zero as placeholder (positional notation), as origin ((0,0)), as the limit Δx → 0, as the binary 0. No claim about physics or time.

**Assessment.** All four mathematical remarks are standard and correct [C]; the title's thesis ("created everything") is a figure of speech. The one image with any bearing on the plan — an *absence that carries information by position* — is a pleasant illustration of Q12's privation (a lack with structure, which "is not a being and needs no ground") but adds nothing to it, and the plan's rule 11 forbids promoting an image to a premise. **Borrow: nothing.**

## 2. Ciupa — *Zero's Journey from Nothing to Everything* (LinkedIn)

**What it says.** A paper-shaped essay: history of zero (Seife, Kaplan — fine); zero as additive identity and "boundary"; Gödel's incompleteness "mirrored" by zero; the halting problem "triggered by zero-related conditions"; zero-point energy aligned with the brain's default-mode network as a "baseline consciousness"; an Annex: *"Nothing is 'pregnant' with the potential of Everything. It has natural law within it, and that's Something — the act of observation brings into being its kinetic change … the question 'why is there something rather than nothing?' needs a watcher in time for it to make sense."*

**Claims checked.**
- Gödel/zero, halting/zero, zero-point field/consciousness: category errors [F]. Incompleteness is a statement about provability in formal systems; it has no relation to zero as a number or a limit. The halting problem is not about division by zero. Zero-point energy is a property of a quantum field's ground state; the default-mode network is a pattern of cortical metabolism; "baseline" is the only word they share.
- **The bibliography contains fabricated entries.** Ref. 10, "Shannon, C. E. (1963). *Zero and Infinity: The Foundations of Physics*. Academic Press" does not exist; the real book is Peter Rowlands, *Zero to Infinity: The Foundations of Physics* (World Scientific, 2007), a different author, year and publisher. Ref. 5, "Leslie, J. (2010). *Zero: A New Approach to Non-Aristotelian Logic*. Routledge" — no such book is known. Ref. 17, "Antin, D. (1999). *The Nothing That Is: Essays on Art, Literature, and Mysticism*" duplicates Kaplan's title under another author. This is the signature of a machine-generated reference list. **The article must not be cited for anything.**
- *"A nothing with natural law within it is Something."* Correct [C] — it is David Albert's objection to Krauss's *A Universe from Nothing*: a quantum vacuum with laws is not nothing. In the plan this is already settled at the root: Q9 (everything other than God is created and depends on Him) makes a law-laden void a *creature*, not a ground, and §4.2's provenance paragraph rejects any ground that is itself a structure in a meta-time. The Annex's own next step — the pregnant nothing *is* the ground, actualized by observation — is the opposite of Q9 and is not borrowable.
- *"The act of observation brings into being its kinetic change."* Consciousness-causes-collapse. v1.1 struck the CCC remark from the Lighthouse (§23.4, item 8); D-7 bets against collapse; the measurement reading holds outcomes absolute (§8, C10, G20). Excluded [F] relative to the plan, and argued, not assumed.
- *"The question needs a watcher in time to make sense."* Conflates the asking of a question with the truth-maker of its answer; it is the verificationist slide §4.2 and §9.4 (observer-level time = meta-time) already close. As a statement about *creatures* it is the clue-9 point ("the problem of time concerns creatures, not God", item 80) said less carefully.

**Borrow: nothing.** Useful only as a foil for §4.2.

## 3. Ciupa — Bohm and the "Brain-Centric Many Blockworlds Interpretation" (LinkedIn)

**What it says.** BC-MBWI = consciousness-causes-collapse + Everett, with the "worlds" as branches of a "many blockworlds" ensemble, a universal wavefunction "in Hilbert spacetime" (Wheeler–DeWitt "work in progress"), and the mind doing the Bohmian "piloting" that brings an explicate order (a coherent personal path) out of the implicate order; validation via Friston-style neurophysics; the information paradox "reframed" as subjective. A long annex paraphrases Bohm on the holomovement.

**Claims checked.**
- The hybrid is incoherent on its central point: CCC says collapse happens (once, physically); Everett says it never does. A "brain selects a branch" view is a many-minds interpretation (Albert–Loewer 1988) without the measure that many-minds at least tried to supply. No rule is given for when or how the brain "pilots". [F]
- "The mind's Bohmian piloting" replaces Bohm's guidance equation — a deterministic, mind-independent law — with a brain process. What makes Bohmian mechanics a theory is precisely that equation; remove it and nothing is left to test. The article's "experimental validation" is therefore empty: there is no equation whose predictions a neurophysical experiment could check. [F]
- Relative to the plan: outcomes relative to brains is position 3 of §16.1 (relative facts), **overridden** — facts are absolute, known to God singly, perspectival only for creatures. CCC is excluded by D-7. Nothing survives. [F]
- Bohm's *implicate/explicate* pair, as imagery: it maps loosely onto the ledger's distinction between a timeless structure and its ordered unfolding given a split, a state, a clock (§9.4). Imagery only; rule 11.

**What it points at, and what is borrowed after modification.** Bohm's *own* theory — not the brain-centric version — has a definite answer to the problem of time that the landscape of §7.3 did not list: in de Broglie–Bohm quantum cosmology the Wheeler–DeWitt wavefunctional guides an *actual* 3-geometry (and matter configuration), and the Schrödinger *t* and the ordering of events are read off that actual history. No probability interpretation of the wavefunction of the universe and no collapse are required (Vink 1992; Shtanov 1996; Goldstein–Teufel 2001; Pinto-Neto–Fabris 2013, the standard review). It is a working method in minisuperspace (singularity avoidance, perturbation spectra). Its price is precise and is the problem of time returning in another form: in full superspace the guidance dynamics is *not* refoliation-invariant in general — the quantum potential distinguishes a foliation (Shtanov 1996; Pinto-Neto–Fabris 2013) — so either the foliation is extra absolute structure or it must be extracted covariantly from the wavefunction (Dürr–Goldstein–Norsen–Struyve–Zanghì 2014). This belongs in the plan for a structural reason: §16.1 already commits to position 4 (single outcome without collapse = a hidden-variable theory in Bell's sense) and §16.3 leans on the Bohmian typicality instance of D-10. The plan should therefore say what position 4 does to time. **Added:** a row in §7.3; a row in the §9.4 ledger ("trajectory time": order and duration output of the wavefunction *plus an actual configuration*; the arrow still needs quantum equilibrium plus a low-entropy state — Prop. 11.1's pattern again); a cross-reference at §16.1 position 4; references in §22.2 (identifiers verified).

## 4. Eneanya — *Spatial Time Dimension: Using the Timeline Event Series* (LinkedIn)

**What it says.** "Nothing happens in time without an anchor"; the arrow of time is "passive until anchored" by a sustained action, decision or cosmic event; a "dimensional ladder" (1D = a life from birth to death; 2D = shadows, holography; 3D = space; 4D = spacetime); case studies (relativity, JWST, DNA). The author's other articles are a product-marketing framework and "The Fate Causation Effect"; the piece cites itself ("According to Austin Eneanya").

**Claims checked.**
- Physics details (Eddington 1927; Hafele–Keating 1971; GPS corrections; JWST look-back) are correct as popular science [C] and carry no weight.
- "The arrow is the track; anchors are the driver" — a category error [F]. The thermodynamic arrow is not a passive background that agents orient; it is a consequence of a contingent low-entropy *state* (§17.1(a); Prop. 11.1), and the agential/psychological arrow (deliberation, record-making, "anchoring") is downstream of it, not upstream. TES has the dependency backwards: it promotes the agential arrow to master arrow, which is the one order the plan's results exclude.
- The "dimensional states" ladder is numerology [F]; a human life is not a one-dimensional object, and the "2D = holography" remark is a pun.
- What is dimly right: "every event has a catalyst" is a weak causal principle, present in the plan at Part I (Q-kernel; layered concurrence) in a form that distinguishes grounding from triggering, which TES does not.

**Borrow: nothing.** Foil for §17.1: the one real "anchor" is the Past Hypothesis, and it is a state, not a decision.
