# Gap audit of v2.2 — 15 October 2026 (tenth amendment)

**Question asked:** after twelve clues and nine amendments, is anything left to incorporate, and are there gaps in the plan still to be addressed?

**Method.** (i) Every open register of v2.2 was dumped and read against the current text (D-1–D-14, P1–P15, G1–G20, WP0–WP7, N-1–N-7, dead ends 1–18). (ii) The Qur'anic side was checked against the local corpus (`v2.2/verify/quran/`, diacritic-stripped regex) for a class of verses the plan had never cited — the text's own temporal vocabulary for creatures — and the classical tafsir positions on the disputed verses were checked on the web. (iii) The physics side was searched on the arXiv API (sixteen queries, 2025–26, sorted by date) and crossref for every open item that had a 2025–26 literature: gravitational algebras and the observer (P15, G18), York/unimodular time (D-14), locality of modular flow (G10/T1/WP4), Lorentzian signature (G15), typicality (G12, G19), the clock ambiguity (§9.3), the experimental interface (§7.5, WP7), causal-set dynamics (§12.3), the arrow (§17.1), temporal quantum reference frames (G2, WP6). Twenty-eight abstracts were read; journal references were verified on crossref where they exist. Every identifier below resolved on the arXiv API on 15 October 2026 (`search/gap1.xml`, `gap2.xml`, `gap3.xml`).

**Headline.** Two gaps of substance, one on each side of the Lighthouse — the physics-side fill in three parts (the closed-universe observer results; the intrinsic-clock constructions; the York-time crossed product, where D-14's branches meet) — and a dozen status updates. *(Headline corrected 15 October 2026, eleventh amendment, §23.2 item 106: the first wording counted gaps, not fills, and under-described the physics side, whose three parts enter three different sections and are not of one kind — the third changes a register entry (D-14) and a caveat (T2′), the first two add content to §9.3 and §11.4.)*

1. *Qur'anic side.* The plan quotes 54:49 fifteen times and 25:2, 87:2–3 once each — all *qadar* verses — and never once the verses in which the text speaks of creature-time itself: the counted, calendrical duration defined by created cycles (10:5; 17:12; 6:96; 55:5; 36:40; 9:36); the reckoner-relative "day" (22:47; 32:5; 70:4); the appointed term (*ajal musammā*, 21 verses, including the sun and moon "each running for an appointed term" and the heavens and earth created "in truth and for an appointed term"); the underestimated stay (2:259; 18:19, 25–26; 23:112–114; 10:45; 46:35; 30:55; 79:46; 20:103–104; 17:52); and the rejection of time as an agent (45:24). These are exactly the verses a document on the problem of time would be expected to have read, and exactly the verses most abused by "scientific miracle" readings — so the plan also lacked an explicit guard against reading time dilation out of 22:47/70:4. **Filled** (§5 "Time, in the text"; §8 row and closing paragraph; §6 item 3 applied).
2. *Physics side.* The plan's thesis that time is the **output of a split** (clock/rest; observer/fields) now has a non-perturbative cousin it did not know about: the 2025–26 closed-universe results — without an observer the gravitational path integral gives a *one-dimensional* Hilbert space; with an observer of S_Ob degrees of freedom, a Hilbert space of dimension ≈ e^{S_Ob} (Harlow–Usatyuk–Zhao, JHEP 02 (2026) 108) or scaling as e^{1/G_N} (Abdalla–Antonini–Iliesiu–Levine, JHEP 05 (2025) 059), the two prescriptions disagreeing (Akers–Bueller–DeWolfe–Higginbotham–Reinking, JHEP 05 (2025) 201). And the plan's §11.4 statement that the observer/clock "is the condition of existence of the algebra" has acquired constructions in which the clock is **not added by hand**: it is built from the fields (Speranza, CQG 42 (2025) 215023), from the gravitational field's own dressing time (Freidel–Kirklin, JHEP 2026, 130), or — most consequential for this plan — from **York time** inside a crossed product (Blommaert–Chen, arXiv:2602.22153, preprint). The last is the first exact toy model in which an M3 clock (conjugate to a volume) is inserted by the M2 mechanism (adjoining a flow to an algebra), i.e. the first place where the two branches of D-14 meet in one construction — and what it yields is a trace whose entropy is *not* an area, which bears on T2′. **Filled** (§9.3 item 2; §11.4; §12.3/D-14; P15, G17, G18 status notes).

Everything else found is a status update to an already-registered item, recorded below with its verdict; nothing found reopens a dead end or changes a Part I or Part II grade.

The one thing it changes in the plan is a guard: the plan now says explicitly that 22:47/32:5/70:4 may not be read as time dilation, and that the relativity those verses assert is the relativity of *reckoning* — the creatures' count does not measure God's acting — which is the Lighthouse's "prior in being, not in time" stated by the text in its own idiom.
