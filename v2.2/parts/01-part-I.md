# Part I — The governing framework (Lighthouse v1.1)

Part I restates Lighthouse v1.0 in condensed form, with the audit's repairs applied. Every [C] and [F] of v1.0 is retained in substance; where the wording changed, §23 says why. Readers who hold v1.0 may read only §4 (the engine, repaired), §5 (lexical discipline, new), and the additions to §6.

## §1 Reality, plainly stated

There is one reality. At its root is God — not a component of the world, not a force or a field among others, but the ground on which everything else depends; nothing is like Him (42:11). Everything other than the ground is created: real, active, never self-sufficient, dependent on the ground at every moment and not only at its first (*al-Qayyūm*, 2:255; *al-Ṣamad*, 112:2). The created order is lawful and measured (54:49; 55:7); layered, with real levels; polar, in the disciplined sense of §4; informational at the level of its structure [H]; personal at the root; and it has an inside — it is not exhausted by its measurable face (57:3). Its structure is knowable in part by observation and never exhaustively; some things are marked, inside the revelation, as beyond knowing (17:85).

**Its characteristics** (grades as in v1.0):

1. **One at the root, and not composite there.** The ground has no parts (112). "One" does not mean "the only thing that exists": the creation is real. It means one ground, one dependence, one order.
2. **Ground and creation are not two causes at one level.** God creates all things (39:62); the creature's act is genuinely its own and genuinely grounded. The best-supported reading is **layered concurrence with a real distinction**. Neither pure occasionalism nor pure autonomy survives the text at the level of first principles. When a model seems to need two sufficient causes at one level: look deeper, not double.
3. **Dependence is continuous.** The world is upheld (2:255), not set going and left.
4. **Lawful and measured.** Everything by measure (54:49) and in balance (55:7); the divine pattern does not change (33:62); the world is not play (21:16). Structural corollary, held at heuristic level: no brute facts (§18, D-8, D-9).
5. **Layered, with real levels and graded being.** Ground → manifestation → structure → world → life → mind → norm. Levels are real; each is grounded in the one above without competing with it; none is final.
6. **Polar.** Each admissible axis of §4 is one reality under two poles that constitute rather than compete; on every axis the ground is prior in being, the manifestation prior in appearing.
7. **Informational at the level of structure** [H]. Finite information density, redundancy and error correction, level-relative rendering, an interface between knower and known. Anchored in the Qur'anic vocabulary — *qadar*, *ʿilm*, *ḥifẓ*, *lawḥ maḥfūẓ*, *kitāb*, *kun* — which supplies terms, not derivations.
8. **Intelligible — a book, not a program** [H]. *Kun* (36:82) is speech, not execution; the cosmos is *bi-l-ḥaqq* (6:73). That is why mathematics reaches it.
9. **Personal at the root; value is not an illusion.** The ground is not inert: living, knowing, willing, speaking (*al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm*, 2:255; *kun*, 36:82; 6:59) — the active attributes that §4.1 states as axioms (Q6, the two wills; Q8, knowledge). Norms are real and not reducible to facts. How norm is grounded is open (P6); that it is grounded is a constraint. *Restored from the Master Synthesis §VII (§23.2, item 54):* **the moral order is real at the ultimate level** — judgment and recompense are not theatrical (21:47; 99:7–8; 3:182), and God wrongs no one (4:40; 10:44; Q5). The document takes no position on the moral order beyond what the text says, and reads no physics off it.
10. **It has an inside.** What physics describes is the relational, structural aspect; the intrinsic aspect in sense **I1** (§5) is what it is like to be that structure — received, graded, grounded in divine knowledge. The *rūḥ* is marked beyond us (17:85).
11. **Purposive, not play** (21:16) — **and with an end.** *Maʿād*: the return (39:7), the renewal of creation (21:104), the resurrection of the body (22:7; 36:78–79) are affirmed at band 0 [C] and carry no physics. What its end will be belongs to the revelation's register; physics may neither read a calendar off it nor deny it a place. The Master Synthesis (§IX) inferred from *maʿād* that the arrow of time "is structurally oriented, not merely statistical"; v1.0 withdrew the inference and §17.1(b) states why: a direction affirmed in the first-principles register may not be entered as a physical premise (§6, items 2, 10, 14). The affirmation is restored here; the inference is not (§23.2, item 54).

**Two relations, two warrants [C]** *(restored from the Master Synthesis §II and GPP §3; §23.2, item 53).* The dependence of the world on God contains two relations that must not be collapsed. **Grounding** — ground → created order, the informational order at its foundation — is metaphysical: argued, not measured. **Emergence** — informational order → geometry, time, causation — is physical: modelled, tested, revised. Physics addresses the second and cannot address the first. Confusing them produces both over-claim (physics "confirming" or "refuting" the ground) and under-claim (declaring emergence ill-posed because the whole depends on God). The rule is already at work in §2 (jurisdictions), in §3 rule 7 and in §8 (the override applied); it is named here because the v2.0 plan's "dissolution track" was exactly this confusion (§6, item 16).

**What can be said, and with what warrant.** *The Qur'an fixes:* one ground; nothing like Him; creation real, dependent, upheld; the world lawful, measured, purposive; norm real; the boundaries of knowledge declared. *Reason requires:* no regress of grounds; ground and grounded not two causes at one level; the ground not a member of the world it grounds; no totality contains itself; contradiction is not a feature of reality. *Evidence decides:* everything structural — what the levels are like, which structures exist, every value, every mechanism, every prediction in §18. Evidence never decides the first two lists; it corrects interpretations of them. *Not like anything:* the ground is given by what He is not, what He does, and how He is named — never by depiction. **Stated once so that no later section has to whisper it: the ground is not pictured in this document, and any sentence that pictures it has left the jurisdiction of §2.** This rule is what retires the v2.0 plan's "the standard form is the Absolute," "the modular fixed point is self-existence," and "the mind has nothing but itself to know itself with" (§23).

**Rules for using the three together** (all [C]): (1) label every claim — criterion, band, grade; (2) jurisdiction over preference; (3) silence is a boundary, not a licence; (4) a claim must be able to fail; (5) the symmetry of undecidability — what shields a claim from disproof shields it from proof; (6) authority is not totality — a synthesis can be faithful to the Qur'an without being derived from it; (7) **grounding and emergence are two different relations**: emergence (higher structure produced by lower structure) is what physics studies; grounding (why there is anything, and on what everything depends) is not a process inside physics. Do not present an emergence result as a verdict on grounding, nor grounding as a mechanism inside physics.

Rule 7 is the rule that does the most work in Part III. It is why the v2.0 plan's "dissolution track" is gone: under a real distinction, a one-way derivation of time from a timeless structure is *not* ill-posed — it is the ordinary business of emergence — while the dependence of the whole created order on its ground is not a derivation at all and is never in physics' hands.

## §3 The map

Seven bands, one direction of grounding (down), one direction of access (up). The v1.0 picture is kept, with one correction: v1.0 placed both "formal structure — the grammar of the axes" and "informational structure" in band 2 without saying they were the same. They are not. Band 2 is now the **formal grammar** (the calculus in which axes and levels are representable: adjunctions, dualities, conjugate pairs — §4); the **informational layer** is the content of band 3's foundations, i.e. what the fundamental physical description is made of (states, algebras, measures, codes), and it is written there.

**How to read it** (all [C]). Grounding runs downward and is non-competitive: a lower band's real causal power is not a second sufficient cause beside the higher's. (The relation at band 0, divine and creaturely agency, is argued from the text and stated as axioms in §4.1.) Access runs upward and is partial: evidence reaches structure, not ground; the ground enters a programme only as constraint and boundary. Open questions stay visible: a bracketed P-entry is never filled by a later sentence; candidate answers enter §19 with a status line.

**The point–circle picture, stated plainly and kept small.** The point is the ground: prior in being, without parts, without scale. The circle at radius *r* is the created order: one reality appearing at a degree; the radius is the first measure the world has (54:49; sense **M3**). The point is prior in being; the circle is prior in manifestation. What the picture may claim: orientation and coherence. What it may not claim: proof, uniqueness, an equation — or any identification of the point with a mathematical object.

## §4 The engine, repaired

**The rule [C], unchanged.** Every apparent contradiction is a polarity misread as a competition. The resolution is not to pick a pole; it is to find the axis.

**The repair.** v1.0's inventory held thirteen axes and admitted that an axis which classifies or predicts nothing is a metaphor. It proposed "which conserved quantity does each polarity have?" as the test. That is the wrong test: conservation laws come from continuous symmetries (Noether), not from dual pairs. The audit replaces it with two admission tests, either of which qualifies an axis for band 1; an inventory entry that passes neither is retired to the glossary.

- **The trade-off test.** A constituting polarity that does work is a *conjugate pair*: two descriptions each of which requires the other and which cannot both be sharp — time/frequency, position/momentum, number/phase, **volume/York time** (§12), the discrete/continuous pair under Pontryagin duality (the dual of the integers is the circle). Such an axis carries an inequality (uncertainty, duality bound, rate–distortion curve), and the inequality is its research content.
- **The adjunction test.** A constituting polarity may instead be an *adjoint pair of constructions* — a free/forgetful, discrete/continuous, or syntax/semantics relation in which neither side is reducible to the other and each is determined by the other up to canonical isomorphism. This is the formal home of "unity and identity of opposites": Lawvere proposed it in 1969, developed it through the 1990s, and in *axiomatic cohesion* (2007) made discrete ↔ continuous a precise string of four adjoints (components ⊣ discrete ⊣ points ⊣ codiscrete); his *Aufhebung* is a formal notion of levels in which each level resolves the one below it — which is this map's band structure. Schreiber's differential cohesion extends the apparatus to gauge fields. **This is P7's best current candidate** and it is now written there.

**The inventory after the tests [H].** Admitted on the trade-off test: measure ↔ duration (as volume/York time, and as state/flow — §11–§12); discrete ↔ continuous; definite ↔ superposed (as the complementarity trade-off); intrinsic ↔ relational in sense **I2** (as the contextuality trade-off, §16). Admitted on the adjunction test: being ↔ manifestation (as grounding/emergence — but only as *formal grammar*; the ground itself is not a term of any adjunction, §1); timeless ↔ temporal (as the stationary/conditional pair of §9 and the auxiliary/physical pair of §10); background-free ↔ background-full (as the slice adjunction of §10, whose right adjoint is Newtonian time). Retired to the glossary pending a test: spaceless ↔ spatial; simple ↔ composite; unity ↔ multiplicity; stability ↔ novelty; manifest ↔ hidden; necessary ↔ contingent; silent ↔ speaking. Retirement is not denial: a retired entry may re-enter band 1 the day it carries an inequality or an adjunction.

**The asymmetry [C].** On every admitted axis: ground prior in being, manifestation prior in appearing. In a conjugate pair this asymmetry is *not* a priority of one variable over the other — the pair is symmetric — and so the slogan "measure *precedes* duration" is read as in §12: not that measure is prior to duration within physics, but that duration requires a scale and a state, while order does not.

**The internal check [C], unchanged.** Polarity (formal), gradation (ontological), and two-aspect structure (epistemic) must be one idea viewed three ways. If they become three assumptions, the framework costs more than it earns.

**The dissolutions, re-graded [C].** A re-description earns nothing; a dissolution counts only where it rules something out. v1.0 credited one of its four dissolutions (measurement) with forbidding something. The audit withdraws even that credit as stated: what the measurement reading forbids and can be scored on (collapse) is forbidden equally by Everett, Bohm, relational quantum mechanics and QBism; what is distinctive to it (no branching) is not empirically scorable. D-7 stands as a bet (§18); it is not earned by the dissolution. All four "dissolutions" are now **readings**; the problem of time in particular is **open** (P2) and nothing in this document dissolves it.

**§4.1 The relation, stated as axioms — the Q-kernel** *(amendment of 14 October 2026; first-principles register throughout; rule 4 of §24 applies — nothing here is matched to a physical result).*

**Why this subsection exists.** L2 of §3 says the best-supported reading of the Qur'an on how the ground relates to the world is *layered concurrence with a real distinction*. Until now that was a statement; here it is argued from the text and written as a kernel of twelve axioms, so that the Qur'anic side of the problem this document works on can be formalized, checked for consistency, and contested verse by verse. A standing instruction of the user governs the order of operations: the earlier override (the Lighthouse's real creator–creation distinction prevails over the non-dual working assumption of the time document, §23.4) is *not* a premise of the reading. The reading is established on textual grounds first; the override stands only because, and only insofar as, the text supports it. Under that discipline the question asked of the text was: which account of divine and human agency lets the sovereignty verses and the agency verses both be read literally — in the sense fixed by the hermeneutic rules below — and which accounts force one set into figure?

**Hermeneutic rules (the Qur'an's own, as far as they can be read off the text).**

- **H0 Self-consistency is a textual requirement, not a logician's preference** (4:82 "had it been from other than God they would have found in it much discrepancy"; 39:23). A reading on which two verse-sets contradict is thereby excluded by the text; the burden lies on the reading, not on the verses.
- **H1 "Literal" means the apparent sense in context** (ẓāhir): what a verse asserts, to its addressee, in its sūra and genre. Isolating a clause and generalizing it into a metaphysical axiom is the method of the kalām proof-text, and it is the generator of the apparent incoherence, not its discovery. The Qur'an itself forbids partial reading (2:85 "do you believe in part of the Book and disbelieve in part?").
- **H2 Firm and ambiguous** (3:7, muḥkamāt/mutashābihāt): the operative, addressed, repeated content — commands, capacity, earning, warning, the conditions under which God withholds guidance — is firm; the compressed sovereignty formulas are read in its light, not the reverse. Where a verse admits two grammatical readings (37:96; 7:179; 11:119) both are carried and the kernel is required to be consistent with each.

**What the text does, counted.** Checked on the full Arabic text (Uthmani-simple, Tanzil edition via the alquran.cloud API; translations Sahih International, Arberry, Pickthall; `v2.2/verify/quran/q.py`):

- With God as subject, "God does not guide …" (lā yahdī) occurs in **24 verses**; in 23 the object is a morally characterized class — the wrongdoers (10), the defiantly disobedient (5), the disbelievers (4), "the plot of the betrayers" (12:52), "those who do not believe in God's signs" (16:104), "whoever is a liar and ingrate" (39:3), "whoever is a transgressor and liar" (40:28); the 24th, 16:37 "God does not guide those He leads astray", takes its object from 16:36, "those upon whom error became due" after a messenger had been sent. Not one has an unqualified object.
- The sealing of hearts (khatama/ṭabaʿa) is asserted in **13 verses** (two further verses are hypothetical, 6:46 and 42:24; 36:65 seals mouths); in each of the 13 a ground is stated in the verse or the verse before, and it is the person's prior conduct — "because of their disbelief" (4:155), "they believed, then disbelieved, so their hearts were sealed" (63:3), "for their sins" (7:100), "thus God seals the hearts of the disbelievers / the transgressors / every arrogant tyrant / those who do not know" (7:101; 10:74; 40:35; 30:59), "he who took his desire as his god" (45:23), "they followed their desires" (47:16), those content to stay behind (9:87; 9:93), those who disbelieved after believing (16:106–108). 2:7 is preceded by 2:6 ("those who disbelieve …") and 83:14 says the rust is "what they were earning".
- "Had God willed" (law shāʾa) occurs in **33 verses**. Four of them place the appeal to the divine will *as an excuse* in the mouths of polytheists and reject it in the same breath (6:148–149; 16:35; 43:20; 36:47). The Qur'an therefore itself rules out the inference "God willed it, so I am not answerable" — while affirming in the same passage that "had He willed, He would have guided you all" (6:149). This is the text's own verdict against *jabr*.
- The claim of self-sufficiency is rebuked in the same way (Qārūn, 28:78 "I was given it for knowledge I have"; 96:6–7 "man transgresses because he sees himself self-sufficient"; 18:39 "no power except by God"; 10:100 "no soul believes except by God's permission"). This is the text's own verdict against *tafwīḍ*.
- "Creator of all things" occurs in 6 verses (6:101–102; 13:16; 25:2; 39:62; 40:62), and 54:49 "everything We created by measure". Yet the root kh-l-q is also used of a creature's making: Jesus "creates" the form of a bird "by God's permission" (3:49; 5:110), the idolaters "create a lie" (29:17), God is "the best of creators" (23:14; 37:125). The word is not univocal across the two levels; the competition premise "if God created x, no creature made x" is not the Qur'an's usage.
- "They wronged themselves" and its variants occur in 18 verses; "God does not wrong / did not wrong them" in 17 (4:40; 10:44; 18:49; 11:101; 41:46; 3:182 …), and "they shall not be wronged" in 19 more. The locus of wrong is fixed at the agent level every time.
- The Qur'an states the two-level structure in its own voice: 4:78–79 ("say: all is from God" — "whatever evil befalls you is from yourself", consecutive verses); 3:165–166 (Uḥud: "it is from yourselves" — "it was by God's permission"); 42:30 with 57:22 (the calamity "is for what your hands have earned" — "is in a Book before We bring it into being"); 18:79–82 (the same class of deeds attributed as "I intended", "we intended", "your Lord intended", closing "I did it not of my own accord"). And it marks which register of attribution is the righteous one: Iblīs says "because You have put me in error" (7:16; 15:39), Adam says "we have wronged ourselves" (7:23); so Moses (28:16), Jonah (21:87), Bilqīs (27:44).

Three verses are harder than any on the list above, and they are carried, not hidden. **7:179** "We have created for Hell many of jinn and men" — read either with the *lām* of consequence (the tradition's reading, from al-Zamakhsharī to Mawdūdī; required by 51:56, which states the purpose of creation as worship) or as purpose; in both the verse's own characterization of those people is that they had hearts, eyes and ears and did not use them. **11:118–119** "and for that He created them" — "that" is mercy or differing; both views are in the tradition (al-Ṭabarī reports both). **32:13** "had We willed We would have given every soul its guidance, but the word from Me will come true: I will fill Hell …" — the "word" is 38:85, "with you and those of them who follow you", a conditional sentence. None of the three asserts that a creature is not a cause; all three assert the grounding level and the prior decree, which the kernel affirms (Q1, Q8).

**The kernel.** Grades as in §2: [C] commitment, [H] best-supported reading, [P] open.

- **Q1 Continuous grounding [C].** Every created item, at every instant — acts, powers and willings included — is grounded in God (39:62; 6:102; 13:16; 40:62; 35:41 "He holds the heavens and the earth lest they cease"; 55:29 "every day He is upon some task"; 50:15 "a new creation", in context resurrection, read by the Sufi and Sadrian traditions as renewal — carried as a secondary anchor).
- **Q2 Real secondary causation [C].** Creatures are real causes, and regularities are God's unchanging way, sunnat Allāh (13:11; 14:32 "and brought forth *thereby* fruits"; 3:49; 2:102 "they harm no one thereby except by God's permission"; 29:17; 33:62; 35:43; 48:23).
- **Q3 Level separation [C].** Grounding (God's) and causing (the creature's) are relations of different type; there is no common scale on which an act is divided into divine and creaturely shares, and the agent-level vocabulary — doing, willing, being able, being guided or led astray, being questioned — is creaturely (8:17; 4:78–79; 21:23 "He is not questioned about what He does, but they will be questioned").
- **Q4 Double complete attribution [C].** Every creaturely act is attributed wholly to God at the grounding level and wholly to its agent at the agent level; both attributions are literal (4:78–79; 18:79–82; 3:165–166; 42:30 with 57:22).
- **Q5 Responsibility at the agent level [C].** A creature is answerable for an act iff it did it, could do it, and willed it (2:286; 65:7; 53:39; 17:15; 74:38; 99:7–8); God is answerable for nothing (21:23) and wrongs no one (4:40; 10:44).
- **Q6 Two wills [H].** God's creative will (irāda kawniyya; idhn) is a necessary condition of every event (81:29; 76:30; 10:100); His legislative will (irāda sharʿiyya; riḍā) is deontic; an event can fall under the first and against the second (39:7 "He does not approve disbelief for His servants", while disbelief occurs, 18:29; 16:93; 5:48). The distinction is Māturīdī and Taymiyyan in formulation; its textual basis is 39:7 read with 81:29.
- **Q7 Responsive withholding [H].** God's leading astray presupposes the person's prior transgression and a prior clear warning; turning and striving are met with guidance (2:26; 61:5; 14:27; 40:34–35; 45:23; 63:3; 9:115 "God would not let a people stray after guiding them until He makes clear what they should avoid"; 13:27 "guides to Himself whoever turns"; 29:69; 47:17; and the 24 lā-yahdī verses). Graded [H] because it is a reading; it is the reading with the strongest corpus support in this document.
- **Q8 Decree records the act as the agent's [H].** What is written beforehand (57:22; 17:13; 9:51; 64:11) is written *as* the creature's earning (42:30); the decree is not static (13:39 "God effaces what He wills and confirms"). The later kalām formula "knowledge discloses, it does not compel" (al-ʿilm kāshif lā muʾaththir) and Ibn ʿArabī's stronger "knowledge follows the known" are interpretive devices, not text; adopted as a rule, not proved.
- **Q9 Asymmetric dependence [C].** God is not created and nothing is like Him; everything other than God is created and depends on Him; nothing grounds God (112; 42:11; 57:3; 2:117; 36:82; 28:88 read as dependence, not identity).
- **Q10 No excuse from decree — anti-jabr [C].** The creative will under which an act occurred never cancels the agent's answerability (6:148–149; 16:35; 43:20; 16:93 "He leads astray whom He wills and guides whom He wills — and you will surely be questioned about what you used to do").
- **Q11 No self-sufficiency — anti-tafwīḍ [C].** No creaturely act, power or will is independent of grounding and permission (18:39; 28:78; 96:6–7; 10:100; 81:29).
- **Q12 Evil is privation [H].** What God grounds in an evil act is its being — the power, the will, the motion; the evil is the act's lack of its due measure, which is not a being and needs no ground, and which belongs to the agent who withheld the measure (4:78–79; 91:8–10; 39:7; 24:40 "he for whom God has appointed no light has no light"; 6:1 and 113:2 carried as the verses the reading must accommodate). Carried from the Master Synthesis and GPP §20 ("light has units — photons; darkness does not"), where it was graded moderate; v2.1 omitted it from Part I, an omission corrected here (§23.2 item 50).

Q10 and Q11 together are the content of the formula transmitted in the Imami tradition from Jaʿfar al-Ṣādiq — "neither compulsion nor delegation, but a matter between the two" (lā jabr wa-lā tafwīḍ wa-lākin amr bayn amrayn, al-Kāfī) — but they are derived here from the Qur'an's two rebukes (of the polytheists' excuse and of Qārūn's self-sufficiency), not from the hadith; the hadith is a confirmation, not a premise.

**Evil at the grounding level — closed as attribution, open as permission.** The privation reading is the classical Islamic answer, not a modern import: Ibn Sīnā (Shifāʾ, Ilāhiyyāt IX.6: evil has no essence; it is the non-existence of a substance or of a due perfection, entering the decree accidentally), Ṣadrā (al-sharr ʿadamī), Ibn ʿArabī (evil is non-existence), al-Ghazālī in part, and Ibn Taymiyya, who locates evil's sources in the human agent and in non-existence and reads the hadith of the opening supplication "all good is in Your hands, and evil is not attributed to You" (Muslim) with 113:2 "the evil of what He created" — the evil is in the created receptacle, not in the creating. The Qur'an's own imagery carries the asymmetry exactly: *nūr* occurs 40 times and never in the plural; *ẓulumāt* occurs 23 times and never in the singular (checked on the corpus; `counts.py`). Light is one and positive; darknesses are many, each a way of lacking the one. 24:40 states misguidance itself as non-bestowal — "he for whom God has appointed no light has no light" — which is why Q7 (withholding, khidhlān) and Q12 are one structure: what God does in leading astray is not to give. And the word for wrong, *ẓulm*, is lexically "placing a thing other than in its place" (Lisān al-ʿArab): a privation of due order, which presupposes a due order — a measure (54:49; 87:2–3 "created and proportioned, measured and guided"; 20:50). So the privation reading needs what the Lighthouse already holds, that measure is the first thing the world has, and it needs nothing else. What it closes: the attribution question. Under Q4 an evil act is attributed wholly to God *as being* and wholly to the agent *as defective*; nothing positive is left over at the grounding level to be a divine evil. What it does not close: the *permission* question — why the defective act is sustained in being, and the suffering of the innocent — to which the Qur'an gives answers that are partial by its own account: trial (67:2; 2:155–157; 21:35 "We test you with evil and with good"; 18:7), the greater good hidden from the sufferer (2:216; the three deeds of 18:65–82, each evil in appearance and good at a higher level of knowledge), pardon (42:30 "and He pardons much"), and the limit of knowledge (17:85; 21:23). Two verses are carried as the ones the reading must accommodate rather than as support: 6:1, where God "made (jaʿala) the darknesses and the light" — the verb changes from *khalaqa* for the heavens and the earth to *jaʿala* for the pair, which the exegetes noticed and the privation reading takes as the appointing of an order that includes absence; and 113:2. Suffering, on this reading, is the experience of privation (Master Synthesis); whether pain is itself a privation or a good signal of one is left to the tradition's debate and does not affect the kernel.

**The schools against the kernel.** The question is not which school is most coherent as a system — each is coherent on its own terms — but which satisfies H0–H2 and Q1–Q11 without making one verse-set figurative.

**Verdict [H].** The reading that satisfies H0–H2 and Q1–Q11 is layered concurrence with a real distinction: one act, two complete attributions at two levels that do not compete, with one-way dependence. It is what the Qur'an performs at 4:78–79 and 18:79–82. Its classical witnesses are given per axiom in the table below — the Imami amr bayn al-amrayn as given metaphysical form by Ṣadrā (without his late ontology), the Māturīdī khalq/fiʿl distinction, Ibn Taymiyya's two wills and real causes, Ibn Sīnā's privation — and no school is adopted. Identity ("both are ultimately one") is not needed to resolve the tension — asymmetry and non-competition do all the work — and it is excluded by the text on two grounds that owe nothing to the Lighthouse: 37:96 says "God created *you*", 112:3 and 57:3 say God is not created; and under identity the addressee of every command, threat and "when you threw" disappears. Rule 12 of §6 stands: this is not "the one true harmonization"; it is the reading that is jointly satisfiable with both verse-sets, and the two alternatives that are not.

**On adopting a school.** The question was raised whether the Māturīdī position should replace GPP's core for better textual alignment, or serve as its canonical instantiation. Neither. The core is the kernel — H0–H2 and Q1–Q12 — because the kernel is what was argued from the text and what was checked; a slogan ("layered concurrence with a real distinction") is its summary and a school is at most a witness to it. No school instantiates all twelve axioms cleanly, as the table shows; the kernel is cross-school by construction, its sharpest classical formula is Imami (amr bayn al-amrayn), its evil-axiom is Avicennan, its two-wills axiom is Taymiyyan and Māturīdī, its withholding-axiom is Māturīdī and Muʿtazilite. Naming one school canonical would import that school's other commitments — for the Māturīdīs, the created power that exists only with the act, the kalām atomism of the later school on Q2 in nature, the Ottoman doctrine of the uncreated "particular will" — none of which the text requires, and would position a cross-school reading sectarianly for no textual gain. Schools enter this document as *witnesses* (showing an axiom has been held by a major tradition, so the reading is not idiosyncratic) and as *suppliers of machinery* (khalq/fiʿl, khidhlān/tawfīq, creation/command, privation, proximate agency), cited per axiom. Rule 12 of §6 applies to schools as it applies to harmonizations.

**Māturīdī and Imāmī readings — the mechanism question** *(added after a second external comparison was put to the document; §23.2, item 60).* A comparison was offered on which the privation reading (Q12) "pushes away from Māturīdī *kasb* toward concurrence", so that the Imāmī reading would be "more faithful on the mechanism", the Qur'an being "clear on structure, silent on mechanism". Three things are to be said. **(1) The target is misidentified.** The critique that acquisition "adds only a label" is al-Rāzī's own verdict on *Ashʿarī* kasb ("a word for a meaningless concept"; al-Juwaynī's defection in the *Niẓāmiyya* is the other witness); it does not reach al-Māturīdī, for whom the human has a real prior power valid for opposites and a real choice (*ikhtiyār*), God creating the act's being *according to* that choice, the act being God's by creation and the human's by doing "in reality, not metaphorically". Privation therefore has a locus in the Māturīdī account — the choice — and check 8a confirms that Q12 is satisfiable with it. **(2) The genuine difference is the mechanism,** and it is this: does creaturely causation reach the *being* of the act — the Imāmī ṭūlī (vertical) reading of Mufīd, al-Ḥillī, Ṣadrā and Ṭabāṭabāʾī, on which the human is the proximate agent who produces the act through a power God gives and upholds, and which is structurally the Thomist concurrence — or only its choice and quality, God alone producing its existence (Māturīdī; a fortiori Ashʿarī)? The kernel separates Grounds (being) from Causes (agent level) and says nothing about a third relation, *production of existence*; that is deliberate, because **no verse uses a term that fixes it.** The candidates do not: 37:96 is ambiguous between "what you make" and "your doing", and even on the second reading it asserts creation (Q1), not sole proximate production (check 8d); 8:17 negates the Prophet's throwing at one level while affirming it at another and is about attribution, not mechanism; 56:58–59 ("is it you who create it, or are We the Creator?") concerns a natural process, not a free act; 13:11 and 18:79–82 speak at the agent level only. Formally (checks 8a–8d, 29/29): with a predicate Produces(a, e) added, the Māturīdī instantiation (God alone produces; creatures never do) and the Imāmī instantiation (a creature that causes an act produces it) are *each* satisfiable with the kernel, both verse-sets and Q12, and are *jointly* unsatisfiable with Q2. The encoding thus reproduces exactly the comparison's last sentence — clear on structure, silent on mechanism — and refutes its first: privation does not decide between them. **(3) "More faithful on the mechanism" is therefore a philosophical judgment, not a textual one.** The Imāmī reading arguably has the better metaphysics of causation (it escapes the vacuity objection without making the creature self-sufficient), and this document's Q2 row already leans toward real secondary causation in *nature*; but the text does not award the point, 17:85 and 3:7 forbid awarding it in the text's name, and §6 item 12 applies to the Imāmī reading as to every other. Two further corrections to the comparison's table: Māturīdī theology is not "kasb" but *khalq* (God's) with *fiʿl/ikhtiyār* (the human's) — the shared word hides the difference; and the Imāmī position's distinctive *textual* resources are 13:39 (*badāʾ*, which bears on Q8 and which Māturīdīs reject) and the *irāda takwīniyya/tashrīʿiyya* pair (Q6, which both schools hold), not a different method of reading — Ṭabāṭabāʾī's *al-Mīzān* is Qur'an-by-Qur'an exegesis before it is anything else.

**The point–circle derivation (clue 6a)** *(added 15 October 2026; §23.2, item 63; checks 9a–9e).* A short piece was later offered which derives the Māturīdī position from the GPP picture itself: being radiates from the Point; a radius (the creature) can *determine* but cannot *give being*; therefore God alone creates the act and the creature's share is choice — "entailed by the structure". The derivation fails, for reasons the picture's own §4 status predicts. First, the point–circle image is admitted here as orientation only ([P]) and is forbidden as a derivation engine (§4; §6, what to avoid); a model cannot entail a doctrine. Second, the argument equivocates on "give being": that no creature is the *ultimate* source of being (ījād ab initio) is common ground of every school and is Q1 + Q9; what divides them is whether a creature can be the *proximate* producer of an effect's existence by a power given and upheld — and the picture has no inter-radius causation at all, so it cannot adjudicate ṭūlī from ʿarḍī readings. Third, it proves too much: nothing in the picture singles out free acts, so if it establishes anything about being it establishes P* — no creature produces the existence of *any* effect — which is occasionalism about nature (fire does not produce burning) and is stronger than the Māturīdī thesis about acts. Fourth, the later Māturīdī device itself — the *juzʾī ikhtiyār* as iʿtibārī, "neither existent nor non-existent" (Ṣadr al-Sharīʿa) — is a determination-without-being, which the piece's own second step forbids. Fifth, the text does not exclude creaturely production: 3:49/5:110 (*akhluqu … bi-idhnī*, "I create … by Your leave") and 23:14 (*aḥsan al-khāliqīn*) use the creation verb of a creature, by leave. Formally (`q_kernel.py`, checks 9a–9e, 34/34): P* + NATURE (some creature produces a non-act effect) is unsatisfiable (9a); the Māturīdī thesis restricted to acts is satisfiable with NATURE (9b) — the argument's conclusion does not need its premise's strength; kernel + verses + Q12 are satisfiable with NATURE and the Imāmī instantiation (9c) *and* with P* (9d); and kernel + verses + ¬P* is satisfiable (9e), so the kernel and the text entail neither. What survives as a steelman: if "being flows *through* the creature" replaces "the creature cannot give being", the picture describes layered concurrence — both schools' common kernel — and nothing more; graded [P], recorded as a model, not an entailment.

**What is formally checked** (`v2.2/verify/q_kernel.py`, Z3, 34/34; SMT-LIB export `q_kernel.smt2`). Two sorts (agents, events); grounding-level attribution Grounds(G, e) and agent-level attribution Causes(a, e) as distinct relations; the kernel Q1–Q7, Q9–Q12 as axioms (Q3 as the restriction of the agent-level vocabulary to creatures; Q12 minimally, as a predicate for acts lacking their due measure; Q8 is prose only); eleven sovereignty verses and ten agency verses, each a separately tracked assumption; four rival theses — C, occasionalism's competition premise (a creature is never a cause); T, delegation (a creature's act is not grounded); J, decree-as-excuse (what God creatively wills no one answers for); I, identity (every agent is God). Results: (1) kernel + both verse-sets is satisfiable; the model prints the two-level structure, with each act attributed wholly to God by Grounds and wholly to a creature by Causes. (2) Both verse-sets without any kernel axiom are satisfiable — the verses do not contradict each other; adding C alone makes them unsatisfiable, with deletion-minimal core {C, 37:96, 8:17}; C is also refuted by 13:11 alone. (3) I is unsatisfiable with the literal pair 37:96 + 112:3/57:3; the minimal core from the verse-sets is {I, 112:3/57:3, 3:49}. (4) T is unsatisfiable with 8:17 + 37:96 and with 39:62 + 13:11; J is unsatisfiable with 16:93 alone. (5) Each of C, T, J, I is consistent on its own, so the exclusions are not artefacts. (6) With Q12 added and the verse-sets' evil acts (disbelief, wronging, shirk) marked defective, the whole remains satisfiable; "God is answerable for a defect" is unsatisfiable with the kernel, and "God approves a defective act" with Q12 (checks 7a–7c). What this does not show: that the encoding is the right reading. Every verse is a named assumption; a reader who renders a verse differently changes one line and reruns. (7) The mechanism is not decided by the text as encoded: a Māturīdī instantiation (God alone produces the act's existence) and an Imāmī ṭūlī instantiation (the creature produces it by a given power) are each satisfiable with kernel, verses and Q12, and jointly unsatisfiable with Q2; 37:96 on either reading does not entail the first (checks 8a–8d). (8) The point–circle derivation of the Māturīdī mechanism (clue 6a) is not an entailment: its generalized premise P* is occasionalism about all creaturely production, and the kernel with the verses is satisfiable both with P* and with its negation (checks 9a–9e).

**What stays open [P].** The *how* of concurrence (P1, P14) — no axiom supplies it and the Qur'an marks it as not given (17:85); the *permission* of privation — why a defective act is sustained in being, and the suffering of the innocent — to which the Qur'an's answers (trial, hidden good, pardon, the limit of knowledge) are partial by its own account; foreknowledge beyond Q8; and the two readings each of 7:179 and 11:119, which the kernel is required to be consistent with and does not decide. Evil *as attribution* at the grounding level is closed by Q12 and is no longer listed.

*Note (item 75, fifth amendment).* "Finite information density" and "interface structure" name a bounded observer; clue 8 (§10.6 addendum) supplies a mathematics in which such an observer is a machine with state budget M, with a representational shortfall Δ(M) that vanishes above a finite threshold for finite-state targets, an identification cost of Θ(M log M) mistakes, and a direction-dependence of the retention budget when the law is not time-symmetric. These are theorems about machines; under the rule of this section they are vocabulary for stating N-1 and D-11 precisely, not evidence for either.

**Provenance, stated so that it cannot be forgotten.** The layer was borrowed *structurally* from the simulation hypothesis, whose substance is rejected: no simulator — a simulator would be a regress and a contingent ground, a bigger creature rather than the ground; no staged moral order; representation is not ultimate. The cosmos is *bi-l-ḥaqq* (6:73) — a **book**, not a **program**; *kun* (36:82) is speech, not execution. Because the layer was extracted from physics and computing, "it fits physics" proves nothing (§6, "borrowed fit"). *(Item 80, sixth amendment.)* The rejection of the simulator now has a formal form as well as a theological one. A simulation is a sequential process and runs in the simulator's time — its step sequence is a meta-time (clue 9's own observation about transducers, §9.4). "Replacing the aliens with God" therefore places God in a meta-time, a created-order structure (Part II: prior in being, not in time): either the ground *executes* the world step by step, and then has a time and is a bigger creature — the regress — or the ground *speaks and knows* the whole, and the world's internal time is not the ground's. Only the second is this document's position; the layer's structure never needed the first. *(Added 15 October 2026, clue 11: the same holds for a "nothing pregnant with everything" that "has natural law within it" — a void with laws is a something, and on Q9 a created something; it can no more be the ground than a simulator can.)*

**What the layer commits the programme to [H].** (i) *Measure precedes duration* — if time is emergent, what it emerges from is measure (M3 before duration; §12; Conjecture M). (ii) *No orphan levels* — no level is final; classical spacetime, the Standard Model as framed and quantum theory as framed are incomplete by construction, and the Standard Model's internal structure (gauge group, generations) should be derivable from something deeper (D-8). (iii) *One interface bound* — the Bekenstein, holographic and Landauer bounds and the quantum-measurement limits should be one inequality (D-11). (iv) *No free discreteness scale* (D-9). (v) *Redundancy is not an AdS artefact* (D-12). (vi) *Dynamics and information are one* — *qadar* and *ʿilm* as two aspects of one structure; speculative, held at heuristic level, used nowhere in Part III.

**The four dissolutions, stated and graded.** v1.0 §4.3 stated them; v2.1 re-graded them (§4, "The dissolutions, re-graded") without restating them. Both belong here. *The hard problem:* the relational aspect of the informational structure is what physics describes; the intrinsic aspect (I1) is what it is like to be that structure — **received**, grounded in divine knowledge, graded. This is participationist dual-aspect monism, **not** Russellian: Russell places the intrinsic in matter, immanent and self-standing; here it is a mode of participation. The Master Synthesis added "graded, not combined — there is no combination problem"; that is a claim, not a result: gradation would have to be shown to do the work, and until it is, the combination problem remains a burden on every candidate, this one included (P5 guard). *The measurement problem:* the superposition is the relational description; the definite outcome is the intrinsic fact — no collapse, no branching, no observer-creation. What this forbids and can be scored on is a collapse mechanism (D-7); v2.1 withdrew the rest of the credit (§16; G20). *The problem of time:* the whole at once (*totum simul*) is the view "from the point", sequence the view "from the radius" — a reading only; under the real distinction the point is not the Wheeler–DeWitt state or any structure of this document (§6, item 17), and the demand "derive time" remains well-posed as emergence (§3, rule 7). *The quantum-gravity conflict:* the programmes as complementary views of one informational structure — re-verdicted at §13 as criterion-relative. **The rule [C]:** a re-description earns nothing; a dissolution counts only where it rules something out. Of the four, only the measurement reading does, and only on collapse.

## §5 Lexical discipline [C]

Five terms carried more than one sense across the sources. Each use in this document carries its tag; a claim that mixes tags is a design error.

**Measure.**
- **M1** — *qadar*: determination, proportion, ordination (54:49). First-principles register only. Never a quantity.
- **M2** — a weighting over possibilities: a probability measure; a quantum state as a positive normalized functional on an algebra (a noncommutative probability measure); the Born measure; a cosmological measure on histories.
- **M3** — metric measure: a volume element, the conformal factor of a metric, a scale, a discreteness scale, the "Number" of causal set theory.

The Lighthouse's heuristic "measure precedes duration" is true in two exact senses and conjectural in a third (§12): duration requires M3 (a scale); a flow is determined by M2 (a state); and the conjecture is that the M2 and M3 structures of the fundamental description have a common origin. "*Qadar* is the bridge" is the M1 anchor for that conjecture — vocabulary, not evidence (§6.10).

**Intrinsic.**
- **I1** — what it is like to be a structure (the inside; §1.10; P5).
- **I2** — the actual outcome, as opposed to the superposed description (§16).

v1.0 used one word for both and so assigned creaturely experience to opposite poles in two of its dissolutions. The two senses are now separated; nothing in Part III uses I1.

**Information.** Unless stated otherwise, *information* means quantum information: Hilbert-space structure, entanglement, von Neumann entropy, and — in the algebraic setting — states on von Neumann algebras and relative entropy. Shannon and algorithmic senses are named when used. "Information is physical" (Landauer; Bekenstein) is **established** in the sense that erasure costs work (verified experimentally) and that entropy bounds hold in the regimes tested; "information is more basic than matter" is **[H]**.

**Time.** *Label* — a coordinate; gauge in general relativity; *not* gauge in quantum mechanics, where the Schrödinger parameter is external and reparametrizing it changes the dynamics. *Order* — causal precedence; invariant; determines the metric up to a conformal factor in distinguishing spacetimes (Hawking–King–McCarthy; Malament). *Duration* — proper time; a path functional read by a clock. The tripartition is internal to classical relativity and restates the problem rather than solving it (§7).

**Time, in the text** *(added 15 October 2026, tenth amendment; [C] as text, tafsir-neutral; §22.13; §23.2 item 97).* The document had cited only the *qadar* verses (54:49; 25:2; 87:2–3). The Qur'an also has a vocabulary for creature-time, and it is used here as orientation and as a guard, nothing more. (i) *Creature-duration is counted against created cycles:* the sun and moon are made "that you may know the number of years and the reckoning" (10:5; 17:12), they run "by reckoning" (6:96; 55:5), "each in an orbit" (36:40); the count of months is fixed "on the day He created the heavens and the earth" (9:36). Human time is relational and calendrical — defined by the motions of created bodies and by counting. (ii) *The reckoner-relative day:* "a day with your Lord is as a thousand years **of what you count**" (22:47; the same formula at 32:5 with *miqdār*, "measure"; "fifty thousand years" at 70:4). Two things are in the text: the creatures' count is marked as theirs, and the "day with your Lord" is explicitly not of that count. The context of 22:47 is the demand to hasten the punishment; the point is God's patience and the non-applicability of the creatures' reckoning to His acting. What the thousand and the fifty thousand refer to is disputed among the classical commentators (Ṭabarī on 32:5 lists four readings and prefers the descent-and-ascent of "the affair"; Ibn Kathīr: what creation counts as a thousand years is as a day with Him; Rāzī, as reported in Asad's note to 22:47: a day and a thousand years are alike before Him because He is not in time). This document encodes none of those readings and **excludes** the modern one: these verses are not a statement of relativistic time dilation, no commentator before the twentieth century read them so, and reading a ratio of proper times out of them is verse-to-theory matching (§6, item 3). (iii) *The appointed term* (*ajal musammā*, twenty-one verses): the sun and moon "each runs for an appointed term" (13:2; 35:13; 39:5); the heavens and the earth are created "in truth and for an appointed term" (30:8; 46:3). Created processes, and the created order as a whole, have finite fixed terms — the text's own statement that duration belongs to the created order and is bounded within it; it favours none of §7.3's families. (iv) *The underestimated stay:* "a day or part of a day" (2:259; 18:19; 23:113), "but an hour" (10:45; 46:35), and 30:55, 79:46, 20:103–104, 17:52 — experienced duration is not a measure of elapsed duration, which is the §9.3/§9.4 distinction between constructed and physical duration (the one the Ciupa article conflated, §23.2 item 94). (v) *Time is not an agent:* "nothing destroys us but time (*al-dahr*)" is placed in the mouth of the deniers and dismissed as conjecture (45:24); the ḥadīth qudsī "do not revile *al-dahr*, for God is *al-dahr*" (Bukhārī; Muslim) is read by the commentators as a correction of speech — the disposer of what you attribute to time is God — not as a theory of God's relation to time. On the text's own terms, then, *time is output and never driver*, which is the ledger of §9.4; and the images of time as an engine of change (clue 7) or of an agential master arrow (clue 11, item 90) commit the error of 45:24. **What this entry licenses:** vocabulary (M1) and the guard in (ii). **What it does not license:** any mechanism, any number, any identification of an *ajal* with a cosmological parameter, any claim that the text anticipates a physical theory of time. The Lighthouse's "prior in being, not in time" is (ii) stated in the text's own idiom; it is a claim about the ground's relation to created reckoning, and it enters Part III nowhere.

**Observer.** Never used without one of three senses: a *relational quantity* (a subsystem relative to which a description is given), *subsystem information* (what a subsystem's state encodes), or an *epistemic limit* (what an agent can know). "The observer collapses the state" is not a sentence of this document.

## §6 What to do and what to avoid

**What to do** (condensed from v1.0 §5; all retained): label before you claim; name every hidden premise; fix the criterion before ranking; prefer non-competitive, layered models; look for invariants, measures, and constraints — and now, specifically, for **trade-off inequalities and adjunctions** (§4); treat dualities as clues to axes; grade every verdict (settled / proposed-with-premises / withheld-with-reason); keep the neuroscience default frameworks at their proven status; search for structure before substance; record, don't resolve, where nothing can decide (P1, P8, P14); pre-register contact with data; check every "no other option" against the classical record (worked example P14: Aquinas accepts that the good is self-diffusive and still denies that creation is necessary); cite by status (the "Hidden Treasure" saying is a motif, not an argument); **borrowed fit is not confirmation**; keep this document open to its own revision.

**What to avoid [F]** (v1.0's fifteen, retained in substance, plus four forced by v2.0):

1. God as an explanation for gaps.
2. Mechanism read out of verses.
3. Verse-to-discovery matching and numerology — including, in its respectable-looking form, reading a scientific theory out of verses. *The audit applies this to itself:* a remark made during the review that conformal cyclic cosmology "resonates" with 21:104 is struck from the record as support for anything (§23).
4. Making the ground an object — "God field," "divine energy," "consciousness field as the ground," and now also **"the standard form is the Absolute," "the modular fixed point is self-existence," "type III₁ is the non-dual algebra."**
5. Quantum free will.
6. Oneness from entanglement — and now also **metaphysics from the type of an algebra**: the absence of atoms, trace, and pure states in type III₁ is a fact about the local observables of a quantum field on a background, not about reality's unity; and the fact changes when the clock is included (§11).
7. Mechanism of the *rūḥ*.
8. Quantum-mind shortcuts as premises.
9. Borrowed vocabulary — "self-existence," "completeness," "necessity" are not predicates of operators.
10. Compatibility as evidence — including "the metaphysics selects exactly the mathematics physics had already chosen" counted as a resonance in favour of the metaphysics.
11. Interpretation as datum.
12. Claiming the one true harmonization.
13. Plenitude as a physics premise.
14. Turning the unseen into physics.
15. Counting null results as confirmation.
16. **Dissolution as result.** Under a real distinction there is no "dissolution track." A framing that makes the demand "derive time" ill-posed is not available; the demand is well-posed as emergence (rule 7) and is the plan's business.
17. **The stationary whole as the ground.** The Wheeler–DeWitt state, a cyclic separating vector, the standard form of an algebra — each is a description of the created order at some level. None is timeless in the sense in which the ground is prior to time; each is stationary in the sense that a constraint equation is satisfied. The two senses are not the same, and only the second is in this document's jurisdiction.
18. **Structural circularity as a requirement.** Fixed-point equations (X = F(X), ΔΩ = Ω) are ordinary mathematics. The claim that any account of the world *must* be circular because ground and appearance are not two things is withdrawn with the premise that produced it.
19. **A privileged clock assumed.** The real distinction *permits* a preferred state or foliation (§8); it does not supply one. Any privileged clock in this plan must be derived (D-14) or it is a brute fact (D-8).
