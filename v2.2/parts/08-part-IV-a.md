**What this framework does not predict** *(v1.0 §10, restored verbatim in substance — §23.2, item 57).* No new particle, no new force, no new number, no new effect. No specific cosmological signature; no quantum-gravity signal. On the origin of time's arrow it predicts the *form* of the answer to (a) and nothing about (b) (§17.1). It predicts the *shape* of future physics, not its content — which is why its predictions are of the kind "there should be a derivation" and "these should turn out to be one structure".

**Patterns to look for** *(v1.0 §10).* Wherever two complete descriptions of one system compete, look for the axis. Wherever a causation puzzle shows over-determination, look for the missing level. Wherever a bridge claim appears, ask for its independent warrant; the expectation is that most are unwarranted, and the discipline is to say so. Wherever a framework becomes compatible with everything, its discriminating core has been lost; find where it used to bleed. Wherever a dissolution appears, ask what it forbids; a dissolution that forbids nothing is a re-description (§4.2). Wherever a borrowed layer is doing explanatory work, separate assumption from evidence.

**Questions the map makes askable** *(v1.0 §10, with v2.1's one correction).* (1) What is the adjoint of each fundamental construction? (2) Which trade-off inequality or adjunction does each polarity carry? — v1.0 asked "which conserved quantity", and §4 retired that test: conservation follows from symmetry, not from polarity. (3) Where are the dissociation pairs in each claimed polarity (N-5 is the template)? (4) Which open question does a proposed finding fill — and does it meet the fill-conditions, or merely touch the word? (5) What would the absence of the predicted structure look like, stated in advance? (6) Which level is ours (P10), and does any answer explain, or merely absorb, the question? (7) Which dissolution could be turned into a forbiddance — a claim the world could fail (D-7 is the worked example)?
