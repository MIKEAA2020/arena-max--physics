## §0 How to read this

**Four parts.** Part I is the governing framework: what an acceptable programme may and may not say, with the repairs the audit forced. Part II states the problem of time as it stands and what the metaphysics is permitted to contribute to it. Part III is the plan: the relational core, the formal no-go layer (corrected), the modular programme (re-anchored, and brought up to date with the 2022–2025 crossed-product results), the measure-first layer (new), the landscape, the dependency architecture and the work packages. Part IV is the registers: predictions, open questions, gaps, dead ends, citations, and the consolidation record.

**Three registers for every load-bearing physical claim** (inherited from v2.0 and kept as a global convention):

- **Theorem** — established mathematics or an established physical result, with a citation.
- **Conjecture** — a precise statement whose proof or test is open.
- **Interpretation** — a reading that guides the search and constrains nothing.

The third is never presented as the first. Where a claim is physics but not mathematics (an experimental fact, a consensus), it is labelled **established**, **strong consensus**, or **partial**, and never promoted.

**Four grades for every statement of the framework** (inherited from the Lighthouse):

- **[C] Constraint** — must not be violated anywhere in a programme built on this map.
- **[H] Heuristic** — points the search; may be wrong; revised on evidence.
- **[P] Open question** — unknown; kept visible; may not be quietly filled.
- **[F] Forbidden** — do not enter without new evidence; the only reopening condition is stated.

**Stable identifiers.** Open questions **P1–P15**; predictions **D-1–D-14**; research questions **RQ-1–RQ-9** (physics) and **N-1–N-7** (neuroscience); gaps **G1–G20**; atoms **A1–A9**; glues **GL-1–GL-6** (renamed from v2.0's "G1–G5" to end the collision with the gap register); placeholders **PH-1–PH-3**; work packages **WP0–WP7**. **Lexical discipline [C].** Five terms in these documents carried more than one sense, and several errors in the sources were produced by sliding between senses. §5 fixes the senses and tags them. ## Summary in one page

**The problem.** Quantum theory writes its basic equation with an external, universal, classical time parameter. General relativity has no such parameter: coordinate time is a gauge label, causal order is invariant, and duration is a path-dependent functional read by a clock. Canonical quantization of a closed universe turns this into the Wheeler–DeWitt constraint ĤΨ = 0, in which no time appears, the gauge-invariant observables are constants, there is no agreed inner product, and a superposition of geometries is a superposition of causal structures with no shared "before" and "after." Nothing in this document solves this. The field's working answer — time is relational and emergent, recovered as correlations with physical clocks inside a stationary whole — is adopted as the preferred shape, with its open problems (which clock; inequivalent quantum theories for different clocks; no global clock; recovery of classical spacetime; ordering versus passage) kept visible.

**What the metaphysics contributes, and only this.** Constraints: no God-as-clock, no God-as-field, no gaps filled with the divine; time belongs to the created order and the ground is prior in being, not in time; the created order is closed *as physics* (no external physical clock) and dependent *metaphysically* (a grounding, not a clock). Permissions: the created state is contingent, so a selected state and a privileged clock are admissible — physics must earn them, not assume them. Prohibitions on itself: no dissolution of the problem by re-description; no verse yields a mechanism; no structure of physics is identified with the ground.

**What the plan does.** It keeps the relational core (Page–Wootters, the Trinity, quantum reference frames) and the modular programme (Tomita–Takesaki flow as candidate time; the Connes cocycle as the transformation between clocks), repairs three errors in v2.0's formal core, verifies the one result v2.0 had taken on report (the no-adjoint obstruction — now a theorem in finite dimensions, and, by its own author's account, a theorem about normalization rather than about time), and brings the modular programme up to date: since 2022, adjoining a clock to the type III₁ algebra of quantum field theory — the crossed product by the modular flow — yields type II algebras with a trace, an entropy, and (in de Sitter) a distinguished maximum-entropy state. That work is *the plan's own title carried out*, it bridges the plan's relational and modular layers, and it changes the honest status of the type question, the clock-ambiguity question, and the state-selection question.

**The one new structural claim.** *Measure precedes duration* — the Lighthouse's central heuristic — has three exact physical instances: a Lorentzian metric is causal order plus a volume measure (M3); a clock requires a scale, so a scale-free world has order but no duration (M3); and a state on a noncommutative algebra determines a canonical flow, so a measure (M2) determines a time — which is the thermal time hypothesis, exactly. In the third form the clock ambiguity *is* the measure ambiguity, and the Lighthouse's two open questions "time's emergence" (P2) and "which measure" (P3) are one question. This is a conjecture about structure, not a result, and it is the plan's organizing bet.

**What could fail.** Fourteen predictions with falsifiers (§18); eight work packages (WP0–WP7) with go/no-go criteria (§15); twenty registered gaps (§20). The plan predicts no new particle, no new number, and no quantum-gravity signal. It predicts the shape of the final theory, and names the result that would show the shape wrong.
