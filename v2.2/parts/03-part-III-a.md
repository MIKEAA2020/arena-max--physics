# Part III — The plan

## §9 The relational core

**9.1 The mechanism** (Page–Wootters 1983). A universe satisfying a constraint of the form (7-2) is partitioned into a clock C and a system S with non-interacting total Hamiltonian Ĥ = Ĥ_C ⊗ 1 + 1 ⊗ Ĥ_S. The clock carries a time observable T̂_C conjugate to Ĥ_C (ideally [T̂_C, Ĥ_C] = iħ; realistically a covariant POVM), with clock states |t⟩. Expanding the global state in the clock basis, |Ψ⟩⟩ = ∫dt |t⟩ ⊗ |ψ(t)⟩, the constraint yields the conditional Schrödinger equation iħ ∂ₜ|ψ(t)⟩ = Ĥ_S|ψ(t)⟩. "At time t" means "conditioned on the clock reading t." The correlations are entanglement in a stationary global state; an internal observer sees ordinary evolution; the global state does not evolve. Realized with an entangled photon pair, one photon serving as the clock for the other (Moreva et al. 2014).

**9.2 Critique and repair.** Kuchař's objection — that conditioning applies a projection that does not commute with the constraint, and so yields wrong propagators for relativistic systems — is answered, within its scope, by the *Trinity* (Höhn–Smith–Lock 2021), which establishes by explicit reduction maps the equivalence of three pictures: relational Dirac observables in a clock-neutral formulation; Page–Wootters conditional states; and a relational Heisenberg picture obtained by symmetry reduction. Relational observables are quantized with covariant POVMs encompassing non-ideal clocks, and conditional probabilities are computed in the physical Hilbert space from the start. Giovannetti–Lloyd–Maccone (2015) had already repaired the propagator issue by conditioning on clock POVMs. Castro-Ruiz, Giacomini, Belenchia and Brukner (2020) extend the picture to clocks that gravitate and to the temporal localizability of events among them.

**Scope [C].** The constructions are theorem-grade in finite-dimensional and idealizable settings with constant constraint algebras. Gravity has a constraint algebra with structure functions; the observable technology is not directly available there (G6). The first work package is confined to constant algebras.

**9.3 What remains open, and where each item goes.**

1. **The clock ambiguity (multiple-choice problem).** Different clock choices produce inequivalent conditional dynamics; nothing in the state ranks them. In v2.0 this was "the central quantitative object." In v2.1 it is sharpened twice: algebraically, clock choice is state choice and the transformation between clocks is the Connes cocycle (§11.3); and in the gravitational algebras of §11.4 the choice is *not* pure gauge — different clocks give different algebras and different entropies (De Vuyst–Eccles–Höhn–Kirklin 2024–25). A 2026 preprint sharpens it a third time, in the plan's direction: under a purely relational stance the ambiguity extends from histories to the *evolution laws* themselves (an ideal clock's spectrum uniformizes the world's evolution operators, leaving only Hilbert-space dimension invariant), the non-interaction condition does not remove it, and what removes it is the physical meaning of the operators — something the bare Page–Wootters structure does not contain (Stoica 2026, arXiv:2604.21805; preprint, §22.10). A second preprint by the same author makes the point from the state side: an entangled clock–world vector has infinitely many product decompositions, each a valid time operator of the *same* clock, each giving a different history, so the reading on which the world is passively conditioned on a clock reading cannot hold — fixing "the" time operator imports the temporal meaning Page–Wootters was to explain (Stoica 2026b, arXiv:2608.16732; preprint, §22.13). The open question is P15.
2. **The tensor-product structure.** Page–Wootters presupposes a split into clock and rest. The Wheeler–DeWitt state does not come with one. Any principle that selects the split from the Hamiltonian's spectrum alone (the "quantum mereology" programme of Carroll–Singh 2021 is one attempt) is a principle that derives a clock from a measure in sense **M2** — and is, for that reason, exactly what the Lighthouse's heuristic asks for. Registered as G17. (Stoica 2026 adds a constraint on the candidates: fixing the split by fiat removes the ambiguity but "blocks spacetime symmetries", and treating all splits as equally valid perspectives makes records uncorrelated with what they record; the selecting principle must do neither.) Theorem 10.2 adds a constraint on any answer: once a clock system is fixed, "the rest given the clock" is not a system of the normalized theory, so the split cannot be undone inside the theory by currying; conditional dynamics is a higher-order object, which is the type the Trinity's relational observables already have. *Added 15 October 2026 (tenth amendment; §22.13; §23.2 item 99).* The split now has a non-perturbative cousin. Gravitational-path-integral and quantum-extremal-surface arguments give a **one-dimensional, real Hilbert space for a closed universe without an observer**; including an observer *Ob* — a subsystem with many degrees of freedom in a pointer basis, nothing more — yields a quantum mechanics on a Hilbert space of dimension ≈ e^{S_Ob}, with errors exponentially small in S_Ob (Harlow–Usatyuk–Zhao, JHEP 02 (2026) 108), or of dimension scaling as e^{1/G_N} under a different rule for keeping the observer in its universe (Abdalla–Antonini–Iliesiu–Levine, JHEP 05 (2025) 059); the two prescriptions differ and give different dimensions (Akers et al., JHEP 05 (2025) 201). For this plan the result is the strongest available form of the thesis that time is the output of a split: with no split there is not merely no evolution — there is no state space in which anything could happen; and the dependence of the *dimension* on how the observer is included is dead end 8 ("the clock ambiguity as pure gauge") refuted at a deeper level than DEHK's. Graded **[H]** (path-integral evidence; prescriptions not yet unique). Nothing in it is about minds (§6, item 8), and nothing in it supplies the selecting principle G17 asks for.
3. **Ideal clocks.** Finite precision, back-reaction, decoherence; corrections only partially understood (G2; WP1). *Sharpened (tenth amendment):* the idealization fails first in the **measurement model** — the twirled-observable and purified-measurement implementations of a Page–Wootters measurement agree for ideal clocks and describe operationally distinct situations for finite-resource clocks (Hausmann–Schmidhuber–Castro-Ruiz, Quantum 9 (2025) 1616); and a single composite system serving as both rod and clock obeys a Heisenberg-like trade-off between spatial and temporal resolution, from quantum speed limits plus mass–energy equivalence (Mattei–Castro-Ruiz 2025, preprint) — a conjugate pair in §4's sense, and a resource bound for WP1 and WP6.
4. **Dynamical geometry.** The mechanism is demonstrated on fixed backgrounds (G6; the crossed-product constructions of §11.4 are the first setting in which the clock is inserted *with* the gravitational constraint).
5. **Ordering versus passage.** Conditional correlations give a B-series (an ordering), not an A-series (a flow). "Becoming" is a posit the formalism does not supply. The one programme that supplies it — causal-set sequential growth — is admitted to the landscape at §13. The question is excluded from the falsifiable core (v2.0 §12.3 retained).

**Statement.** An account of time as output with *no* meta-time is exactly the modular programme of §11, and the ledger records what it yields: flow, yes — canonically up to inner automorphisms; order, yes given a split; duration, given a state; arrow, never. This is the plan's position restated from the side of the question, and it is why Rule 7 reads as it does: a one-way derivation of some components of time from a stationary structure is ordinary emergence and physics' business; the dependence of the created order, time included, on its ground is not a derivation and is never in physics' hands.

*A lemma from the "clock theory" (verified).* In the deterministic (commitment) regime the L-tick clock (1 0^{L−1})^∞ has state complexity exactly L, and its commitment gap is Com(M) = 1/L for every M < L and 0 at M = L: below the threshold no machine does better than no clock at all (proof by the Chinese remainder theorem on the common period; exhaustive check for L = 3…6). The deterministic clock is all-or-nothing; only a *stochastic* clock can be partially right (the graded gap curve of §10.6 addendum, item 74).

**In plain terms.** Split the universe into a clock and everything else, and ask what everything else is doing given the clock's reading: the answer obeys ordinary quantum mechanics, and a photon experiment has shown it. The old objection has a careful repair, in small systems. The residue is the choice itself — which part is the clock, and which split of the whole into parts — and this plan treats that residue not as a nuisance but as the thing to be derived.

## §10 The formal no-go layer, corrected

v2.0 unified its two analogies — a chess game read only through its notation, and a sphere whose inhabitants cannot reach the ambient room — into a "temporal confinement triple" and a conjecture (C-NOADJ) that it called the formal core's load-bearing wall. The analogies are kept in formalized form. The conjecture, as stated, is false, and the correction changes what the wall is.

**10.1 The analogies, formalized** (kept from v2.0 §4). *Chess:* E a finite set of half-moves totally ordered by ⪯; the move number c₀(eᵢ) = i; the players' time τ: E → ℝ strictly increasing with increments Δᵢ (thinking times). The intrinsic description is (E, ⪯); the external description is (E, ⪯, τ); the two clocks are related by a monotone, generally nonlinear change of variable, and an observer with access only to c₀ cannot reconstruct τ because the lost information is exactly the increment vector. Two corrections stand: the chess order is total and general relativity's is partial (Layer 1 uses causal sets); and chess has a preferred clock by rule, general relativity does not. The "twin paradox" reading of two chess clocks was refuted in v2.0 and stays refuted. *Sphere:* (M, g) isometrically embedded by i: M ↪ (E, h); intrinsic distance (arc) versus extrinsic distance (chord); the chord is not a path in the inhabitants' world. The mandatory repair (G3) stands: general relativity has no ambient room, so i is reinterpreted as the inclusion of the physical (constraint-satisfying) sector into the kinematical arena — the two-tier structure of Dirac quantization — and τ lives on the auxiliary space and is never an observable.

**10.2 The triple and the functor** (v2.0 §5.1, restated). Σ a causal poset or Lorentzian manifold with its intrinsic order; i: Σ → M the inclusion into the auxiliary kinematical space; τ: M → ℝ a global parameter on the auxiliary space. The category **Int** has ordered structures (X, ⪯) and order-preserving maps; **Ext** has parameter-equipped ordered structures (X, ⪯, τ) with τ monotone, and order- and parameter-preserving maps (f with τ′∘f = τ). The forgetful functor U: **Ext** → **Int** discards τ. A *clock* is a section of U. v2.0 conjectured that U has no natural adjoint, and read that absence as "no universal clock."

**10.3 The correction (theorem).** **Ext** is the slice category **Int**/(ℝ, ≤): ordered structures over the real line. For any category with binary products, the forgetful functor from a slice C/B to C has a right adjoint, X ↦ (X × B → B). Posets have products (the product order). Hence:

> **Proposition 10.1.** U: **Int**/ℝ → **Int** has a right adjoint R: X ↦ (X × ℝ, product order, τ = projection to ℝ). The unit sends (Y, σ) to y ↦ (y, σ(y)); the bijection **Ext**((Y, σ), R X) ≅ **Int**(Y, X) is f ↦ π₁∘f. U has no left adjoint: a left adjoint L would make **Ext**(L X, (ℝ, id)) — a one-element set — biject with **Int**(X, ℝ), which has more than one element whenever X is non-empty.

So an adjoint exists, canonically, and it is worth saying what it is: R X is X with an independent real line adjoined at every event — **Newtonian absolute time**, the cofree clock. What is genuinely absent is a natural *section* that selects one clock correlated with the order. Even that fails for the wrong reason: with τ merely monotone, the constant clock τ ≡ 0 is a natural section; with τ required strictly monotone, no section exists at all because some posets (ω₁, for instance) admit no strictly monotone real-valued map — a cardinality fact, not a physical one. The poset formalization therefore carries none of the intended content. It is retained only as a definition of vocabulary (dead end 15).

**10.4 Where the content actually lives — now verified.** v2.0's evidence for "no universal clock" came from an unrefereed manuscript on quantum combs (v2.0 ref. [10]), described second-hand. The manuscript itself, in its eighth revision (Abaee, *The Opfibration Ontology: Quantum Instruments, Irreversibility, and the Epistemic Asymptote*, Rev. 2.8, October 2026), was supplied after v2.1 was issued. Its main-text theorems were re-derived independently for this document — twenty symbolic and numerical checks, all passing (`v2.2/verify/opfibration_checks.py`, log alongside). What it proves, in its own registers:

> **Theorem 10.2 (no adjoint to environment decoration in normalized process categories; Rev. 2.8 Thms 4.3, 4.5, 4.8, 4.12, 5.2, Prop. 5.1, Thm C.2).** Let E be a finite-dimensional quantum system with d_E ≥ 2. (i) In **Chan** (completely positive trace-preserving maps between finite-dimensional systems) the functor −⊗E has no right adjoint: a right adjoint would give an affine bijection Chan(E,E) ≅ States(R(E)), forcing d_R² = d_E⁴ − d_E² + 1, which lies strictly between the consecutive squares (d_E²−1)² and d_E⁴. (ii) More strongly, for d_E, d_B ≥ 2 the convex body Chan(E,B) is not affinely isomorphic to the normal state space of any Hilbert space, finite or infinite: its extreme boundary has real dimension 2d_E²(d_B−1), the pure states of a d_R-dimensional system form a manifold of dimension 2(d_R−1), and the two dimension counts cannot be matched simultaneously. (iii) In the category of finite-outcome instruments (ordered tuples, or their outcome-permutation quotient) the presheaf A ↦ Instr(A⊗E, B) is not representable for *any* target B: comparing the one- and two-outcome slices at A = ℂ, the representing side gives q(2) − 2q(1) = 1 and the process side gives d_E². (iv) The same two-slice mismatch holds for classical finite-outcome stochastic instruments: the obstruction is normalization, not quantumness. (v) −⊗E has no left adjoint in Chan or in Instr; Instr has neither terminal nor initial object. (vi) In unnormalized **CPM** the right adjoint exists and is E*⊗−.

*Register:* theorem — elementary, and independently re-derived here for (i)–(vi) in their main-text forms. The appendix's extensions (coarse-grained, zero-dropping, dyadic and measurable-outcome quotients; infinite-dimensional E with a separable program register, C.5–C.28; the programming benchmarks C.29–C.35) were not re-derived and are carried as the author's, unrefereed. The headline of (i) agrees with folklore in categorical quantum mechanics — CPTP is semicartesian (discarding is unique), not closed, which is why Kissinger and Uijlen built **Caus** — and the manuscript makes no priority claim. The manuscript's own value is the quantified, pointwise and quotient-by-quotient treatment, and something rarer: the discipline of its retractions.

So v2.0's "intercept principle" as a wall against intrinsic observers was never the manuscript's claim in its mature form. **Theorem C-NOADJ (v2.2; replaces Conjecture C-NOADJ).** *In the finite-dimensional normalized process categories — Chan, finite-outcome instruments and their permutation quotient, and classical finite-outcome instruments — decoration −⊗E by a nontrivial system E has no adjoint on either side; the would-be internal hom [E, B] is not a system of the theory but a higher-order (comb-type) convex object.* Register: theorem (Theorem 10.2). **Conjecture C-NOADJ′ (what remains open).** *The same holds for normal channels between von Neumann algebras of types II and III, and for the clock-dressed gravitational algebras of §11.4.* Register: conjecture. The dimension counts do not transfer (the hom-sets are infinite-dimensional); the manuscript's C.5 — a continuum of extreme channels whose exact programs must be mutually orthogonal — is the method that might, and it currently needs a separable register. v2.0's "five-fold equivalence" and its clause "no universal finite-memory evaluator recovers a clock-free description" are *not* restored: the manuscript proves non-representability, not an epistemic limit; its no-programming theorem (C.5) is the Nielsen–Chuang obstruction in a new form and says nothing about observers.

**The reading for the time problem (interpretation, kept small).** Let the clock be a system E of a normalized process theory. Theorem 10.2 says that "the rest, conditioned on the clock" is not a system of that theory: conditional dynamics is a higher-order object — an element of the internal hom that exists only in a completion. That is a categorical reason for a choice the Trinity already made: relational content is carried by relational *observables* (maps) and frame changes (supermaps), not by a conditional *system*. Nothing about the direction, rate or selection of time follows, and the theorem holds for classical, quantum, reversible and irreversible dynamics alike.

**10.5 The layer distinction** (v2.0 Table 5-1, kept; statuses updated). First-order normalized categories (**Chan**, **Instr**, **FinStoch**): no internal hom for −⊗E (Theorem 10.2). Higher-order compact-closed (**Caus[CPM]**): internal homs exist; price: higher-order types, trace preservation lost at the base. Unnormalized (**CPM**): internal homs exist; price: probability conservation lost. The reformulation that follows is one of v2.0's cleanest surviving statements and is kept: *the external time of quantum mechanics is a higher-order causal structure — not representable in the first-order normalized process theory, emerging as an internal hom at the price of a higher-order type system.* In the analogy, the first-order category is the sphere's surface and **Caus[CPM]** is the room. In the Lighthouse's grammar (§4) this is an adjunction-type axis, and Proposition 10.1 adds a second, humbler one: the slice adjunction whose right adjoint is Newtonian time. Of the three questions v2.1 sent to WP3: the first — whether the first-order category sits inside a higher-order completion under a physically motivated condition — is now partly answered by the manuscript itself, which constructs a typed affine-slice completion 𝒯 in which −⊗𝔈 ⊣ [𝔈, −] for every finite-dimensional typed input, whose first-order subcategory is Chan and whose Bell counit is trace-preserving only on the admissible slice (C.15–C.19), and proves closedness for the balanced subcategory of **Caus[CPM(FHilb)]** (C.24), with maximality and equivalence beyond it open (R5). The second — whether the intercept is a cohomological obstruction — is dropped: the intercept is an affine codimension (C.17), and no class has been exhibited. The third — whether the Connes cocycle of §11.3 supplies descent data between the layers — is the one genuinely open question and moves to WP5.

With T_ext the auxiliary parameter and C a clock reading, R(D) = min I(T_ext; C) subject to mean-squared estimation error ≤ D prices each clock. This is Shannon theory and stands on its own; it neither supports nor is supported by C-NOADJ — Rev. 2.8 §5.4 and R8 state that δ_n = d_E²−1 is not a cost or distortion measure, and v2.0's phrase "supports C-NOADJ" is withdrawn. *Addendum (fifth amendment, 15 October 2026 — §23.2, items 73–74).* The shell's intuition now has a worked-out general theory by the same author as Rev. 2.8: *The Rate–Distortion Theory of Bounded Sequential Transduction* (Zenodo, 10.5281/zenodo.22260638, 2 September 2026; audited in `v2.2/assessments/bst-rate-distortion-review.md`, independent checks `v2.2/verify/bst_checks.py` 17/17, the author's two programs re-run with identical output). Three things follow for this section, none of them a change of status. **(i) Different rate.** There the rate is a *state budget* M (the index of a right congruence on histories, or an operator rank), not an information rate in bits; there is no coding theorem; the relation to the R(D) above is analogy. **(ii) Time is presupposed.** Every object is defined over prefix-ordered histories and causal functions I^ℕ → O^ℕ; the "temporal axis" is the round index of an online protocol; the retention regime is the causal-state construction of computational mechanics, oriented past → future by convention. The theory is a theory of bounded modelling *inside* a given order — a fourth independent instance of the pattern behind Prop. 11.1: where a formalism contains a direction, the direction entered as a hypothesis. The paper claims nothing else (a scan finds no physics claim of any kind). **(iii) The asymptote, corrected from the right side.** What v2.0 called an "epistemic asymptote" and Rev. 2.8 withdrew (item 17) is, done properly, a *gap curve* Δ(M) with **zero thresholds at finite budget** — κ_det(F) for exact commitment, the refinement index N* for retention (which exceeds the number of distinct predictive kernels when lumpability binds), rank H_ν for linear grounding — and a true asymptote only for infinite-rank targets, where the unrestricted shortfall is exactly σ_{M+1}(H_ν) (Eckart–Young–Mirsky) and the Hankel-structured shortfall is ≥ σ_{M+1}, with equality in the one-letter Adamjan–Arov–Krein case and open in the multiletter case. A bounded observer of a finite-state world is exact above a finite threshold; of an infinite-rank world, short by a computable number. Neither is a wall against intrinsic observers. Item 17 stays withdrawn; the shell keeps its status (decoupled; G4; WP2) and gains the correct formal object to point at. The one place where the apparatus touches the arrow — direction-dependent retention budgets, via causal irreversibility C_μ⁺ ≠ C_μ⁻ (Crutchfield–Ellison–Mahoney 2009) — is stated as an open problem in the review (§4(d)) and logged as item 76: a *diagnostic* of time-asymmetry in a law, never a source of it.

**10.7 Irreversibility and reversal, as the manuscript leaves them** (new). Two results of Rev. 2.8 §6 are relevant beyond the no-go layer and both were checked numerically. *Exact recovery is a property of the channel* (Prop. 6.1): a channel has a CPTP left inverse iff its Kraus operators satisfy the full-space Knill–Laflamme condition K_i†K_j = c_ij I, i.e. iff it is a flagged mixture of isometries with orthogonal ranges — more than a single isometry, which the earlier draft had claimed. *Reversal is prior-indexed* (Prop. 6.2): for a full-rank prior σ and τ = N(σ) > 0, the Petz transpose N^♯_σ(X) = σ^{1/2} N*(τ^{−1/2} X τ^{−1/2}) σ^{1/2} is CPTP, recovers σ from τ, and composes contravariantly, (M∘N)^♯_σ = N^♯_σ ∘ M^♯_τ — a functor on *pointed* channels, not on channels. In the language of §11 this is the same lesson from the other side: the Petz map for faithful normal states on a von Neumann algebra is built from relative modular operators (Petz 1988), so **the state that fixes the flow is the state that fixes the reversal, and the category fixes neither** (dead end 5). Register: theorem for each half; the juxtaposition is an interpretation that §17 uses. Finally, the one categorical asymmetry that survives — ℂ is terminal but not initial in Chan; "discarding is unique, preparation is not" (Prop. 5.5; the Coecke–Lal causality axiom) — is *order* in the lexicon of §5: presupposed by a process theory, not derived by it, and not an arrow.

**In plain terms.** Forget everything about a world except which events come before which; a clock is any principled way of putting a parameter back. v2.0 said there is no canonical way to do this. There is: adjoin a free real line to every event — Newton's absolute time, exactly what relativity denies. The real obstruction is elsewhere, and it is now a theorem rather than a report: in any theory that must conserve probability, you cannot fold a nontrivial clock into the system it reads and get back a system of the same theory — "the rest, given the clock" is a different kind of object, one order up. The author of the manuscript that first proposed this has since removed every claim that it says anything about the direction of time, the irreversibility of processes, or the limits of observers; the mathematics was checked here and holds. There is also a door: in a richer, higher-order theory of processes the missing construction exists, and the manuscript now builds one.
